*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic's Amodei calls for "pacing the frontier" of AI development
In a 3,800-word essay posted Saturday, CEO Dario Amodei said the industry "must slow the pace at which we improve the capabilities of AI models," warning that a swarm of AI agents could be capable of taking over the internet with a persistent botnet within 6-12 months; Anthropic is committing to an embedded team of third-party evaluators, and OpenAI's Sam Altman backed the call with plans for independent system audits. — [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/12/tech/anthropic-ceo-essay-ai)

### Second Anthropic safety researcher quits, warns of "extinction-level risks"
Joe Benton, who led Anthropic's Scalable Oversight team, resigned to join AI-safety nonprofit METR, calling for mandatory incident reporting and independent evaluations of frontier labs — the second Anthropic safety departure in two days after a colleague warned AI "could kill us all in the next decade." — [Free Press Journal](https://www.freepressjournal.in/world/joe-benton-quits-anthropic-safety-team-warns-ai-race-could-pose-extinction-level-risks)

## 🛠 Development

### Cognition ships SWE-2, closing in on frontier coding performance at a fraction of the cost
Post-trained from Moonshot AI's 2.8-trillion-parameter Kimi K3, SWE-2 scores within one point of Claude Fable 5.1 on FrontierCode 1.1 while costing 64-70% less, thanks to a "Pareto-informed" RL recipe that penalizes wasted tokens at every reasoning-effort level; it's live now in Devin Desktop and Devin CLI. — [Cognition](https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2)

### GitHub Copilot CLI adds session/memory import and tightens sandbox behavior
The update brings commands to import sessions and memory via a semantic JSONL interchange format, more reliable sandbox path handling and error messages, and subagent launches that now respect model and reasoning-effort preferences from custom instructions. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Sam Altman rules out an OpenAI IPO in 2026, citing AI safety risk
In a Fortune interview published Saturday, the OpenAI CEO said going public now would be an "ill-advised moment" given safety challenges, adding that even a small chance of AI causing human extinction would be "unacceptable" — the remarks land the same weekend Anthropic's CEO published his own call to slow AI development. — [The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/future/technology/2026/09/13/openai-rules-out-2026-ipo-as-sam-altman-flags-ai-safety-risks/)

### Microsoft targets tripling data-center capacity to 38 gigawatts by 2032
The buildout, up from roughly 12 gigawatts today, follows a capacity crunch that reportedly pushed Temu's cloud business to Oracle and contributed to an eight-hour GitHub outage in August; about a third of the new capacity is earmarked for AI workloads. — [Technology.org](https://www.technology.org/2026/09/11/microsoft-38-gigawatts-data-center-capacity-2032/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### CVSS-10 GitLab path traversal flaw already under active exploitation
CVE-2026-85706 lets an unauthenticated attacker read arbitrary files — including credentials and configuration data — from GitLab CE/EE servers via the repository commits API; GitLab patched it September 10 in versions 19.3.2, 19.2.6 and 19.1.8, but in-the-wild scanning began within 24 hours, and CISA has set a September 14 remediation deadline for federal agencies. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/gitlab-cvss-10-file-read-flaw-draws-in.html)

### Extortion group FulcrumSec breached Novo Nordisk for over two months via hardcoded tokens
The "Hardcoded Horrorshow" campaign started with a personal access token exposed in a public JavaScript bundle, then used a leaked GitHub token to move laterally across GitHub, Azure DevOps, AWS, Hugging Face and Okta, ultimately stealing 4,748 source-code repositories and data on more than 163,000 employees. — [Cybersecurity Insiders](https://www.cybersecurity-insiders.com/pharmaceutical-data-breach-novo-nordisk-fulcrumsec/)
