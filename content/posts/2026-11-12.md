*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google launches Tabletop, an experimental AI planning hub
The waitlist-gated beta turns Gemini into a customizable personal "planning hub" for things like trips and projects, Google's latest bet on AI-native productivity surfaces beyond the chatbot box. — [Source](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1031526/google-tabletop-ai-experiment-waitlist-availability)

### Anthropic unveils office tools ahead of its IPO
Anthropic rolled out a Claude-powered video-call assistant and a spreadsheet tool, pushing directly into productivity-suite territory long owned by Microsoft and Google as it prepares to go public. — [Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/anthropic-unveils-office-tools-ahead-of-ipo-taking-on-microsoft)

### Chinese AI firms rush to rent Nvidia compute overseas
Fallout from the Jensen Huang espionage scandal is stoking fears in Beijing that Washington will move to close the loophole letting Chinese firms access Nvidia chips through offshore data centers, driving a scramble to lock in capacity now. — [Source](https://www.reuters.com/world/china/chinese-ai-firms-rush-secure-nvidia-chip-capacity-spy-scandal-stirs-fear-us-2026-11-12/)

## 🛠 Development

### AWS ships a free local emulator for 200+ cloud services
AWS Emulator lets developers run stand-ins for most of AWS locally without an account or cloud costs, and the company says the tool was built largely by AI coding agents — a notable proof point for agentic software development at scale. — [Source](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/aws-gives-developers-a-free-local-stand-in-for-its-cloud-built-from-scratch-by-ai-agents/)

### Google launches the Gemini Decisions API
The new API returns predefined answers with confidence scores rather than freeform generations, aimed at developers who need reliable, constrained AI outputs for business logic instead of open-ended chat. — [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/technology/developers-tools/gemini-decisions-api/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Nvidia's board investigates Jensen Huang over alleged Chinese-intelligence ties
The board launched an independent probe, run by outside directors and counsel, after reports that Huang had a relationship with an executive identified by US authorities as a Chinese intelligence asset; the board says it has no evidence national security or trade secrets were compromised and that Huang retains its confidence as CEO, while three House committee chairs have requested a closed-door briefing. — [Source](https://techrepublic.com/article/news-nvidia-jensen-huang-espionage-inquiry-apac-china/)

### Radical Numerics unveils Omnii Defense, an offline AI biodefense tool
The edge AI model is designed to identify pathogens without an internet connection, a bid to harden biosecurity screening against the same AI-driven bioweapon-design risks it's meant to guard against. — [Source](https://www.wired.com/story/startup-fights-ai-bioweapons-with-ai/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Railway details a 26-hour breach that led to $740K in fraudulent AI charges
Attackers exploited an unknown flaw in Railway's build infrastructure starting October 20 to escape filesystem isolation, steal internal credentials, and ultimately decrypt customer environment variables; up to 100,000 workspaces may have been exposed, and stolen AI-provider keys were used to rack up roughly $740,000 in unauthorized usage before Railway said it closed the holes. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/apple/railway-breach-leads-to-740-000-in-fraudulent-ai-charges/)

### Investigation ties Qilin, now the most active ransomware gang, to a single operator
Qilin has claimed at least 365 victims in three months by recruiting English-speaking affiliates and paying them 80% of ransoms; researchers at NCC Group and independent analysts say forum records point to a St. Petersburg-based administrator behind the "Haise" handle. — [Source](https://krebsonsecurity.com/2026/11/who-runs-the-ransomware-group-qilin/)
