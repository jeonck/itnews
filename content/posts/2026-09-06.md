*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### xAI opens Grok Bot to enterprises with governance controls
The autonomous AI-worker product — each Bot runs in its own cloud microVM and can use apps and websites like a human employee — now ships Network Controls, audit logs, and organization-wide rollout for Enterprise customers, with Grok and Cursor Enterprise users getting free access for two weeks. — [xAI](https://x.ai/news/grok-bot-for-enterprise)

### LG unveils ThinQ Claw, an OpenClaw-based AI home agent, at IFA 2026
Built on the OpenClaw concept, the text-chat agent interprets what users share about their plans or routines and coordinates appliances and services accordingly — managing cooking devices ahead of guests or adjusting climate before someone gets home — instead of relying on manually configured automation rules. — [LG Newsroom](https://www.lg.com/global/newsroom/news/home-appliance-solution/lg-electronics-unveils-the-next-evolution-of-ai-home-at-ifa-2026/)

## 🛠 Development

### Codex 0.153.4 makes GPT-6 Astra the bundled default model
After the model was hidden from the interactive picker in the 0.153 release, OpenAI's fix restores its visibility and sets Astra as Codex CLI's default when no model is explicitly configured, though access still rolls out via Astra's staged Trusted Access Programme. — [openai/codex on GitHub](https://github.com/openai/codex/pull/42874)

### GitHub Copilot and VS Code 1.136 ship agent-focused updates
Copilot now offers Claude Fable 5.1 and Gemini 3.8 Flash to Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise users, while VS Code 1.136 adds tools for finishing pull requests, managing multi-root workspace agent sessions, and organizing related chats. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-04-github-copilot-weekly-releases-august-31/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Nscale seeks $3.5 billion in pre-IPO financing ahead of a US listing
The two-year-old British AI-infrastructure company — which last raised at a $14.6 billion valuation in March and holds a six-year, $45 billion compute deal with Anthropic — is offering convertible notes to investors while separately seeking roughly $2 billion more from Nvidia, ahead of a possible IPO as soon as this month. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/04/ai-compute-provider-nscale-is-looking-for-3-5b-in-pre-ipo-financing/)

### Samsung showcases a 1,100Hz monitor and expanded AI appliances at IFA 2026
Running its Humboldt Carré showcase alongside the main IFA floor, Samsung demonstrated the Odyssey G6 gaming monitor — the first to hit 1,100Hz, via AI frame generation at reduced resolution — alongside new energy-efficient Bespoke AI washers with deeper generative features. — [Samsung Newsroom](https://news.samsung.com/uk/samsung-connect-opens-at-ifa-2026-bringing-the-future-of-ai-powered-living-to-life)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Google patches actively exploited Chrome zero-day
CVE-2026-85046, a type-confusion bug in Chrome's V8 engine that allows sandboxed arbitrary code execution via a crafted web page, is the sixth Chrome zero-day patched in 2026; Google confirmed an exploit exists in the wild but has not disclosed attack details. Users should update to Chrome 152.0.7977.82 or later. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-releases-chrome-update-to-patch.html)

### CISA adds seven exploited flaws to its KEV catalog, including AI infrastructure for the first time
Three of the seven — hitting LiteLLM's AI gateway, workflow engine Kestra, and ASGI framework Starlette — mark the first KEV batch where AI-related components make up nearly half the additions, with attackers deploying reverse shells, forged admin tokens, and cryptominers; federal agencies face patch deadlines of September 5 and September 16. — [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/02/cisa-adds-seven-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)
