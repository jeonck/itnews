*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### IMF: AI has already cut hiring demand by nearly 10% in exposed jobs
A new IMF working paper covering 110 economies finds job postings in occupations highly exposed to AI have fallen almost 10%, versus about 4.7% where AI mainly augments workers rather than replacing tasks — while finding no significant change in advertised wages and no consistent evidence junior workers were hit harder than senior ones. — [Source](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/wp/issues/2026/07/10/aggregate-gains-from-ai-and-their-distribution-global-evidence-from-usage-data-577586)

### OpenAI rolls out PIN-locked chats in ChatGPT
Users on every tier, including Free, can now hide selected conversations behind a 4-digit PIN or device authentication; locked chats disappear from history, pinned chats, and search, and get their own separate memory space. It's a modest feature, but a sign OpenAI is treating ChatGPT increasingly like a repository of sensitive personal data. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/chatgpt-now-lets-you-lock-your-chats/)

## 🛠 Development

### Anthropic donates the Agent Skills standard to the Agentic AI Foundation
Anthropic is handing over the open standard for giving AI agents specialized workflows — along with its name, logo, GitHub org, and domain — to the Linux Foundation-backed AAIF it co-founded with Block and OpenAI. More than 70 platforms including ChatGPT, Cursor, GitHub Copilot, and VS Code have adopted it since its December launch, with roughly 1 million skills published and nearly 280 million installs. — [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/donating-agent-skills-to-the-agentic-ai-foundation)

### curl starts handling low-severity bug reports in public to survive an AI-driven flood
Starting today, curl will triage vulnerability reports it judges low or medium severity in public GitHub issues instead of privately through HackerOne, reserving private handling for high and critical flaws only. Maintainer Daniel Stenberg says a nonstop wave of reports — many AI-generated, and over 90% of all submissions turning out to be below high severity — has pushed the small maintainer team to its limit. — [Source](https://www.theregister.com/2026/10/26/curl_discloses_bugs_in_public/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### CoreWeave's founders have sold $3.3B in stock since its IPO as the stock slides
Bloomberg reporting shows CEO Michael Intrator and co-founders Brian Venturo and Brannin McBee have sold a combined $3.3 billion in shares since the March 2025 IPO, with $2.4 billion of that this year alone. The disclosures landed as CoreWeave shares fell toward 52-week lows and credit markets grew uneasy about debt-heavy AI infrastructure spending — the stock is down more than 60% from its 2025 peak. — [Source](https://247wallst.com/investing/2026/10/17/coreweaves-founders-have-sold-3-3-billion-of-stock-since-its-ipo/)

### AI assistant startup Town hits $1B valuation four months after launch
Town, which builds "Townies" — AI assistants that plug into a worker's email, calendar, and documents — raised a $108M Series B co-led by Forerunner Ventures and Menlo Ventures. The company says paying customers have grown fourfold and paying teams ninefold since July, as the new money goes toward letting individual Townies collaborate with each other across a team. — [Source](https://dealroom.co/news/163936-town-raises-108m-at-1b-valuation-to-make-ai-assistants-work-as-a-team/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Ransomware gangs keep breaking in through a five-month-old Palo Alto VPN flaw
ReliaQuest and Unit 42 say Qilin, Settra, and Inc ransomware operators are actively exploiting CVE-2026-0257, an authentication-bypass bug in PAN-OS GlobalProtect that Palo Alto patched back in May and that CISA added to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog days later. Attackers use it to open unauthorized VPN sessions, then move laterally with Cobalt Strike and BloodHound — and patching alone doesn't revoke access already established before the update. — [Source](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/?p=388600)
