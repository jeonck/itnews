*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Nvidia launches Minerva, an AIOps platform for AI factories
Minerva ingests telemetry from GPUs, networking gear, storage, and schedulers, then uses machine learning to build a knowledge graph of the whole stack so operators can trace a slowdown to its root cause instead of chasing a flood of disconnected alerts. It's part of Nvidia's push to sell the operations layer around its chips, not just the chips themselves — Bitdeer AI is already adopting it. — [Source](https://blogs.nvidia.com/blog/minerva-platform-infrastructure/)

### Accenture caps employee Claude spending at $29 a month
An internal memo reported by 404 Media shows Accenture centrally funding only $29 per employee per month for Claude usage starting October 1, after some engineers' Claude Code bills ran $500–$1,000 a month; anything above the cap now has to come out of a project or team budget. It's a concrete data point on how fast enterprise agentic-coding costs are climbing. — [Source](https://cybernews.com/ai-news/accenture-ai-spending-limits/)

## 🛠 Development

### Python 3.15 ships with an experimental JIT and lazy imports
The annual feature release adds lazy module imports that defer loading until first use, a new low-overhead sampling profiler, a built-in immutable `frozendict` type, UTF-8 as the default encoding, and an experimental JIT that Phoronix measured at roughly 7–8% faster than the standard interpreter on Linux x86-64. — [Source](https://phoronix.com/news/Python-3.15-Released)

### Node.js patches a critical Brotli decoder flaw alongside nine other CVEs
The October 21 security release for the 26.x, 24.x, and 22.x lines fixes an out-of-bounds write in Node's bundled Brotli decoder (CVE-2026-111497, rated critical by Google) that's reachable through `fetch()` or through Express 5/body-parser 2 decoding attacker-controlled compressed request bodies — a realistic path for remote code execution in ordinary web apps. — [Source](https://www.ox.security/blog/node-js-brotli-decoder-out-of-bounds-write-rce-cve-2026-111497/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Anthropic's IPO filing shows a $80.7 billion first-half net loss
The newly public S-1 reports $16.3 billion in first-half 2026 revenue, up from $1.2 billion a year earlier, against an $80.7 billion net loss — mostly a non-cash accounting charge from changes in the fair value of convertible notes and SAFEs. Anthropic is targeting a Nasdaq debut as early as November, in an offering reports suggest could exceed SpaceX's $86 billion IPO. — [Source](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/anthropic-ipo-plans-ai-safety/)

### Meta creates a new non-voting stock class to fund its AI buildout
Meta's board approved a new Class C non-voting share — one issued per existing Class A/B share, arriving January 2027 — that lets the company raise equity for AI infrastructure without diluting Mark Zuckerberg's roughly 60% voting control. It follows a Financial Times report that Meta is weighing a stock offering worth tens of billions of dollars, which Meta itself has called speculative. — [Source](https://decrypt.co/381640/meta-non-voting-stock-fund-ai-buildout-zuckerberg-control)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Microsoft ties a Chinese hacking group's AI-driven supply-chain attack to 2,250+ tenants
Microsoft attributes the ConnectWise breach disclosed October 16 to China-aligned Storm-0940, which used an open-source agentic pentesting framework called ARTEX to automate sign-in attempts against compromised ConnectWise application identities — peaking at 2,900 attempts per minute and reaching privileged access in under three hours in some cases. ConnectWise took its Cloud Backup service offline and is still restoring it in phases. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/china-linked-hackers-use-artex-ai.html)

### Japan's GMO Internet shuts down Onamae Desktop Cloud after mass server compromise
GMO confirmed malicious programs installed across up to 26,739 customer server environments and began progressively shutting the service down on October 21; it has since warned that attackers may have obtained and misused login credentials for forex trading accounts stored on affected servers. The service remains suspended with no restoration date given. — [Source](https://bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/japans-onamae-desktop-cloud-hacked-up-to-27-000-servers-affected)
