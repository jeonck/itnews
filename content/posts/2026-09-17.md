*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic merges Claude Cowork into chat, launches Claude Docs and Slides
Starting September 16, Claude drops from three modes to two — Chat and Code — as Anthropic folds its agentic Cowork product directly into the normal chat interface and adds new Docs and Slides tools, joining OpenAI in a broader industry push to build a single AI "superapp" for enterprise work. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/16/anthropic-merges-claude-chat-and-cowork-in-one-interface/)

### OpenAI starts testing Sponsored Agents inside ChatGPT ads
OpenAI is piloting business-sponsored AI agents that users can converse with after clicking a ChatGPT ad, alongside new AI creative tools for advertisers and integrations with HubSpot and Shopify — an early sign of how the company plans to monetize ChatGPT's user base beyond subscriptions. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/reimagining-advertising-with-ai/)

### Zuckerberg rejects calls for a coordinated AI industry slowdown
Responding to a pacing proposal backed by Anthropic's Dario Amodei and others, Meta's CEO argued that market and liability incentives already push labs toward safety and that Meta will keep shipping rather than wait for industry-wide coordination — a notable public split among AI leaders on safety governance. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/09/16/mark-zuckerberg-meta-ai-safety-jensen-huang-dario-amodei/)

## 🛠 Development

### WebMCP draft spec published, letting web pages expose tools to AI agents natively
The new browser API — backed by a W3C draft published September 15 and available for early preview in Chrome Canary — lets sites register structured, schema-driven actions that AI agents can call directly via `navigator.modelContext`, aiming to replace fragile screen-scraping with a standard agent-to-web interface. — [Chrome for Developers](https://developer.chrome.com/blog/webmcp-epp)

### Docker Desktop ships broad stability update across engine, Compose, and Kubernetes
The September 15 release fixes a cluster of long-standing pain points — PATH handling on macOS shells, a corrupt-network-database bug that could force a full factory reset, stale Docker Scout CLI hints, and several Windows installer and WSL issues. — [Docker Docs](https://docs.docker.com/desktop/release-notes/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### OpenAI weighs $1.5 trillion valuation, pushes IPO to 2027 over safety concerns
OpenAI is in talks for a new funding round that could value it as high as $1.5 trillion, citing more than $40 billion in annualized revenue driven by Codex and its GPT-6 Astra model, while Sam Altman said going public in 2026 would be "ill-advised" given rising AI safety concerns. — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/siladityaray/2026/09/16/openai-is-reportedly-weighing-new-funding-round-at-15-trillion-valuation/)

### Manufacturing-AI startup CADDi raises $114M at $1.2B valuation
The Tokyo-based startup, which helps manufacturers organize CAD files and production data with AI, more than doubled its valuation from last year with backing from Toyota's Woven Capital and Salesforce Ventures as it expands into North America. — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/16/caddi-raises-114m-at-1-2b-valuation-to-bring-manufacturing-ai-to-north-america/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Cisco patches maximum-severity ISE zero-day exploited to gain root
CVE-2026-76460 (CVSS 10.0) lets an unauthenticated attacker bypass Cisco Identity Services Engine's web management interface entirely and execute root-level commands; CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog the same day, giving federal agencies until September 19 to patch. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisco-warns-of-new-zero-day-ise-auth.html)

### WSO2 API Manager JWT bypass under active exploitation, enables full account takeover
CVE-2026-5430 (CVSS 9.8) lets attackers forge JWTs signed with an unsupported algorithm to mint admin-privileged tokens; watchTowr's honeypots captured forged tokens in the wild as early as September 13, with attackers able to reach every backend API and its stored credentials. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/active-exploitation-attempts-target.html)
