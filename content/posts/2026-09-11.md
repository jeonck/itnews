*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic publishes its most detailed threat-intelligence report yet
Covering misuse of Claude disrupted from December 2025 through August 2026, the report documents distillation attempts from seven China-based labs (including Alibaba, Moonshot, DeepSeek and Xiaomi), AI-orchestrated cyberattacks, a Yemen-based weapons cell, and scam operations; Anthropic says it disrupted every operation described. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/threat-intelligence-report-september-2026)

### OpenAI opens its Agents API to all developers in public beta
The API exposes the same harness and hosted-sandbox infrastructure that powers Codex — context compaction, tool search, subagents and crash recovery included — so developers can build long-running agents without writing their own orchestration layer; OpenAI cites early users seeing 4x lower latency and 86% fewer failed responses. — [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/introducing-the-agents-api/)

## 🛠 Development

### .NET 11 reaches Release Candidate 1 with a go-live license
C# 15 becomes the default language version with union types and closed class hierarchies finalized, and the release adds mobile app testing via `dotnet test`, reproducible container publishing, and Native AOT caching for single-file apps ahead of November's general availability. — [.NET Blog](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/dotnet/dotnet-11-rc-1/)

### GitHub previews a redesigned repository pull requests page
The refresh adds AND/OR search with nested filters, a collapsible filter sidebar, and a compact presentation mode, aimed at teams managing large volumes of open PRs. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-10-refreshed-repository-pull-requests-page-in-public-preview/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### DOJ investigates whether Nvidia structured its Groq deal to dodge antitrust review
Prosecutors are examining Nvidia's non-exclusive $20 billion licensing agreement with AI chip startup Groq, announced in December, questioning whether it was deliberately structured as a license rather than a merger to sidestep standard merger review; a formal information request has already gone out. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-10/doj-probes-nvidia-s-license-deal-with-groq-on-antitrust-concerns)

### Universal Music Group and ElevenLabs strike a multi-year AI music licensing deal
The agreement — ElevenLabs' first with a major label — launches a platform letting fans create authorized remixes, mashups and vocal reinterpretations of participating UMG artists' tracks, following a similar UMG-Spotify AI deal in May. — [Variety](https://variety.com/2026/music/news/umg-elevenlabs-ai-powered-music-platform-licensing-1236857240/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### ID-verification giant IDScan confirms breach of 150+ million driver's licenses
Hackers stole names, driver's license numbers and other government-ID data tied to IDScan's corporate customers across the US and Canada; the records surfaced for sale on a dark-web site before IDScan disclosed the incident, and the FBI is now investigating. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/10/id-verification-giant-idscan-confirms-data-breach-with-more-than-150-million-drivers-licenses-stolen/)

### Microsoft's September Patch Tuesday fixes two actively exploited zero-days
Among a record ~966 CVEs patched, CVE-2026-81963 (Windows Update Stack) and CVE-2026-85880 (Advanced Local Procedure Call) were both confirmed under active exploitation before release, alongside 105 critical flaws including Office RCEs triggerable just by previewing a file. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-september-2026-patch-tuesday-fixes-966-flaws-2-zero-days/)

### CISA's patch deadline hits today for a maximum-severity N-able N-central flaw
CVE-2026-86218 (CVSS 10.0), a pre-auth static code injection bug in the remote-monitoring platform used by MSPs, is being exploited in the wild and lets unauthenticated attackers run arbitrary code that can propagate across every connected managed system; federal agencies must patch by September 11. — [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/08/cisa-adds-four-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)
