*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic moves toward a public filing that could rival SpaceX's record IPO
Anthropic is preparing to publicly file IPO paperwork as soon as the end of August, targeting an October Nasdaq listing led by Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan, and Morgan Stanley that could raise more than $60B. Annualized revenue reportedly hit roughly $65B by the end of July as enterprise spending on Claude and AI agents accelerated, and the filing would put Anthropic ahead of OpenAI to the public markets. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/anthropic-file-ipo-could-top-063500275.html)

### Researchers say China-linked hackers ran a near-autonomous AI cyberattack on Taiwan
A four-day campaign combining open-source agent frameworks reportedly deployed up to eight AI agents at once to map 21 Taiwanese government systems, compromise 85 accounts, and exfiltrate more than 2,500 personnel records before expanding to the nuclear safety agency and energy companies. Researchers call it one of the first documented cases of a largely autonomous, AI-driven intrusion against a government. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/08/12/near-autonomous-ai-agents-attack-taiwans-nuclear-safety-agency/5287055), [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/197079/apt/china-linked-hackers-use-ai-agents-in-autonomous-attack-on-taiwan.html)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub ships Agent Plugins 1.0, a portable format for Copilot skills and MCP servers
Agent Plugins 1.0 is now generally available across VS Code, Copilot CLI, the Copilot SDK, and the Copilot app on all plans, letting developers package agent skills and MCP servers once and reuse them across every Copilot surface. Existing plugins keep working without migration. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-12-agent-plugins-1-0-in-vs-code-copilot-cli-and-the-copilot-app/)

### Kimi K3 and MAI-Code-1.1-Flash roll out as new GitHub Copilot models
Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 is rolling out to Copilot Pro, Pro+, Max, Business, and Enterprise plans alongside Microsoft's MAI-Code-1.1-Flash, which adds native image understanding and improvements to coding quality, instruction following, and tool use. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-13-github-copilot-weekly-releases-august-10/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Broadcom seeks $60B–$100B in debt to bankroll Anthropic's AI chip buildout
Broadcom is negotiating with lenders including Blackstone and Apollo Global Management for a special-purpose-vehicle debt deal — potentially a $30B junior tranche plus a $60–70B senior-secured tranche — to fund custom AI chips for Anthropic, extending the $35B financing pact the three firms struck in June. — [Bloomberg via Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/broadcom-seeks-more-60-billion-203818587.html)

### Marvell hands Google a $12.2B stake option in expanded custom AI chip deal
The warrant lets Google buy up to roughly 59 million Marvell shares tied to future TPU-related chip purchases, potentially making Google one of Marvell's largest shareholders and delivering the chipmaker up to $120B in revenue through fiscal 2033; Marvell shares jumped nearly 10% on the news. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/marvell-google-ai-chips.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Maximum-severity Entra ID flaw was exploited before Microsoft's fix landed
CVE-2026-69836 (CVSS 10.0) let an unauthenticated attacker remotely execute code inside Microsoft's cloud identity service via unsafe deserialization, with no privileges or user interaction required — a worst-case bug for the service underpinning Microsoft 365 and Azure access. Microsoft says it has fully mitigated the flaw server-side and no customer action is needed, but confirms it was exploited in the wild. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-entra-id-flaw-cvss-100.html), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/21/microsoft-entra-id-vulnerability-cve-2026-69836/)
