*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Pentagon's GenAI.mil adds ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government alongside Gemini
The Defense Department's secure generative-AI portal, which launched with Google's Gemini and has already onboarded 1.7 million of its 3 million personnel, now bundles custom military versions of ChatGPT and Grok — while a contractual dispute over surveillance and lethal-use guardrails has kept Claude off the platform. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/31/the-pentagon-now-has-its-own-version-of-chatgpt-and-grok/)

### OpenAI to cut off Cursor's model access after SpaceX's $60B acquisition
OpenAI invoked a change-of-control clause to end Cursor's access to its models on November 12, citing concerns SpaceX won't honor its terms of service; Cursor's CEO says OpenAI models account for only about 5% of Cursor's traffic, with Anthropic and Google models covering the rest. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)

## 🛠 Development

### Salesforce and Anthropic launch "Claudeforce," making Claude the default reasoning model across Agentforce
The expanded partnership plugs Claude into Agentforce's Atlas Reasoning Engine, Agentforce Vibes, Agentforce Coworker, and Slack, and adds a Claude plugin with 37 prebuilt sales skills — plus availability inside Salesforce's Trust Boundary via Bedrock for regulated customers. — [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/salesforce-and-anthropic-announce-claudeforce/)

### GitHub tightens Copilot billing and rolls out a global default model policy
Starting September 1, GitHub reenables Copilot Business/Enterprise sign-ups for credit-card and PayPal customers under stricter account vetting, while a newly generally-available default model policy auto-enables new GA models for users unless admins opt out. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-upcoming-changes-to-github-copilot-policies-and-billing/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Tim Cook steps down as Apple CEO; John Ternus takes over
Cook's 15-year run as chief executive ended August 31, with hardware engineering chief John Ternus becoming CEO effective September 1 and Cook moving to executive chairman, where he'll keep handling Apple's relationships with the Trump administration and Beijing. — [9to5Mac](https://9to5mac.com/2026/08/31/tim-cook-last-day-john-ternus-apple-ceo/)

### Google in advanced talks for $1.5B+ deal to license Mechanize's AI coding tech and hire its staff
The proposed hybrid license-and-hire deal would bring Mechanize's AI-agent benchmarking and evaluation technology to Google, deepening its push into agentic coding tools as competition with Anthropic and OpenAI shifts from chatbots to autonomous software agents. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/google-eyes-1-5b-investment-172001789.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Aurora ransomware operators used Cursor's AI agent for hands-on exploitation
Researchers at Gambit Security and CloudSEK found the Aurora (Aur0ra) ransomware crew fed Cursor's AI coding agent VPN credentials and network footholds to help plan and execute post-compromise attacks against at least 10 organizations, bypassing the model's refusals by framing the work as a "simulation" — estimated to make the group 30-50% faster. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/aurora-ransomware-operators-use-cursor.html)

### FulcrumSec claims theft of 86GB from Manchester Airports Group via exposed API credentials
The extortion group says it pulled customer data — already disclosed by MAG as affecting 8.7 million people — out of the airport operator's Iterable marketing platform using API keys that had been left embedded in public-facing JavaScript. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fulcrumsec-claims-manchester-airports-hack-theft-of-86-gb-of-data/)
