*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### China dismisses Amodei's AI slowdown call as "self-serving"
State-run China Daily and Global Times blasted Dario Amodei's weekend essay urging labs to "pace the frontier," calling it a Cold War-style attempt to disguise competitive containment as safety concern, while China's foreign ministry warned against "fearmongering" — sharpening the geopolitical fault line running through the industry's safety debate. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-15/chinese-state-media-dismisses-self-serving-ai-slowdown-call)

### Anthropic, OpenAI and Google DeepMind quietly discuss a joint AI safety standards body
The three labs have held working-group meetings since July — predating Amodei's public slowdown call — on an industry-led body, modeled loosely on Wall Street's FINRA, that would set common evaluation standards for advanced models before deployment; the companies still disagree on how much government involvement it should have, and smaller AI firms worry such standards could become a barrier to entry. — [CNN Business](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/14/tech/ai-standards-body)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub Copilot adds Jira integration and adaptive model routing via Project HydraFusion
This week's Copilot release brings Jira issues into a shared canvas that Copilot can carry through investigation and PR prep, moves Project HydraFusion's automatic cost/quality model routing into Copilot CLI's /experimental, and gives JetBrains admins centralized sandbox policy controls. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-10-github-copilot-weekly-releases-september-7/)

### CodeQL 2.27.0 adds Linux ARM64 support and a new Rust security query
The release lets GitHub's code-scanning engine run natively on Linux ARM64, adds a new Rust security query plus expanded Java/Kotlin and C# framework coverage, and improves analysis accuracy across several languages — rolling out automatically to all code-scanning users on GitHub.com. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-09-codeql-2-27-0-adds-support-for-linux-arm64/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### AI slowdown call triggers a global chip-stock selloff
Nvidia, Broadcom, AMD, Intel and Marvell all fell sharply and SoftBank dropped 10% in Tokyo after Amodei's essay and its backing from Sam Altman and Elon Musk read to investors as a demand shock for the AI hardware stack; South Korea's SK Hynix and Samsung, and Europe's ASML and Infineon, fell in sympathy. — [CNN Business](https://us.cnn.com/2026/09/14/business/ai-stocks-slide-slowdown-development-amodei-altman-intl)

### South Korea's exports smash records as AI chip demand surges
Semiconductor shipments jumped 270% in the first ten days of September, pushing chips to 47% of all outbound shipments and total exports to a record $709.4 billion so far this year — putting South Korea on track to become the fourth country to cross $1 trillion in annual exports. — [Nikkei Asia](https://asia.nikkei.com/economy/trade/south-korea-exports-surpass-full-year-record-hit-709.4bn-so-far)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Microsoft ships record 974-CVE Patch Tuesday, including two exploited zero-days
September's release is the largest in Patch Tuesday history, fixing 723 Windows flaws and 222 Office bugs; two were already under active exploitation — CVE-2026-85880, a Windows ALPC heap overflow enabling local privilege escalation, and CVE-2026-81963, a Windows Update Stack link-resolution flaw. — [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/microsoft-patches-record-974-vulnerabilities-including-two-exploited-zero-days/)

### Florida confirms DMV database breach via stolen police officer credentials
FLHSMV confirmed attackers accessed its DAVID driver-lookup database using a Plant City police employee's credentials that had been improperly stored on a personal device; extortion group ShinyHunters claims it stole over 200,000 driver records, a number the agency has not confirmed. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/florida-confirms-dmv-database-breached-via-stolen-police-account/)
