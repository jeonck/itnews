*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI rolls out GPT-6 and a new "Intelligent UI" to every ChatGPT tier
OpenAI began shipping GPT-6 alongside "Intelligent UI," which lets ChatGPT answer with interactive charts, buttons, forms, and calculators instead of plain text when that format fits the question better; paid tiers got access October 7, with Free and Go users following October 8. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/07/chatgpt-is-getting-a-lot-more-visual-with-the-launch-of-a-new-interface/)

## 🛠 Development

### Supabase raises $150M and acquires Turso to build an agent-native database stack
Supabase says AI agents and coding tools now create 70% of its new databases, and is folding in Turso's serverless, Rust-rebuilt SQLite platform to make spinning up a database as cheap and disposable as writing a file. — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/supabase-raises-150-million-and-acquires-turso/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### China's Manus raises $500M+ after Meta's $2B acquisition was blocked
Manus parent Butterfly Effect closed its first funding round since Beijing scuttled Meta's buyout attempt, with Boyu Capital and IDG Capital leading alongside existing backers Tencent and HSG; the AI agent startup had reportedly been in talks for a $4B valuation. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/chinas-manus-raises-over-500m-in-first-funding-round-since-split-with-meta)

### AI leaderboard Arena raises $200M, nearly doubling its valuation to $3.1B in 10 months
Lightspeed and Khosla led the Series B for the model-evaluation platform (formerly LMArena), a sign of how much investor appetite remains for the infrastructure that ranks AI models rather than the models themselves. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/popular-ai-leaderboard-arena-nearly-doubles-valuation-to-3-1b-valuation-in-10-months/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Critical Atlassian flaw now under active exploitation after public PoC
Attackers began hitting CVE-2026-21589, a 9.3-severity path-traversal bug across eight self-hosted Atlassian Data Center products including Jira, Confluence, and Bitbucket, within hours of researchers publishing proof-of-concept exploit code; watchTowr separately showed how it can be chained for admin-level access in Crowd-integrated deployments. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-critical-atlassian-flaw-after-public-poc-release/)

### Pentagon personnel data breach exposes ~3 million military-connected people
The Defense Manpower Data Center is mailing notification letters after discovering unauthorized access to an unencrypted file-sharing server that ran from October 2025 until July 2026; exposed data includes Social Security numbers, names, and military occupational details, with a year of credit monitoring now offered. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-breach-pentagon-human-resources-management-system-steal-data-of-nearly-3-million-people)
