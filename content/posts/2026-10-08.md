*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic launches Claude Haiku 5.5, its cheapest and fastest small model yet
Priced about 75% below Haiku 4.5 ($0.10/$0.50 per million input/output tokens for prompts under 100k), the model adds an adjustable effort setting and can run as a subagent alongside Opus 5.5 and Sonnet 5.5 on coding tasks; it's live now on Anthropic's API, AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-haiku-5-5)

### Mistral opens a public preview of Large 4, its new flagship model
The 1.05-trillion-parameter mixture-of-experts model supports a million-token context window and claims leading cybersecurity benchmark scores; Mistral is letting security researchers stress-test a less-restricted version for three weeks before open weights ship later this month. — [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/mistral-debuts-large-4-open-weight-model-preview-cbf9e0b5)

## 🛠 Development

### Linux turns 35 as Open Source Summit Europe kicks off in Prague
The Linux Foundation's three-day event (Oct 7–9) features a rare on-stage conversation between Linus Torvalds and Ericsson's Dirk Hohndel on the future of vendor-neutral collaboration, alongside tracks on open AI/data, digital trust, and embedded systems. — [Linux Foundation](https://www.linuxfoundation.org/press/open-source-summit-embedded-linux-conference-europe-2026-schedule-champions-open-source-innovation-and-marks-35-years-of-linux)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Global VC funding hits $159B in Q3 as AI takes a record 64% share
Crunchbase counted an all-time-high 27 mega-rounds of $1B+ in the quarter — including $5B rounds each for Databricks and Safe Superintelligence — even as the total fell 25% from Q2; AI-driven startups alone raised $102B, with US companies capturing 57% of global dollars. — [Four Week MBA](https://fourweekmba.com/ai-crunchbase-q3-2026-159b-venture-27-billion-dollar-rounds-ai/)

### Autonomous-security startup Armadin raises $255.5M Series B at $2.5B+ valuation
Led by a16z and Accel with Bain Capital Ventures and Redpoint joining as new backers, the round brings Mandiant founder Kevin Mandia's AI-agent attack-path platform to roughly $445M raised total just seven months after it emerged from stealth. — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/armadin-raises-255-5-million-series-b-at-over-2-5-billion-valuation/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### ASOS confirms breach after hackers send ransom threats via app push notifications
Customers received rogue in-app alerts claiming attackers had compromised ASOS's Snowflake instance; the retailer confirmed unauthorized access to a third-party communications platform exposing names and contact details, while Snowflake says it found no compromise of its own platform. Shares fell over 10% on the news. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/asos-confirms-data-breach-after-hacked-in-app-notifications/)

### Southern Company breach exposes ~400,000 Georgia Power and Alabama Power customers
An unauthorized third party accessed the utility's online customer portal, potentially exposing names, addresses, phone numbers, emails, and partial Social Security numbers; the company says it found no evidence of ongoing access and is offering a year of free credit monitoring. — [Fox10 News](https://www.fox10tv.com/2026/10/05/southern-company-hit-by-data-breach/)
