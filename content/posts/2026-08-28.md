*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic signs $45B compute deal with Nscale
Anthropic will spend $45 billion over six years to rent roughly 460MW of AI computing power from British infrastructure firm Nscale's West Virginia data center, running on Nvidia's next-generation Vera Rubin chips; the deal is part of a compute-buying spree ahead of a potential IPO. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/anthropic-and-nscale-strike-45-billion-cloud-deal-sources-say.html)

### Nvidia agrees to buy Hugging Face for $12.9B
Nvidia has reached a deal to acquire the open-source AI model and dataset hub, which hosts roughly 2.5 million models and 950,000 datasets for over 13 million registered users — a move that pulls one of the AI ecosystem's most widely used neutral platforms under a chipmaker's ownership. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/nvidia-hugging-face-acquisition.html)

## 🛠 Development

### Kubernetes v1.37 "Garhwal" ships with 67 enhancements
The release graduates 16 features to stable — including pod-level resources and pod certificates — while pruning legacy components like kube-dns, IPVS, and cgroup v1 support, continuing the project's push toward production-readiness over feature sprawl. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/08/26/kubernetes-cleans-house-bins-legacy-kube-dns-ipvs-and-cgroup-v1/)

### LLVM 23.1 released with AMD Zen 6 and partial C++26 support
The first stable release of the LLVM 23 series adds AMD Zen 6 and initial Nvidia Rigel core support, x86 Lightweight Fault Isolation, Arm C1-Ultra scheduling, and partial C++26 language support. — [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/LLVM-23.1-Released)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### AWS and Nvidia to deploy 2 million more GPUs in 2027–2028
The expanded partnership adds Blackwell Ultra, Rubin, and Rubin Ultra systems plus new Vera CPUs to AWS infrastructure, bringing Nvidia's total GPU footprint on AWS introduced this year past 3 million; the deal also covers 100,000 GPUs for secure U.S. government AI infrastructure. — [Nvidia Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/aws-and-nvidia-to-deliver-2-million-additional-gpus-and-next-generation-infrastructure-for-agentic-and-physical-ai)

### Stripe agrees to acquire OpenRouter for over $8B
The deal for the "AI model gateway" that routes tokens across 400+ models from 80+ providers is Stripe's largest ever, valuing OpenRouter at roughly 5.4x the $1.3B mark it hit in its Series B just three months earlier, and folds token routing into Stripe's payments infrastructure. — [Stripe](https://stripe.com/newsroom/news/stripe-agrees-to-acquire-openrouter)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### CISA warns of active exploitation of critical Citrix NetScaler flaw
CVE-2026-8452, a memory-corruption bug in NetScaler ADC and Gateway, is being exploited in "pray and spray" attacks that deploy web shells; researchers showed it can escalate from denial-of-service to unauthenticated remote code execution. Federal agencies must patch by August 29. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-hackers-now-exploiting-citrix-netscaler-rce-flaw-in-attacks/)

### PaperCut rushes emergency patch for unauthenticated RCE zero-day
A flaw affecting every supported version of PaperCut NG and MF let attackers bypass authorization checks and execute arbitrary code; PaperCut shipped out-of-cycle builds for Windows, Linux, and macOS within hours of confirming active exploitation. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/papercut-warns-of-ng-mf-flaw-exploited-in-zero-day-attacks/)

### Carhartt confirms breach after ShinyHunters leak
The clothing retailer confirmed roughly 12.9 million accounts were affected — about half of what the ShinyHunters extortion group originally claimed — after the group published names, emails, phone numbers, and addresses when Carhartt declined to pay a $3.3 million ransom. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/carhartt-data-breach-exposes-information-of-129-million-accounts/)
