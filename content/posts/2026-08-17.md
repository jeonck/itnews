*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI disbands its Preparedness team
OpenAI dissolved the group responsible for evaluating catastrophic model risks — advanced cyber capability, biological misuse, unpredictable model behavior — and redistributed the work across existing biosecurity and cybersecurity teams. It is the third safety unit the company has cut in two years, and it lands while OpenAI is reportedly preparing an IPO, which puts governance back at the center of the investor conversation. — [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2237916/openai-reportedly-disbanded-its-preparedness-team-as-part-of-streamlining-process/), [Calcalist](https://www.calcalistech.com/ctechnews/article/kxpymxav0)

### DeepSeek's V4-Pro leaves preview, and gets more expensive
DeepSeek-V4-Pro-0813 is now generally available across app, web, and API with a 1M-token context window, 384K-token maximum output, and benchmarks tuned for agentic work (87.9 on Terminal Bench 2.1). The V4 family price increase took effect at 16:00 UTC on August 16, along with new peak/off-peak billing that halves off-hours rates — worth re-checking if your cost model assumed the old pricing. — [DeepSeek API docs](https://api-docs.deepseek.com/news/news260813/), [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/deepseek-ships-v4-pro-as-its-flagship-model-leaves-preview/)

### ChatGPT is getting a "Computer History" memory
OpenAI is building a feature that lets ChatGPT recall what you did on your computer without taking continuous screenshots. If it ships, the assistant stops being session-bound and starts being ambient — which is exactly where the privacy argument will be fought. — [Tech Startups roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/17/top-tech-news-today-august-17-2026-ge-microsoft-nvidia-open-stripe-unitree-more/)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub Copilot ships Agent Plugins 1.0
The plugin standard is now live across VS Code, the Copilot CLI, and the Copilot app, with Gemini 3.7 Flash and Grok 4.6 joining the model roster. Defining the plugin interface early is how GitHub makes itself the default integration target for every agent-tool vendor — the same play that made it the default place to host code. — [Developer Tools & Platforms weekly](https://www.originbrief.app/en/reports/developer-tools-platforms/2026-08-17/weekly)

### DeepSeek Harness crosses 135,000 GitHub stars in four days
The developer-preview agent harness ships a design where the model, tools, sessions, loop, sandbox, persistence, and web UI are all swappable plugins. The velocity signals how much appetite there is for an agent runtime nobody owns. — [Developer Tools & Platforms weekly](https://www.originbrief.app/en/reports/developer-tools-platforms/2026-08-17/weekly)

### AWS Bedrock adds OpenAI's Daybreak models; Claude Opus 5 lands in GovCloud
Bedrock picking up Daybreak Red and Blue continues the multi-model consolidation on the hyperscalers, and Opus 5 in GovCloud opens frontier-model use to regulated public-sector workloads. — [Developer Tools & Platforms weekly](https://www.originbrief.app/en/reports/developer-tools-platforms/2026-08-17/weekly)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Stripe closes its OpenRouter acquisition for more than $7B
Stripe is buying the AI model gateway — 8M users, 400+ models — for over 5x the $1.3B valuation OpenRouter carried after its Series B in May. A payments company buying the routing layer is a bet that metering and billing AI traffic is the same business as metering and billing anything else. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-16/stripe-nears-deal-to-buy-ai-firm-openrouter-for-over-7-billion)

### Nvidia is reportedly backstopping ~$100B of OpenAI data center financing
A chip vendor guaranteeing the debt that buys its own chips is a circularity worth watching closely — especially alongside a WSJ tally putting roughly $3 trillion of AI infrastructure commitments across nine large tech companies only partly visible on their balance sheets. — [Tech Startups roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/17/top-tech-news-today-august-17-2026-ge-microsoft-nvidia-open-stripe-unitree-more/)

### Higgsfield raises $400M at a $5.4B valuation
The AI video startup says annualized revenue went from about $20M a year ago to $700M this month, with enterprise now the majority of income. Backers include Goldman Sachs, Intel, DST Global, and Liberty Global. — [Tech Startups roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/17/top-tech-news-today-august-17-2026-ge-microsoft-nvidia-open-stripe-unitree-more/)

### Unitree lists on Shanghai's STAR Market this week
The Chinese humanoid-robotics maker goes public on August 19 with record oversubscription — the first real public-market price on the humanoid thesis. — [Tech Startups roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/17/top-tech-news-today-august-17-2026-ge-microsoft-nvidia-open-stripe-unitree-more/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### GitHub was down for more than three hours
Starting around 13:40 UTC, roughly one in five web and API requests failed, with about half of archive and raw-content downloads erroring out; Actions, Issues, Pull Requests, Webhooks, SAML/OIDC/SCIM auth, and Copilot were all affected before mitigation at 16:59 UTC. If your CI, your deploys, and your agents all terminate at the same vendor, this is your blast radius. — [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/github-outage-disrupts-developers-worldwide-in-latest-setback-for-microsoft-coding-platform/), [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2238231/github-has-been-down-for-most-of-the-morning/)

### CVSS 10.0 SAP Commerce Cloud RCE exploited three days after patch
CVE-2026-58231 is an improper-authorization flaw in the Data Hub Adapter extension that lets an unauthenticated attacker abuse a default auth client to run arbitrary code. Honeypots caught exploitation attempts on August 14 — three days after SAP shipped the fix. Patch now. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/sap-commerce-cloud-cve-2026-58231.html), [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/critical-sap-commerce-cloud-vulnerability-exploited-3-days-after-disclosure/)

### France's tax authority breach exposes ~678,000 people
A national revenue agency is about as high-value a target as identity data gets, and this one is now in the wild. — [Tech Startups roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/17/top-tech-news-today-august-17-2026-ge-microsoft-nvidia-open-stripe-unitree-more/)

### GE and Philips investigating Clop breach claims
Clop is claiming systems access and data theft at both companies; both say they are investigating. Clop's pattern is mass exploitation of a single managed-file-transfer flaw, so a confirmed hit here usually means a longer victim list is coming. — [Tech Startups roundup](https://techstartups.com/2026/08/17/top-tech-news-today-august-17-2026-ge-microsoft-nvidia-open-stripe-unitree-more/)
