*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Sakana AI ships Fugu Max and Fugu Ultra v2, an orchestrator instead of a single model
Rather than one model, Fugu routes each request across a pool of open-weight and specialist models (including Nvidia's Nemotron family); Fugu Max targets cost at $2/$6 per million input/output tokens — 40-60% cheaper than Sonnet 5 or GPT-5.6 Terra on output — while Fugu Ultra v2 chases the strongest answer on hard multi-step work. — [Sakana AI](https://sakana.ai/fugu-max-release/)

### Google ships a native Gemini desktop app for Windows with an Alt+Space shortcut
The app is available globally for Windows 10 and 11, overlaying Gemini on top of any active window for drafting, summarizing and image/video generation, plus an agentic "Gemini Spark" mode tied to connected Google apps — a direct challenge to Microsoft Copilot's OS-level integration. — [Google Workspace Updates](https://workspaceupdates.googleblog.com/2026/09/the-gemini-desktop-app-is-now-available-for-Windows.html)

## 🛠 Development

### VS Code 1.137 adds PR/issue URL attachments and a "Rubber Duck" second-opinion mode
Pasting a GitHub issue or PR URL into a new chat session now auto-attaches it as context, agent chats can start without being tied to a workspace, and an experimental `/rubber-duck` command asks a second model to review the primary agent's work for missed edge cases. — [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/updates/v1_137)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Oracle's cloud backlog swells to $664 billion after $30B+ in new AI contracts
Cloud infrastructure revenue more than doubled to $7.4 billion for the quarter as remaining performance obligations jumped $209 billion year-over-year; capital expenditure soared to $28.5 billion from $8.5 billion a year ago as Oracle races to build out data-center capacity. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/10/oracle-orcl-q1-earnings-report-2027.html)

### Pentagon in talks to lend AI cloud startup Fluidstack $5 billion
The Office of Strategic Capital loan, first reported by the Wall Street Journal, would fund domestic manufacturing capacity for data-center power and cooling equipment rather than a specific facility, reflecting Defense Department concern over the fragility of the US AI-infrastructure supply chain. — [Data Center Dynamics](https://www.datacenterdynamics.com/en/news/pentagon-in-talks-to-loan-fluidstack-5bn-report/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### AI agents autonomously ran a campaign that breached 395 organizations via PaperCut flaws
A likely Russian-speaking attacker built exploits for two PaperCut NG/MF vulnerabilities (CVE-2026-81578, CVE-2026-82078), then handed most of the intrusion work to hundreds of AI agents combining OpenAI's Codex harness with DeepSeek models; the campaign compromised 440+ instances across 48 countries, harvesting credentials from 280 victims and hitting admin-level access at 12 organizations — at one point breaching 11 organizations in 26 seconds. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/papercut-attacker-uses-hundreds-of-ai.html)

### CISA sets a same-day federal deadline for critical Cisco, Citrix and Fortinet flaws
The KEV catalog additions include a maximum-severity (CVSS 10.0) authentication bypass in Cisco Secure Firewall Management Center (CVE-2026-20079), a 9.3-severity NetScaler ADC/Gateway auth bypass (CVE-2026-19490) that saw a surge of honeypot exploitation attempts, and a FortiOS heap overflow (CVE-2025-25249); federal agencies must patch all three by today, September 12. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/cisa-flags-exploited-cisco-citrix.html)
