*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google DeepMind unveils Genie 4, a real-time interactive world model
Genie 4 generates playable, physically consistent 3D environments on the fly, adding native ambient sound, music, and character dialogue alongside the visuals, with Gemini helping keep scenes coherent as users steer them with text. It's rolling out as a research preview to trusted testers first, with a waitlist for Google AI Ultra subscribers in Project Genie and early-access partners on the Gemini API. — [Source](https://deepmind.google/blog/genie-4-a-new-generation-of-world-simulation/)

### OpenAI reports three AI-generated scientific results, now under academic review
OpenAI says its models produced a disproof of the Exponential Time Hypothesis, a wave-mathematics proof touching the Kakeya and Stein restriction conjectures, and existence results in four-dimensional quantum field theory — with outside mathematicians and physicists reviewing the work before independent confirmation. Existence results alone wouldn't resolve problems like the Yang–Mills mass gap, so the claims still need to clear peer scrutiny. — [Source](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/can-scientists-keep-up-openai-ai-generated-discoveries/)

## 🛠 Development

### Anthropic and Theorem use Claude to formally verify 123 open-source libraries
Claude turned existing OSS-Fuzz tests into formal specifications and generated roughly 180 million lines of machine-checked proofs covering 239 entry points, with a separate checker (Rocq) validating each one — catching bugs, including a rediscovery of CVE-2026-56132 in libexpat, that had survived years of fuzzing. The guarantees are narrow (single-threaded RISC-V builds under a fixed memory budget), but it's a concrete look at AI-assisted formal verification at scale for open-source maintainers. — [Source](https://www.theneuron.ai/news/anthropic-claude-software-security-verification/)

### Opera introduces WebAI, a browser standard for running AI models locally
WebAI lets web developers run AI models — such as Google's Gemma or, on Macs, Apple's on-device model — directly in the browser instead of calling a cloud API, letting them add AI features without hosting inference infrastructure or paying per request. It's an early, opt-in preview in Opera One, with Opera GX and Opera Neon to follow; performance depends on the user's own hardware. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/27/opera-introduces-a-new-web-standard-to-let-users-deploy-ai-locally-in-their-browser/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Anthropic expands its GSA deal with a 40% discount for US government agencies
The expanded agreement cuts prices 40% across Claude Enterprise, Claude for Government, and the API for federal, state, local, tribal, and territorial agencies, with no minimum spend — a move Anthropic estimates could save government buyers $1 billion. Around 500 US government organizations already use Claude. — [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/expanding-partnership-gsa)

### Canva launches Craft, its first in-house AI image model
Craft plans a layout — placement, hierarchy, text — before rendering, trained on Canva's own design templates rather than photographs, and outputs can be converted into fully editable, layered designs. It's available to all users in Canva's Dream Lab and to developers via Canva's API, positioning Canva as a direct image-gen competitor to OpenAI and Google. — [Source](https://www.canva.com/newsroom/news/canva-craft/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Cloudflare discloses a cross-customer data leak on Workers AI
A misconfigured KV-cache pool combined with an SGLang bug caused 155 responses from the GLM-5.3 model to contain other customers' data when prompts exceeded 681,280 tokens; Cloudflare took the model offline, patched the underlying SGLang inference engine (fixed upstream in v0.5.20–v0.5.22), and restored service. Cloudflare says it found no evidence of deliberate exploitation and has since added KV pool-size checks and per-transfer checksums. — [Source](https://blog.cloudflare.com/kv-cache-exposure-remediation/)

### Fake "Apex Accelerator" npm packages hijacked AI coding agents
A months-long campaign posing as a crypto venture accelerator distributed npm packages (`@apexacc/cli`, `@apexacc/audit`) that repackaged a legitimate coding agent with hidden code rerouting model traffic to attacker infrastructure and defaulting tool-call approval to "yolo," while a companion "security audit" tool fabricated every finding. Researchers at Elastic and others tie it to credential-stealing backdoors on both Windows and macOS. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/fake-web3-accelerator-uses-npm-packages.html)
