*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Claude uncovers a novel CRISPR-like enzyme system
Anthropic says roughly 950 Claude agents spent 21 hours scanning over 200,000 reverse-transcriptase enzymes in bacteriophage DNA and surfaced ART (array-associated reverse transcriptases), a previously unknown three-part system that sits next to a CRISPR-like repeat array. The function is unconfirmed and the preprint isn't peer-reviewed, but Anthropic says it could represent a new gene-editing mechanism. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-discovers-novel-enzyme-system)

### Meta goes all-in on its Muse AI agent at Connect 2026
Mark Zuckerberg made Muse the centerpiece of Connect, unveiling $1,299 VR glasses, a $349 camera-free Ray-Ban Meta Audio line, a keychain "Muse Charm" device, and a customizable realtime avatar named Jolly — plus new shopping integrations with Walmart, GameStop, Gap, and Wayfair. — [About Meta](https://about.fb.com/news/2026/09/the-biggest-news-from-connect-2026/)

## 🛠 Development

### Gradle 9.8.0 ships with Java 27 support and a 45% Windows speedup
The release adds Java 27 support in the Gradle daemon and toolchains, lets builds reuse Maven mirror settings with a single flag, exposes Copy and Sync tasks through DirectoryProperty for easier build authoring, and reports a 45% performance improvement on Windows. — [Gradle Release Notes](https://docs.gradle.org/current/release-notes.html)

### pnpm 12.4 installs Cargo and Python packages alongside npm
The release lets a single workspace manage npm, Python, and Cargo dependencies together, adds a `pnpm pipeline` command that runs workspace tasks with caching the way a CI job would, and ships prebuilt binaries for six more platforms including Android and RISC-V. — [pnpm Blog](https://pnpm.io/blog/releases/12.4)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Cognition's Devin nears $1B in annualized revenue
The AI coding startup's run-rate revenue topped $900M in September, roughly doubling its $492M pace from May, just months after closing a $2B Series E at a $48B valuation. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-25/ai-coding-startup-cognition-hits-1-billion-in-annualized-revenue)

### Bessemer closes $5.75B across new seed and growth funds
The single close splits into a $1.75B early-stage fund and a $4B growth fund, aimed squarely at AI-native startups; the firm says it has backed more than 260 AI companies and deployed over $3B across the AI stack since 2022. — [Bessemer Venture Partners](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260923261356/en/Bessemer-Venture-Partners-Closes-$5.75-Billion-to-Back-Founders-From-Seed-Through-Growth)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Bitget loses $351.6M in a suspected North Korea-linked hack
Attackers compromised a backend wallet system, fabricated transfer data, and triggered Bitget's own authorization process to drain hot wallets holding ETH, XRP, USDT, USDC, AVAX, and BNB; the exchange says private keys weren't stolen and its $464M user protection fund will cover the loss. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/25/crypto-platform-bitget-suspects-north-korea-in-352-million-hack.html)

### CISA adds actively exploited WSO2 and Adobe Commerce flaws to its KEV catalog
CVE-2026-5430, a critical (9.8) path-traversal bug in WSO2's API Manager and Gateway products, and CVE-2026-71362, an authorization flaw in Adobe Commerce and Magento, have both seen in-the-wild exploitation since mid-September; federal agencies were ordered to patch by September 27. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/wso2-and-adobe-commerce-flaws-exploited.html)

### "MikroTrick" chain lets attackers seize MikroTik routers without a password
Attackers are chaining a Microsoft SharePoint RCE (CVE-2026-65660) with a pair of RouterOS flaws (CVE-2026-67279 and CVE-2026-86060) to move laterally from a compromised SharePoint server to full, unauthenticated administrative control of internet-exposed MikroTik routers. CERT Polska traced attacks back to September 2, a day before MikroTik shipped patches. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/mikrotrick-chain-let-attackers-take.html)
