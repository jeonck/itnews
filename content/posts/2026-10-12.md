## 🤖 AI / ML

### Nadella says AI models need an "emergency brake" humans can pull
Microsoft's CEO argued publicly that AI systems need a human-controlled kill switch as capabilities scale, echoing his earlier line that pursuing superintelligence "is not worth pursuing" unless it stays under human control. The remarks add Microsoft's weight to a broader industry debate over pacing frontier development that Dario Amodei and Sam Altman have also joined this year. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/10/microsofts-satya-nadella-says-ai-models-need-an-emergency-brake/)

### Isomorphic Labs in talks for $40B+ valuation just months after $2.1B round
Google DeepMind's drug-discovery spinout is reportedly negotiating a new funding round that could value it at $40 billion or more, barely five months after closing a $2.1 billion round led by Thrive Capital in May. The jump underscores how much investor appetite remains for AI-driven biotech despite a cooling in some other corners of the AI funding market. — [Bloomberg via Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/161156-deepmind-spinout-isomorphic-labs-eyes-40b-valuation-up-from-2-1b-round-i/)

## 🛠 Development

### Cloudflare acquires the Deno team, winds down its standalone runtime
Deno's entire engineering team is joining Cloudflare, with plans to fold ideas from Deno's "celld" implementation into Cloudflare's open-source workerd runtime so that Workers and Durable Objects can finally be self-hosted at scale. Deno's standalone runtime gets a year of bug-fix support and Deno Deploy will shut down in six months, with paying customers migrated to Cloudflare Workers. — [Cloudflare Blog](https://blog.cloudflare.com/deno-joins-cloudflare/)

### GitHub ships local sandboxing for Copilot CLI to general availability
GitHub's local sandboxing for Copilot CLI, the Copilot app, and VS Code Agent Host sessions is now generally available, restricting the files, network access, and credentials that AI-run commands can touch based on developer or org policy. The feature, powered by Microsoft's eXecution Container tech, gives teams a concrete lever against the risk of letting coding agents run commands unsupervised. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-07-local-sandboxing-for-gitHub-copilot-now-generally-available)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Flock Safety to cut ~270 jobs as AI surveillance backlash reaches its own staff
The license-plate-recognition company plans to lay off about 18% of its roughly 1,500-person workforce following a voluntary buyout program, after dozens of cities canceled contracts amid privacy backlash this year. The CEO has said the backlash's biggest toll so far has been on internal morale rather than revenue. — [Reuters via NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/business/flock-safety-cutting-about-270-jobs-amid-buyout-and-backlash/tldr)

### Nous Research raises $90M to push open-source Hermes agent into the enterprise
The Series B, led by Robot Ventures with Nvidia, Microsoft's M12, and Samsung participating, values the open-source AI lab at $1.5 billion. Hermes Agent has been downloaded 22.7 million times since its February MIT-licensed release, and the new funding goes toward an enterprise version that lets businesses pick models by price and task. — [WSJ via Dealroom](https://dealroom.co/news/160592-nous-research-raises-90m-at-1-5b-valuation-for-open-source-ai/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Max-severity SonicWall SMA1000 flaw already drawing exploitation attempts
Security researcher honeypots detected exploitation attempts against CVE-2026-102255, a pre-auth SSRF flaw rated CVSS 10.0 in SonicWall's SMA1000 Appliance WorkPlace interface, just days after SonicWall patched it. More than 400 SMA1000 appliances remain exposed online, and SonicWall's advisory offers no workaround short of upgrading. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/max-severity-sonicwall-sma1000-flaw-now-exploited-in-attacks/)

### CISA adds five old flaws to its exploited-vulnerabilities list after China-linked campaign
A joint advisory from the US, UK, Australia, Canada, Japan, New Zealand, and Spain says a China-based company, Integrity Technology Group, has been chaining eight vulnerabilities — including decade-old bugs in ProFTPD, ISC BIND, and Apache Struts — tied to the Flax Typhoon threat actor to gain initial access and steal data, with password spraying against Exchange servers for persistence. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/flax-typhoon-exploits-five-flaws-as.html)
