*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Judge rules Pentagon's blacklisting of Anthropic illegal
U.S. District Judge Rita Lin found the Department of Defense violated the First Amendment by designating Anthropic a "supply chain risk" in retaliation for the company refusing to let its models be used for mass surveillance and fully autonomous weapons, calling the move "illegal and baseless." The government is expected to appeal. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/28/judge-blocks-pentagon-anthropic-blacklist)

### Meta closes another privacy loophole in its smart glasses
Meta is rolling out an update that stops Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta, and Meta AI glasses from continuing to record if the capture-indicator LED is covered mid-recording, closing a gap left by an earlier fix that only blocked recordings from starting with the light covered. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/08/28/meta-ray-ban-smart-glasses-privacy-led-loophole-update/)

## 🛠 Development

### Hugging Face launches Microduck, a $399 open-source robot for physical AI
Built with Pollen Robotics, the 25cm waddling duck packs 15 motors, a camera, and a depth sensor, with its firmware, simulation tools, and reinforcement-learning stack released openly so developers can experiment with sim-to-real robotics without expensive hardware. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/27/hugging-face-is-selling-a-cute-399-open-source-duck-robot-microduck/)

### AWS to acquire DuckLabs, the company behind DuckDB
AWS is buying the Amsterdam-based team behind the popular embedded analytics database to speed up its own analytics tooling; DuckDB's creators will keep leading the project, which stays free and MIT-licensed under the independent DuckDB Foundation. — [About Amazon](https://www.aboutamazon.com/news/company-news/aws-ducklabs)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### AI assistant startup Instinct raises $350M at a $2.5B valuation
The 23-year-old founder's phone-and-text AI agent, still in private testing, has seen its valuation rocket from $50M to $2.5B in months on investor bets about AI as the next consumer interface — even as critics flag its broad system permissions and data-handling terms. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/26/viral-ai-startup-instinct-has-raised-350-million-at-a-2-5-billion-valuation/)

### Nasuni acquires DryvIQ for AI-ready data governance
The deal adds content-level classification, sensitivity detection, and policy-driven governance across more than 40 cloud and on-prem repositories to Nasuni's File Data Platform — its second data-management acquisition this year after Resilio. — [PR Newswire](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/nasuni-acquires-dryviq-enabling-organizations-to-classify-secure-and-activate-unstructured-data-to-unlock-ai-business-value-302860644.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### 100+ tech and security firms warn of coming wave of AI-powered cyberattacks
OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, Microsoft, AWS, and over a hundred other companies signed an open letter urging governments and industry to act now to protect hospitals, water systems, and internet infrastructure, warning defenders have "a limited window" before AI models make attacks far more scalable. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/27/ai-cyber-defense-letter.html)

### Manchester Airports Group breach exposes 8.7 million customers
MAG confirmed an "unauthorised third party" accessed data tied to Manchester, Stansted, and East Midlands airports, including emails, phone numbers, vehicle registrations, and postcodes from Wi-Fi sign-ups and parking/lounge bookings; no payment data was taken, and the company has notified the NCSC and ICO. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/manchester-airports-group-says-hackers-stole-travelers-data/)

### VMware vCenter flaw under active exploitation across 47 countries
Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-59310, a critical directory-traversal bug in vCenter's Syslog server, to deploy reverse-SSH tools for persistent access, with compromises found at 361 IP addresses worldwide; Broadcom says patching is the only remediation since no workaround exists. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/attackers-exploit-vmware-vcenter.html)
