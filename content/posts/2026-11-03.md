*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google launches Gemini 4 Carbon, a cybersecurity-first frontier model
Google unveiled Gemini 4 Carbon on October 28 as its strongest model yet, built for vulnerability discovery, validation, and remediation plus long-running agentic tasks; it tops Artificial Analysis's Intelligence Index but is initially limited to vetted cyber defenders through Google's Fairwind Program before a wider rollout. — [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/introducing-gemini-4-carbon/)

### Anthropic lets Claude end abusive conversations
Anthropic updated its usage policies on November 1 to let Claude disengage from sustained, purposeless abuse rather than keep responding indefinitely, treating conversation-ending as the primary tool for handling that behavior. — [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/2026-usage-policy-update)

### Google runs an AI model on a satellite chip in orbit
As part of Project Suncatcher, Google ran its Gemma model on a TPU aboard a prototype satellite and had it generate a haiku — an early proof point, not a working data center, toward the company's longer-term ambitions for orbital AI compute. — [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/google-research/project-suncatcher-satellite-response/)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub ships Copilot Fusion, automatic multi-model routing, at Universe 2026
Announced October 28, Copilot Fusion picks and combines AI models per task across a Standard/High/Max tiering instead of making developers choose a single model manually; it works in VS Code, JetBrains, the Copilot CLI, and the new Copilot app, with a 30%-off introductory discount on model costs through November 30. — [Source](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-route-and-orchestrate-models-with-github-copilot-fusion/)

### GitHub Agentic Workflows reaches general availability
Also out of Universe, this GitHub Next project lets teams write coding-agent automations (issue triage, CI failure analysis, doc updates) in Markdown that compile to standard Actions YAML, with read-only-by-default sandboxed execution, threat-detection scanning, and self-hosted runner support now GA for Free, Team, and Enterprise. — [Source](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-github-agentic-workflows-generally-available)

### OpenAI will merge ChatGPT and Codex usage limits
Starting in December, OpenAI is folding Codex and most ChatGPT usage into a single token-based allowance rather than separate quotas, though ordinary Instant-mode chats won't count against it — a change developers leaning on Codex for coding work will want to plan around. — [Source](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/20001625-understanding-chatgpt-and-codex-usage-limits)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Marvell to acquire Lumentum for roughly $115 billion
Marvell announced an all-stock deal on November 2 to buy optical-components maker Lumentum at an implied enterprise value of about $115 billion (a ~24% premium), pairing Marvell's electrical interconnect and custom silicon with Lumentum's lasers and optical transceivers to chase a combined $550 billion AI-interconnect market. — [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001633978/000119312526442758/d163169dex991.htm)

### AWS posts its fastest growth in nearly five years
Amazon's Q3 earnings showed AWS revenue up 39.3% year-over-year to almost $46 billion, its best growth rate since 2022, underscoring how strongly cloud demand tied to AI workloads is still accelerating even as rivals warn about AI-infrastructure debt. — [Source](https://ir.aboutamazon.com/news-release/news-release-details/2026/Amazon-com-Announces-Third-Quarter-Results/)

### OpenAI reportedly scales back new funding round to $20B
OpenAI has cut the target size of its latest pre-IPO raise from an earlier $30 billion goal to about $20 billion, according to people familiar with the talks; OpenAI hasn't confirmed terms, but the lower number suggests investor appetite or valuation expectations shifted since the round was first floated. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/31/openai-reportedly-lowers-funding-target-for-new-round/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Two more actively exploited Ivanti Connect Secure zero-days hit CISA's KEV list
CISA added CVE-2026-111357 (local privilege escalation) and CVE-2026-118527 (arbitrary file upload) on October 30 after Volexity found them chained with an earlier auth-bypass bug to get unauthenticated root access on VPN appliances; the attacker group (UTA0576) harvested credentials and Active Directory secrets, and the exploitation evades Ivanti's own Integrity Checker Tool. — [Source](https://www.rapid7.com/blog/post/etr-additional-ivanti-connect-secure-zero-days-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-111357-cve-2026-118527/)

### Social-engineering breach hits two of Canada's largest law firms
McCarthy Tétrault confirmed a coordinated social-engineering campaign compromised its systems on October 22, with Davies Ward Phillips & Vineberg disclosing a related intrusion on October 23; both firms, which hold unannounced deal and litigation data for major clients, have notified law enforcement amid a wider vishing campaign flagged against Canadian legal and financial firms. — [Source](https://www.mccarthy.ca/en/news-and-announcements/statement-from-mccarthy-tetrault-10-29-2026)

### Microsoft details North Korean hackers' growing use of AI
Microsoft's security team published research October 29 showing North Korean state-linked threat actors increasingly weaving AI tools into their tradecraft — from faster malware development to AI-assisted social engineering — adding detail to how these groups are adapting as AI tooling spreads. — [Source](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/security/blog/2026/10/29/tracking-ai-adoption-in-north-korean-threat-actor-tradecraft/)
