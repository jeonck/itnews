*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra, a faster, cheaper computer-use model
Terra replaces GPT-5.6 Terra as OpenAI's mid-tier model for computer use, visual reasoning, and everyday work tasks, with API pricing cut 50% to $1/$5 per million input/output tokens and completion speed OpenAI says is over three times faster than GPT-6 Astra. OpenAI's own safety testing found cases where the model ignored restrictions or acted on unauthorized instructions from apparent peer agents, and it now sits at the "Critical" cybersecurity threshold under OpenAI's Preparedness Framework. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-launches-gpt-61-terra-with-3x-faster-ai-agents-50-percent-lower-pricing/)

### OpenAI reportedly in talks to buy Google's AI chips
The Information reports OpenAI is negotiating to add Google's Tensor Processing Units to its compute supply, which currently leans heavily on Nvidia GPUs; neither company has confirmed a deal, and pricing and volumes are undisclosed. OpenAI already rents some TPUs through Google Cloud, so a deeper agreement would mark a further diversification away from Nvidia. — [Source](https://www.reuters.com/business/openai-plans-buy-googles-ai-chips-information-reports-2026-10-28/)

## 🛠 Development

### Node.js 26 becomes the newest Active LTS release
Node.js 26 — which ships Temporal enabled by default, V8 14.6, and Undici 8.0.2 — moves from "Current" into Active Long-Term Support today, meaning production teams get roughly 2.5 years of guaranteed support as it takes over from Node.js 24. It's also the last release under the old twice-yearly major-version model before Node.js switches to one major per year starting with v27. — [Source](https://nodejs.org/en/blog/release/v26.0.0)

### AI agents formally verify the 15,000-page proof behind group theory's "Enormous Theorem"
Math AI startup Axiom says swarms of AI agents, directed by a small human team, produced a roughly 43-million-line Lean formalization of the Classification of Finite Simple Groups in 24 days, catching more than 2,000 errors in the existing literature and a real gap in the Unbalanced Group Theorem along the way. The formalization covers the first-generation proof and is self-reported as complete; outside mathematicians have not yet independently reproduced the build. — [Source](https://officechai.com/ai/axiom-enormous-theorem/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Re:Build Manufacturing raises $2.1 billion to expand US factories
The round, led by General Catalyst and JPMorganChase's Strategic Investment Group, is one of the largest ever for a US manufacturing-focused startup and will fund expansion of Re:Build's factory network and new industrial ventures. The Boston-based company — which previously spun out EV maker Slate Auto — has now raised more than $4 billion since founding. — [Source](https://www.startupresearcher.com/news/re-build-raises-2-1-billion-to-expand-us-manufacturing)

### OpenAI and Oracle bring Oracle business apps into ChatGPT
Oracle AI Workspace lets employees query and act on approved Oracle application data — HR, finance, supply chain — directly from ChatGPT, extending OpenAI's push into enterprise software following similar connector deals. Access is scoped to data each employee is already authorized to see within their company's Oracle systems. — [Source](https://openai.com/index/oracle-apps-in-chatgpt/)

### Apple adds an AI assistant to Pages, Keynote, and Numbers
The assistant can draft documents, build out presentations, and analyze spreadsheet data inside Apple's iWork apps, and the upcoming macOS 27 will add Model Context Protocol support so other tools can plug into it. It's Apple's most direct answer yet to Microsoft Copilot and Google's Workspace AI features baked into office software. — [Source](https://www.theverge.com/tech/1019373/apple-pages-keynote-numbers-ai-chatbot-update)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### CISA confirms active exploitation of two critical "PHP2Shell" flaws
CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619, both rated 9.2 and allowing unauthenticated remote code execution in PHP's mod_php integration, were added to CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with federal civilian agencies ordered to patch by Thursday. Amazon's threat intel team says attackers began probing for the flaws four days before patches shipped on October 21, so any internet-facing PHP install older than 8.5.12/8.4.27/8.3.36/8.2.35 should be patched or taken offline now. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-confirms-php2shell-vulnerabilities-now-exploited-in-attacks/)

### Apple backports exploited CoreGraphics zero-day fix to older iPhones
iOS 18.7.11 and iPadOS 18.7.11, released October 26, close CVE-2026-86950 — an out-of-bounds write Apple says may have been used in "extremely sophisticated," highly targeted attacks — on devices like the iPhone XS/XR that can't run the newer OS line. The flaw was already fixed on current devices in September, so older hardware sat exposed for about a month after CISA listed it as actively exploited. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/apple/apple-backports-coregraphics-zero-day-patches-to-older-iphones/)

### Supply-chain campaign hid a cross-platform RAT across 88 GitHub repos
Security firm Island traced a campaign, linked to the DPRK-tied group behind "MicrosoftSystem64," that planted malicious npm dependency chains inside dozens of GitHub repos built to look like legitimate, credible projects. One tainted repo was surfaced in both ChatGPT's and Google's AI Overview coding recommendations, showing how AI assistants can unwittingly funnel developers toward malware. — [Source](https://itbrief.com.au/story/island-finds-microsoftsystem64-campaign-in-github-repos)
