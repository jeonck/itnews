*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic files S-1 for a Nasdaq IPO under ticker "ANT"
Anthropic submitted a public Form S-1 to the SEC on October 26 for a proposed IPO of its Class A common stock, planning to list on the Nasdaq Global Select Market as ANT; share count and price range aren't set yet, and reports put future compute obligations around $417 billion against more than $80 billion in first-half 2026 losses. It's one of the largest tech IPOs ever attempted, with the roadshow potentially starting as early as the week of November 9. — [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/news/s1-sec)

### Mistral releases open weights for trillion-parameter "Large 4," aimed at cyber defense
Mistral AI shipped the open weights for Mistral Large 4 on October 29 — a roughly 1-trillion-parameter model (about 52B active per token) pitched at helping security teams find, reproduce, and patch vulnerabilities and coordinate incident response. Releasing a frontier-scale model as open weights for cybersecurity use is a notable bet on "sovereign" cyber defense, even as critics note the same capability could help attackers once weights are public. — [Source](https://www.kucoin.com/news/flash/mistral-releases-trillion-parameter-ai-model-for-cybersecurity)

## 🛠 Development

### Node.js 26 "Lithium" enters Long Term Support
Node.js 26.13.0 moved into LTS on October 28 and will get updates through April 2029; the 26.x line ships the Temporal API enabled by default, V8 14.6, and Undici 8 as the HTTP layer, and is the last major release on the old six-month cadence before Node.js switches to annual majors starting with 27. — [Source](https://github.com/nodejs/node/releases/tag/v26.13.0)

### Deno team joins Cloudflare; standalone runtime development winds down over the next year
Ryan Dahl's Deno team is joining Cloudflare, the companies announced, with the open-source Deno runtime getting only a year of further bug-fix and security releases and Deno Deploy shutting down six months out. The plan is to fold Dahl's self-hostable "celld" project into Cloudflare's workerd, aiming to make the Workers model easier to self-host outside Cloudflare's own cloud. — [Source](https://itdaily.com/news/business/cloudflare-deno/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### OpenAI-backed Thrive Holdings to take ADT private for $15.3 billion
Thrive Holdings — a permanent-capital vehicle from Thrive Capital's Josh Kushner, in which OpenAI holds a stake — agreed October 29 to buy home-security giant ADT in an all-cash deal, paying $10/share (a 45.6% premium) with financing solely from Apollo. ADT would go private and anchor Thrive's push into AI-driven smart-home security; closing is expected in Q1 2027. — [Source](https://coverager.com/?p=253214)

### Intel to acquire quantum software firm Classiq for roughly $2.2 billion
Intel announced a definitive agreement on October 26 to buy Israeli quantum-software maker Classiq, whose platform lets developers build quantum applications without hand-designing circuits across hardware types. Intel wants to pair Classiq's software with its own silicon spin-qubit hardware to become a "full-stack" quantum provider — reportedly its second-largest acquisition ever after Mobileye. — [Source](https://www.techzine.eu/news/infrastructure/145301/intel-acquires-quantum-software-company-for-2-2-billion/)

### AI-infrastructure debt jitters keep rattling credit markets
CoreWeave's five-year credit-default-swap spread blew past 1,000 basis points in late October — implying over a 60% five-year default probability by standard pricing — as investors question whether AI infrastructure spending is generating cash fast enough to service roughly $50 billion in debt. Stress has spread to peers including Oracle and SoftBank, a sign that doubts about the AI capex boom are now showing up directly in credit pricing, not just stock prices. — [Source](https://www.businessday.co.za/world/international-companies/2026-10-15-ai-credit-fears-push-tech-default-insurance-to-record-highs/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Trojanized Claude Code extension on Cursor's marketplace planted SSH backdoors
A tampered build of Anthropic's Claude Code extension (v2.1.313) was live in Cursor's marketplace for about 12 hours on October 29, reaching up to 5,000 users; it added an attacker-controlled SSH key, set up persistence via cron/scheduled tasks, and phoned home over plain HTTP. Cursor pulled the build, rotated keys, and shut the marketplace temporarily — Anthropic says its own systems weren't compromised, but uninstalling the extension alone doesn't remove the backdoor. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/official-claude-code-vs-code-extension.html)

### Amadeus breach widens: Cathay Pacific, Japan Airlines, ANA all confirm exposure
Travel-tech vendor Amadeus confirmed unauthorized access to reservation data — via a compromised system-account login — stretching from August 2023 to September 2026, affecting roughly 53,000 Cathay Pacific bookings, about 92,000 Japan Airlines Group customers, and around 37,800 ANA passengers, plus Korean Air, Asiana, and Malaysia Airlines. Exposed data mostly covers names, booking references and flight details, with no evidence payment data was accessed, but the breach now spans most of Asia's major carriers. — [Source](https://www.ibtimes.co.uk/amadeus-breach-exposes-airline-booking-data-1829581)

### Binance restores withdrawals after record $6.3B hack, sees $3.6B in net outflows
Binance resumed customer withdrawals on November 1 after the largest crypto theft on record, which the FBI attributes to North Korea; the exchange logged roughly $3.6 billion in net crypto outflows as customers pulled funds, even as Binance says it has replaced about 95% of the stolen assets and will cover all losses itself. Market-wide price impact stayed muted, suggesting confidence in Binance's backstop so far. — [Source](https://thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/11/02/binance-details-us36bil-in-net-outflows-after-cyber-theft)
