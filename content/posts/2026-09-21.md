## 🤖 AI / ML

### US military "almost started a war" with China over an AI-generated intel report
CNN reports that this spring, US forces prepared to intercept a Chinese ship believed to be carrying nuclear-weapons materials after a chatbot-assisted intelligence report concluded the cargo was a threat — a conclusion sources call "entirely false," produced when an analyst asked a chatbot to synthesize a ship's manifest and it blended open-source and classified intel into a faulty result; the operation was called off just before contact. — [CNN](https://www.cnn.com/2026/09/18/politics/us-military-ai-false-intelligence-china-ship)

### StepFun launches Step 5, a 600B-parameter open-weight model at $1 per million tokens
The Chinese lab's Step 5 Preview is a sparse mixture-of-experts model with 600B total parameters (27B active per token), a 1-million-token context window, and native image input, aimed at long-horizon coding and agent workloads; API access opened September 20, with open weights due October 15 — one of the cheapest frontier-class models on the market. — [Pandaily](https://pandaily.com/stepfun-step-5-preview-600b-moe-1m-context)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub Copilot's weekly release adds self-resolving code review and model-selection tiers
The September 14 update makes Copilot code review automatically resolve comments it judges addressed in a later push while leaving real feedback open, adds commit-message suggestions when applying its fixes, lets reviews run shell tools to validate changes, and introduces efficiency/balance/intelligence tiers for auto model selection in VS Code, the CLI, and the Copilot app. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-18-github-copilot-weekly-releases-september-14/)

### Firefox 156 ships a 45% faster PDF viewer and DevTools fixes
Mozilla's release speeds up the built-in PDF viewer by up to 45%, cuts memory and CPU use for large scaled JPEGs, adds macOS auto-start, fixes a misleading viewport-size readout in the Inspector, and patches several high-impact security flaws including use-after-free and privilege-escalation bugs. — [Mozilla](https://www.firefox.com/en-US/firefox/156.0/releasenotes/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Consumers sue Anthropic, OpenAI, SpaceXAI and Google over alleged AI-slowdown collusion
A proposed class action filed September 18 in San Francisco federal court accuses the four companies of violating the Sherman Act by agreeing to "pace the frontier" after Dario Amodei's September essay and same-day public endorsements from Sam Altman, Elon Musk, and Demis Hassabis — arguing an agreement to slow product improvement is an illegal agreement to restrict output, and seeking treble damages for subscribers. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/ai-slowdown-lawsuit-openai-anthropic-google/)

### Bloomberg: $33 trillion in stock gains now rides on AI staying on track
Almost $33 trillion has been added to the S&P 500 since late 2022, the vast majority tied to companies betting on AI, and AI-related spending accounts for roughly half of US GDP growth by some estimates — meaning the industry's own "pace the frontier" debate now doubles as a question about the broader economy's exposure to a single technology bet. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/newsletters/2026-09-21/there-s-33-trillion-in-stock-gains-riding-on-the-future-of-ai)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Google patches actively exploited Pixel modem zero-day
CVE-2026-58704, a high-severity flaw in the cellular modem used on some Pixel devices, required no user interaction and let an attacker on an adjacent network potentially take control of the device; Google says it was under limited, targeted exploitation and fixed it in the September Pixel update, which patched 110 vulnerabilities in total. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/google-fixes-actively-exploited-android-zero-day-on-pixel-devices/)

### Apache Airflow FAB provider flaw lets stolen sessions survive a password change
CVE-2026-86462 (CVSS 9.1) means changing a user's password through the Admin PATCH endpoint doesn't invalidate their existing database-backed sessions, so an attacker holding a stolen session cookie keeps access even after the victim resets their password; it's a second, independent route to a bug a prior patch (CVE-2026-82311) didn't actually close. Fixed in apache-airflow-providers-fab 3.9.0. — [Strix](https://www.strix.ai/cve/CVE-2026-86462)
