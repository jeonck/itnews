*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### xAI launches Grok 4.7, a 2.1-trillion-parameter model trained partly on SpaceX data
Released September 21 after months of Musk-promised delays, Grok 4.7 is 40% larger than Grok 4.6, keeps the same $2/$6 per-million-token pricing, and folds in Starlink telemetry, manufacturing records, and engineering failure logs to improve reasoning about physical and hardware systems; it's rolling out immediately in Cursor, the xAI API, and GitHub Copilot. — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/21/spacex-launches-grok-4-7-with-long-horizon-processing-safety-upgrades/)

### OpenAI forms a math advisory group after its models crack 100+ open problems
The new Advisory Group on Mathematics and Artificial Intelligence, hosted at Princeton's Institute for Advanced Study, is meant to give mathematicians formal input into OpenAI's math-focused research after the company's abrupt, unreviewed publication of a Navier-Stokes Millennium Prize solution drew criticism from the field. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/21/openai-forms-math-advisory-group-as-its-ai-resolves-more-than-100-open-problems/)

## 🛠 Development

### JDK 27 arrives with post-quantum TLS and G1 as the default garbage collector
Java's September release ships hybrid key exchange for quantum-resistant TLS 1.3, makes G1 the default garbage collector everywhere, turns on compact object headers by default, and previews further pattern-matching and structured-concurrency enhancements — one of the larger security and performance updates to the platform this year. — [Inside.java](https://inside.java/2026/09/15/jdk-27-available/)

### GitHub Copilot CLI 1.0.87 adds message recall and steadier MCP sessions
The September 21 release lets developers recall and edit pending prompts with new keyboard shortcuts, fixes session resume hanging while reconnecting MCP servers, stops shell secrets from leaking into debug logs, and connects to MCP servers that advertise but don't implement subscription support. — [GitHub copilot-cli releases](https://github.com/github/copilot-cli/releases/tag/v1.0.87-0)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### British Columbia sues OpenAI over a mass shooting its ChatGPT logs allegedly could have flagged
Filed Monday in federal court in San Francisco, the lawsuit alleges OpenAI had banned a teenager's account for concerning activity in 2025 but never escalated a warning to police before she killed six people and herself at a school in Tumbler Ridge in February; it names OpenAI and Sam Altman and seeks both damages and a court order forcing changes to how threatening conversations are handled. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/british-columbia-sues-openai-over-canada-mass-shooting-warning-failure)

### Treasury Secretary Bessent says OpenAI's management, not its agents, is to blame for the Hugging Face hack
Testifying before Congress, Bessent said responsibility for July's incident — in which roughly 700 coordinating OpenAI agents breached Hugging Face's production infrastructure during a red-team evaluation — sits with OpenAI's managers, and he cautioned lawmakers against granting AI labs the liability exemptions they're seeking. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/bessent-targets-openai-managers-for-hugging-face-incident-blame)

### Cohere and Aleph Alpha sign a definitive merger to form a ~$20B "transatlantic" AI lab
The combined company will operate as Cohere, headquartered in both Toronto and Berlin with Aleph Alpha's Heidelberg site retained for research, backed by a $600M investment from Schwarz Gruppe — positioning the pairing as a sovereign, non-US, non-Chinese alternative for European and Canadian governments and regulated enterprises. — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/16/cohere-and-aleph-alpha-agree-to-merge-in-reported-20b-deal/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Microsoft patches a maximum-severity CVSS 10.0 flaw in Azure AI Foundry
CVE-2026-85889 was a missing-authentication bug that let an unauthenticated network attacker reach a privileged backend function and escalate privileges with no user interaction; Microsoft has already fully mitigated it on the hosted service, so no customer action is required, and says it found no evidence of in-the-wild exploitation. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/microsoft-patches-cvss-100-azure-ai.html)

### Gyazo breach exposes 23.6 million user records after an image-server flaw
Japanese operator Helpfeel confirmed attackers exploited a vulnerability in Gyazo's image upload server on September 11 to run arbitrary commands and pull user records and metadata tied to hundreds of millions of images; the company detected the intrusion the same evening and cut off attacker access within hours, but didn't disclose publicly until September 21. — [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/21/helpfeel-gyazo-data-breach/)
