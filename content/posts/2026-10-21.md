*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI says GPT-6.1 Sol is dramatically better at migrating legacy code
OpenAI reports GPT-6.1 Sol hit 86.6% accuracy translating COBOL to languages like Java, Python, and Rust without changing behavior — versus 5.4% for GPT-5.6 Sol at the same low reasoning setting, and up to 97.9% at higher effort. COBOL and Fortran still run banking, ATM, and nuclear-safety systems, so a real jump in automated migration quality could meaningfully cut the cost of modernizing decades-old infrastructure. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/openai-says-gpt-61-sol-is-dramatically-better-at-migrating-legacy-code/)

### Anthropic's draft AI safety bill would let a judge block risky model releases
Politico obtained details of Anthropic's proposed federal bill: if independent, Commerce-registered evaluators flag a "foreseeable, imminent risk" of 50+ deaths or $1B+ in damage from bioweapons, loss of control, or cyberattack, they'd have 72 hours to notify Commerce, which could ask a D.C. judge to halt release. It's a notably more interventionist ask than most labs' preferred voluntary-oversight model, and critics note the scenarios it targets are narrower than the AI-hacking and agent-misbehavior incidents dominating current industry concern. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/anthropic-pitches-ai-safety-bill-that-may-block-a-model-if-50-people-could-be-killed/)

## 🛠 Development

### Google open-sources TorchTPU, letting PyTorch run on TPUs with no rewrites
Google released TorchTPU, letting existing PyTorch apps run on its TPUs and Google Cloud via "a simple configuration change," plus a TPU agent suite that helps migrate code and fix errors. Since PyTorch defaults to Nvidia GPUs today, this — alongside Amazon's parallel TorchNeuron push for Trainium — is a direct, coordinated attempt by cloud rivals to loosen Nvidia's grip on the AI developer stack. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/google-has-joined-amazon-in-attacking-nvidias-stronghold-with-ai-app-developers/)

### Figma expands MCP support while its co-founder calls the protocol imperfect
At Figma's first Config conference in India, the company added 20 new Model Context Protocol partners (including Amp, Linear, Notion, and Mistral) even as co-founder Dylan Field publicly argued MCP still has room to improve and currently benefits frontier AI labs more than other players — pointedly criticizing Anthropic for not exposing its own cloud resources through the protocol it created. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/figma-co-founder-dylan-field-thinks-mcp-is-not-a-perfect-protocol/)

### Backdoored OVHcloud CLI releases stole developer secrets from disk
Attackers who'd controlled a developer's GitHub account for six-plus months pushed trojanized releases (v0.11.0–v0.15.0) of OVHcloud's open-source CLI after disabling GitHub's release-immutability protection, planting malware built to harvest secrets from any machine that ran it. Anyone who installed one of the affected builds on macOS, Linux, or Windows should treat every local secret as compromised and rotate it. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/ovhcloud-cli-releases-backdoored-on-github-to-steal-developer-secrets/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### SpaceX and Tesla boards form special committees to weigh a merger
Both companies' boards created independent-director committees to evaluate a possible combination, which Elon Musk — the largest individual shareholder in both — says shareholders requested; he's recused from the talks. Musk has pitched the merger as enabling shared work on AI, robotics, the Starmind orbital data-center project, and the Terafab chip venture, but Tesla's broader shareholder base makes approval there far harder than at SpaceX, where Musk already controls over 50% of the vote. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/spacex-and-tesla-boards-form-special-committees-to-review-merger/)

### Meta's Muse AI companion hits 10M US downloads in 40 days
Muse, Meta's AI agent app launched in September, reached the milestone faster than all but 11 apps ever have, aided by heavy cross-app promotion and ad spend; it's also out-retaining rivals (30% seven-day US retention vs. a ~19% competitor average) and outpacing Claude, Grok, and ChatGPT's first-40-day US download counts. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/metas-muse-hits-10m-u-s-downloads-in-40-days-a-milestone-reached-by-only-11-other-apps/)

### Bot-defense startup Blackwall quadruples valuation to $2.2B
The Estonia-based company, which sells DDoS protection, threat intelligence, and bot/AI-agent filtering to hosting and cloud providers under their own branding, raised a $100M Series C led by AVP. Its channel-partner model sidesteps direct competition with Cloudflare and Akamai, and it's betting that rising AI-agent web traffic will make that filtering business even more valuable. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/20/blackwall-quadruples-valuation-to-2-2b-as-websites-wrangle-an-increasingly-crowded-internet/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Emergency PHP patch coming today for two critical unpatched RCE flaws
PHP rates a pair of flaws researchers are calling "php2shell" at 9.2 on CVSS v4.0 and plans an out-of-cycle release at 14:00 UTC today; only the 8.2–8.5 branches will get fixes, leaving end-of-life versions (8.1 and older) permanently exposed. Shodan shows roughly 884,000 internet-facing hosts still running PHP 8.1 or older, so the window between disclosure and mass patching is the real risk here. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/php2shell-two-unpatched-critical-php.html)

### Eight compromised eMudhra CA keys force emergency blocks across every major browser
Mozilla and Google confirmed eight eMudhra intermediate CA certificates — six for TLS, two for code signing — carry weak keys traceable to a flawed 2025 key-generation ceremony, letting anyone who derives them forge trusted certificates for any website. Chrome, Firefox, Apple, and Microsoft all blocked the certificates within hours, Go and NSS shipped fixes, and eMudhra's stock fell about 20% in Mumbai as Chrome reviews the CA's other roots. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/eight-emudhra-ca-keys-compromised.html)

### CISA and FBI: SafePay ransomware has hit 600+ critical infrastructure orgs
A joint advisory with German, US, and UK agencies says SafePay — which operates without affiliates and only leaks data from non-paying victims — has struck at least 600 organizations across the Americas, Europe, and Australia since August 2024 via credential stuffing, brute force, VPN abuse (including Mullvad-linked IPs), and deletion of shadow copies and NAS backups to block recovery. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-and-fbi-safepay-ransomware-has-hit-over-600-critical-infrastructure-organizations/)

### DHS orders DeepSeek removed from all federal civilian systems
The order, published in the Federal Register, bars federal civilian agencies from procuring or running DeepSeek products — including affiliates and third-party tools built on its technology — citing risk of Chinese government access to government data and a 2025 security report that found weak encryption and data sharing in DeepSeek's iOS app. It's not a ban on private or consumer use, and agencies have 60 days to request exceptions. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/dhs-bans-deepseek-products-across-us-federal-civilian-agencies/)
