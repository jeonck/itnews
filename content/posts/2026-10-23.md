*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI's decision-model API goes generally available
OpenAI's Decisions API — a fast, cheap classifier repurposed from its Luna model, now answering in roughly 100-180ms instead of 1-3 seconds — is now open to all developers, directly challenging TypeSafe's Jev models in the emerging "decision model" category. Early testers like Speak cut action latency from 1.4 seconds to 0.13 seconds. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/openai-makes-its-jev-competitor-widely-available-as-decision-model-competition-heats-up/)

### Anthropic launches Claude Duet, its bet on proactive agents
The new iOS/Android/web app, built on Claude Opus 5.5, builds daily briefings and dashboards and can complete background tasks on its own virtual machine, only pausing for approval on sensitive steps like payments. It enters a crowded field already including Meta Muse, OpenAI Dots, and Grok Bot. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/claude-duet-app-is-anthropics-bet-on-agents/)

### Canva ships its first in-house image generation model
Canva Craft was trained on Canva's own design templates rather than photographs, generates up to 2K images in 30 aspect ratios, and plugs into Canva Magic for layered, editable output — a move to cut reliance on third-party image APIs as Canva's growth forecast cools. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/canva-releases-its-first-image-generation-model/)

## 🛠 Development

### The Browser Company is shutting down Arc, pushing users to AI-first Dia
Arc stops working on Mac and Windows on December 22, with accounts deactivated and data deleted within 30 days; the company says the "constant, dedicated work" of keeping a browser secure "is only increasing in the age of AI." Passwords, bookmarks, and extensions carry over to Dia, but Arc won't be open-sourced since it can't be separated from Dia's proprietary SDK. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/arc-browser-sunsets-december-22-as-the-browser-company-doubles-down-on-dia/)

### 1Password lets AI agents log in without ever seeing your password
1Password for Agents verifies an agent's identity via a digital key, prompts the user to approve or deny the login, then fills credentials itself — the agent never touches the raw password. Claude Duet, OpenAI's Dots and ChatGPT Work, Grok Bot, and Meta Muse are supported at launch, as banks report rising, hard-to-distinguish agentic activity on customer accounts. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/1password-launches-a-way-for-ai-agents-to-log-in-on-your-behalf-without-seeing-your-password/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### OpenAI reportedly in talks to raise up to $30B at a $1.25T valuation
Led by Coatue and UAE investors, a round at this size — possibly paired with a compute-capacity deal — would make OpenAI the most valuable private company ever, and raises the bar for its eventual IPO after a $852B valuation just in March. Rival Anthropic is reportedly seeking $1.5-2T for its own public debut. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/openai-working-on-new-30b-raise-at-1-25t-valuation-ahead-of-ipo/)

### DeepSeek reportedly raises ~$15B days after US agencies were told to drop it
The Hangzhou startup's round, backed by CATL and Tencent among others, lands just two days after the US ordered federal civilian agencies to purge DeepSeek's AI technology over security concerns — underscoring how much capital is still chasing Chinese AI labs despite Western restrictions. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/deepseek-reportedly-raises-15b-days-after-us-restrictions/)

### Tesla starts Optimus production as profit falls 43%
Q3 net income fell to $787M from $1.37B a year earlier as EV sales stayed flat and spending ramped on Optimus, AI, and autonomy; Musk calls the Fremont robot line "early production" with a bumpy ramp toward a goal of 20,000 units/week by end of 2027. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/21/tesla-stars-optimus-robot-production-in-california-as-profit-falls-43/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### China-linked hackers used AI agents to breach ConnectWise, hit ~2,250 companies
Attackers stole ConnectWise cloud-backup credentials linking the IT management vendor to customers' Microsoft-hosted environments, then accessed those customers' email and files. Microsoft attributes the attack to a China state-aligned group and says it used the open-source ARTEX AI framework to attempt roughly 3,000 sign-ins per minute — the same tool reportedly used in a recent attack on South Korean financial systems. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/22/chinese-hackers-spied-on-thousands-of-companies-after-breaching-it-giant-connectwise/)

### "PHP2Shell" critical RCE flaw sees millions of exploit attempts within a day
CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 (CVSS 9.2) allow unauthenticated code execution via crafted HTTP requests, with vulnerable code dating back to PHP 5.4 (2012). Cloudflare logged millions of exploit attempts within hours of PHP's October 21 advisory, mostly scanning, though attackers have tried deploying a Mirai-derived botnet. Patches are in PHP 8.5.12, 8.4.27, 8.3.36, and 8.2.35. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/php2shell-exploit-attempts-hit-millions.html)

### Sandworm used a novel backdoor hidden in the Yggdrasil mesh network against Ukraine
ESET found Russia's Sandworm group collaborating with UAC-0099 to deploy "MythicRatatoskr," a previously undocumented backdoor whose command-and-control server runs inside the Yggdrasil mesh network — the first documented state-actor abuse of that network. The intrusion, which began with a July 2025 spear-phishing email at a Ukrainian logistics firm, ended two months later in a data-wiping attack. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/russian-linked-hackers-used-new-mesh.html)

### Critical flaw in Python's Pillow library could let attackers run server commands
CVE-2026-112900 lets an attacker who controls just a file*name* (not its contents) trigger command execution when Pillow hands an EPS image to Ghostscript — a realistic risk for any upload endpoint built from Pillow, which is downloaded roughly 436 million times a month. Pillow 13.0.0, released October 16, fixes this along with three other flaws across JPEG, TIFF, and thumbnail handling. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-flaw-in-pythons-popular-pillow.html)
