*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2
The upgraded frontier model beats Opus 5.5 on nearly every evaluated benchmark and runs up to 30% faster with 55% fewer unnecessary safety blocks; in one demonstration, a 1,000-agent team fully formalized the proof of Fermat's Last Theorem in Lean in 30 hours. Fable is open to all users at $8/$40 per million input/output tokens; Mythos, with stronger cyber and bio capabilities, is limited to vetted organizations. — [Source](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-fable-and-mythos-5-2)

### Meta launches Muse Ember, a cut-rate model for agent grunt work
The small model is built for repetitive agentic tasks — tool calls, routing, extraction, long-document parsing — and Meta says it scores 37.8% on AutomationBench-AA versus 13.9% for Claude Haiku 5.5, at a fraction of the cost. — [Source](https://research.meta.ai/blog/muse-ember)

### Meituan open-sources LongCat-2.5, a 1.6T-parameter multimodal model
The mixture-of-experts model handles image and video understanding alongside coding and agentic repo work, and ships on Hugging Face under the permissive MIT license. — [Source](https://venturebeat.com/technology/meituan-releases-the-open-weights-of-its-longcat-2-5-llm-with-vision-under-a-permissive-mit-license)

## 🛠 Development

### Mitchell Hashimoto ships libghostty-vt 1.0, a stable embeddable terminal engine
Extracted from the Ghostty terminal app, the MIT-licensed C library gives IDEs, editors, and coding agents a shared, tested terminal-emulation engine instead of each reimplementing it; JetBrains IDEs and Neovim are already adopting it. — [Source](https://runtimewire.com/article/libghostty-1-0-mitchell-hashimoto-stable-c-abi)

### Cognition launches SWE-2 Flash, a cheaper coding model for Devin
Built on Thinking Machines' open-weight Inkling-Small with added mid-training and RL, it runs at roughly a fifth of the cost and three times the speed of Cognition's flagship SWE-2 model, and is free in Devin through November 30. — [Source](https://cognition.com/blog/swe-2-flash)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### SoftBank posts $7B quarterly loss on AI data-center costs
The loss stems largely from changed procurement plans for US AI data-center power equipment and a decline in Intel's share price; SoftBank's OpenAI stake nonetheless shows a $45B gain, with any bump from OpenAI's reported new valuation still to come next quarter. — [Source](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/11/10/softbank-2q-earnings-tech-selloff.html)

### China blocks SoftBank's $5.4B acquisition of ABB's robotics division
China's foreign-investment security review body prohibited the deal, which would have given SoftBank control of a unit that makes about 90% of its robots in China; both companies say they're still working the deal and talking with Chinese authorities. — [Source](https://www.robotics247.com/article/softbanks-proposed-5.4b-acquisition-of-abb-robotics-blocked-by-china)

### Moonshot AI reportedly rented restricted Nvidia chips through China-based servers
Bloomberg reports documents showing a contract that may have run advanced Nvidia chips through servers tied to mainland China, potentially breaching US export controls; Moonshot disputes the documents' characterization. — [Source](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-11-10/moonshot-rents-nvidia-s-top-ai-chips-in-china-documents-show)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Fireworks AI confirms a second breach in weeks
An October 24 intrusion through a cloud provider reached development machines, exposing billing data and, for some accounts, secrets, customer datasets, and incomplete model weights; the AI infrastructure company notified customers on November 9. — [Source](https://runtimewire.com/article/fireworks-ai-second-security-breach-customer-data)

### DOJ indicts three in $129M AI deepfake CEO-impersonation fraud scheme
Prosecutors say the group used AI to clone the voices and likenesses of CEOs and lawyers at major law firms to trick accounting staff into wiring money, targeting thousands of companies worldwide from July 2024 to February 2026. — [Source](https://cyberscoop.com/ai-deepfakes-impersonate-ceo-lawyer-defraud-companies/)

### Australia to create an AI safety regulator after an OpenAI agent breach
Prime Minister Anthony Albanese announced a new regulator empowered to levy "significant penalties" on AI companies and require firms to disclose incidents when models "go rogue," following reports that an OpenAI agent accessed a Medicare statistics portal. — [Source](https://asia.nikkei.com/business/technology/artificial-intelligence/australia-plans-new-regulator-for-artificial-intelligence-after-openai-hack)
