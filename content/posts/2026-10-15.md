*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Meta ships Muse Wonder, its first frontier-class model
Meta Superintelligence Labs released Muse Wonder and a subscriber-only Muse Wonder Full, trained at its new Prometheus data center in Ohio and built around "Active Memory" and "Active Focus" features aimed at longer-running agentic tasks. It's Meta's first frontier model since Alexandr Wang's AI reorganization, and it lands just behind Claude Opus 5.5 on third-party benchmarks. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/12/meta-releases-its-first-frontier-model-muse-wonder)

### Google widens access to Gemini 4 Argon
After a limited rollout to vetted cyber-defense partners, Google opened Gemini 4 Argon to Gemini app AI Pro and Ultra subscribers and to developers on its paid API tier, with AI Studio and the Antigravity coding tool reserved for Ultra. Introductory API pricing of $2/$10 per million input/output tokens holds through November 30. — [Google](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/products/gemini-app/gemini-4-availability)

## 🛠 Development

### Cloudflare acquires Deno, winds down its standalone runtime
Cloudflare bought Deno in an undisclosed deal, with the whole team — including creator Ryan Dahl — joining to fold Deno's "celld" project into Workers' open-source runtime, workerd. The standalone Deno runtime gets a year of fixes before development ends, and Deno Deploy shuts down within six months with paid customers moved to Cloudflare Workers; the JSR package registry continues under Cloudflare. — [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/cloudflare-acquires-deno-ryan-dahl/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Arena nearly doubles valuation to $3.1B in new funding round
The AI evaluation platform formerly known as LMArena raised a $200 million Series B co-led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and Khosla Ventures, up from a $1.7 billion valuation just ten months ago. The company says it has crossed $100 million in annualized revenue as demand grows for independent model benchmarking. — [Angel Investors Network](https://angelinvestorsnetwork.com/venture-capital/arena-raises-200m-in-series-b-funding-at-3-1b-valuation)

### Anthropic opens its fifth Asia-Pacific office, in Singapore
Anthropic opened a Singapore office — joining Tokyo, Bengaluru, Seoul and Sydney — and named Dale Finlay as general manager for ASEAN. The company says Singapore ranks second globally for Claude usage relative to population and expects strong demand from regulated sectors including government and financial services. — [DealStreetAsia](https://www.dealstreetasia.com/stories/anthropic-to-open-singapore-office-as-demand-for-claude-grows-495410/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Attacker chains three Ivanti Connect Secure zero-days for root access
CVE-2026-104935, an authentication-bypass flaw in Ivanti Connect Secure, is being actively exploited by a group tracked as UTA0576, which combined it with two other unpatched bugs to run commands as root on fully patched VPN appliances without logging in. CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on October 13; a fix for the third chained flaw is still pending. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/attacker-chains-three-ivanti-connect.html)

### Microsoft's October Patch Tuesday fixes a record 1,411 flaws
The haul includes three zero-days — one, CVE-2026-96951, a Windows Shell security-feature bypass already under active exploitation, plus two more that were publicly disclosed before patching. Admins are being urged to prioritize the exploited flaw given the unusually large batch size. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/microsoft/microsoft-october-2026-patch-tuesday-fixes-1-411-flaws-3-zero-days/)
