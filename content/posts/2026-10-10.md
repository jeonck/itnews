*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google launches a universal Gemini agent for work
At its Gemini at Work 2026 event, Google Cloud unveiled "Gemini agent," a single agent meant to handle knowledge work, Q&A, content creation, and coding from one prompt box — it plans the task, picks tools and models, connects to a company's business systems, and returns a finished result. It's rolling out in private preview across Workspace, Slack, and Microsoft 365. — [Google Cloud Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/google-cloud/gemini-at-work/)

### Anthropic ships Claude Haiku 5.5, its fastest and cheapest model yet
Haiku 5.5 replaces Haiku 4.5 as the bottom tier of Anthropic's lineup, built for high-volume tasks like summarization, classification, and subagent work under Opus 5.5 or Sonnet 5.5; Anthropic says it runs roughly 75% cheaper on average while posting large gains on agentic benchmarks like OSWorld and Terminal-Bench. — [Let's Data Science](https://letsdatascience.com/news/anthropic-releases-claude-haiku-55-for-high-volume-tasks-1c6260b4)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### AI chip startup Etched fields offers at a $40–50B valuation
Just weeks after raising $700M at a $21B valuation in September, Etched is reportedly reviewing fresh funding offers that would value the transformer-specialized chipmaker at $40 billion to $50 billion — a sign investors still see room to compete with Nvidia on inference hardware. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/05/etched-fields-funding-offers-at-40b-valuation-sources-say/)

### AI agent startup Instinct closes $1B round at a $10B valuation
Instinct, founded in 2025 by ex-Sierra engineer Noah Shinn to build an autonomous personal AI agent, quadrupled its valuation to $10 billion in a Series C led by Sequoia, Benchmark, and Coatue — one of the largest checks yet for a consumer agentic-AI bet. — [OODA Loop](https://oodaloop.com/briefs/technology/ai-agent-startup-instinct-secures-1-billion-at-10-billion-valuation/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Attackers hijack three ccTLD registries to forge Google HTTPS certificates
Google disclosed that attackers compromised the .gh (Ghana), .sl (Sierra Leone), and .as (American Samoa) country-code domain registries, altering DNS records to pass domain-control checks and obtain valid TLS certificates impersonating Google and YouTube domains. Chrome has blocked the fraudulent certificates via CRLSet, but Google warns non-Chrome browsers may stay exposed longer since it can't control other vendors' revocation timing. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-hijack-google-domains-after-breaching-cctld-registries/)

### Critical AhsayCBS backup-server flaws under active exploitation
Huntress reports attackers have been exploiting an authentication-bypass bug (CVE-2026-105133) chained with a critical command-injection flaw (CVE-2026-105134) in AhsayCBS backup software since October 7, hitting at least five organizations with webshells and XMRig cryptominers disguised as Microsoft Edge. Versions up to 10.3.2 are affected; Ahsay's fix is in 10.3.4. — [Huntress](https://www.huntress.com/blog/ahsaycbs-flaws-exploit)
