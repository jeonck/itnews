*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic commits 5% of its equity to a new "public benefit fund"
Anthropic will put 5% of its shares into a wholly-owned fund aimed at societal benefit, with the ability to add 2% more for every 50% rise in the company's valuation; it says governance details will follow in the coming months. The move lands as Anthropic reportedly prepares for an eventual IPO, giving the pledge an unusually large built-in price tag. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/18/anthropic-says-its-committing-5-of-its-shares-to-a-public-benefit-fund)

### Mistral launches Large 4 preview, a 1-trillion-parameter MoE model
Mistral opened a public preview of Large 4, a mixture-of-experts model with roughly 1.05T total and ~49-52B active parameters and up to a 1M-token context window, which it claims is the best open-weights model out of the US or Europe on aggregated benchmarks. Open weights are promised for October 27; independent benchmarking (Vals Index) so far ranks it well behind that claim, at 32nd of 44 models tested. — [BetaNews](https://betanews.com/article/mistral-large-4-preview-open-weights/)

## 🛠 Development

### Python 3.15 ships with an experimental JIT and UTF-8 by default
Python 3.15 landed with a still-experimental JIT compiler that's roughly 7-8% faster than the standard interpreter on Linux x86-64, plus UTF-8 as the default encoding, explicit lazy imports, a new `frozendict` built-in, and frame pointers enabled by default for easier system-level profiling. — [Phoronix](https://www.phoronix.com/news/Python-3.15-Released)

### Cloudflare acquires Deno; standalone runtime winds down within a year
The entire Deno team, including creator Ryan Dahl, is joining Cloudflare to merge Deno's self-hostable `celld` Workers runtime into Cloudflare's open-source `workerd`. Deno the standalone runtime gets only bug and security fixes for one more year before development stops, and Deno Deploy shuts down within six months, with paying customers migrated to Cloudflare Workers. — [IT Daily](https://itdaily.com/news/business/cloudflare-deno/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Netflix cuts ~1,000 jobs in its largest-ever layoff after losing Warner Bros. Discovery
Co-CEOs Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters told staff the company is cutting roughly 6% of its workforce, concentrated in tech, product, legal, and HR, as subscriber growth cools and YouTube eats into streaming watch time. The cuts follow Netflix's February exit from the bidding war for Warner Bros. Discovery, which went to Paramount Skydance, and a roughly 42% slide in Netflix's stock over the past year. — [DNYUZ](https://dnyuz.com/2026/10/15/after-nearly-buying-warner-bros-netflix-is-laying-off-1000-workers-what-happened/)

### Bending Spoons lays off a large share of Airtable's pre-acquisition staff
Weeks after closing its roughly $2.25B purchase of Airtable, Bending Spoons began cutting a large portion of the no-code platform's team, saying the move reflects a belief that "small teams ship more and better" rather than any reflection on affected employees' work; departing staff get separation packages and leave by week's end. — [DNYUZ](https://dnyuz.com/2026/10/12/airtables-new-owner-is-laying-off-a-large-part-of-its-team/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### GitLab patches maximum-severity, unauthenticated RCE in its CI/CD regex compiler
CVE-2026-97229 (CVSS 10.0) is an integer overflow in GitLab's CI/CD Regular Expression Compiler that lets an attacker run arbitrary code via a crafted regex in a CI/CD config file, with no authentication or user interaction required. GitLab fixed it October 12 in 19.2.8, 19.3.5, and 19.4.2; Rapid7 reproduced the exploit against an unpatched instance, though no in-the-wild attacks have been confirmed yet. — [Help Net Security](https://helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/13/gitlab-ci-cd-regular-expression-compiler-vulnerability-cve-2026-97229)

### Attackers exploit Collabora flaw to plant web shells on Nextcloud servers
CVE-2026-110447 is a command-injection bug in Collabora's built-in CODE server app for Nextcloud, exploitable when the app's local document server briefly isn't running (e.g., during a restart). Collabora fixed it in version 26.4.303, but admins who patch late should also check for planted web shells, since patching alone won't remove malware already dropped on the server. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-exploit-collabora-rce-flaw-to-backdoor-nextcloud-servers/)
