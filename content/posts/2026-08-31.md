*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### DeepSeek nears $7.4B funding round at a $74B valuation
The Chinese AI lab is closing its first outside-investor round by the end of August, more than doubling the valuation of its June raise, as it builds toward a possible 2027 IPO on Shanghai's STAR Market. — [China Money Network](https://www.chinamoneynetwork.com/2026/08/29/deepseek-nears-7-4-billion-funding-round-at-74-billion-valuation-ahead-of-2027-ipo)

### AI chatbots resisted foreign propaganda better than search engines, NPR/NewsGuard test finds
Testing ChatGPT, Gemini, Copilot, Meta AI, Grok, and Claude against false narratives pushed by China, Iran, and Russia, researchers found the chatbots mostly debunked the claims — outperforming AI summaries placed above traditional search results. — [NPR](https://www.npr.org/2026/08/30/nx-s1-5876436/chatbots-search-propaganda)

## 🛠 Development

### Kubernetes v1.37 "Garhwal" ships with metrics API graduating to stable
After nearly nine years in beta, the metrics.k8s.io API — which powers `kubectl top` and resource-based autoscaling — is now stable, alongside the StorageVersionMigration API graduating and enabling by default. — [Kubernetes Blog](https://kubernetes.io/blog/2026/08/26/kubernetes-v1-37-release/)

### VS Code 1.135 adds cross-app agent sessions and a "Rubber Duck" second opinion
The release lets developers continue a Copilot or Claude agent session started in another app, and adds an experimental feature that asks a second model to review agent work for missed details and edge cases. — [VS Code Updates](https://code.visualstudio.com/updates/v1_135)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Nvidia pauses AI cloud financing program over antitrust concerns
Days after a blowout earnings report with $96.2B in quarterly revenue, Nvidia pulled back parts of its two-month-old AI Compute Partnership — which offered smaller cloud providers financing in exchange for a cut of revenue — after employees flagged fears the arrangement could draw regulatory scrutiny. — [Yahoo Finance / WSJ](https://finance.yahoo.com/news/nvidia-pauses-revenue-sharing-deals-223140237.html)

### Andreessen Horowitz launches $1.1B "Machine Age" fund for AI hardware
The firm's first vehicle dedicated to physical AI infrastructure will back startups building chips, data centers, power, networking, and robotics, marking a formal expansion beyond its software-investing roots. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/28/a16z-creates-a-1-1b-machine-age-fund-to-accelerate-the-physical-buildout-of-ai/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Anthropic warns infostealer malware is hijacking Claude sessions to drain usage
Malware families including Vidar, LummaC2, StealC, and RedLine are stealing authenticated Claude session cookies from infected PCs, letting attackers replay logins and burn through paid usage without a password; Anthropic is signing out affected users, removing saved payment methods, and refunding unauthorized charges. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/artificial-intelligence/anthropic-warns-infostealer-malware-is-hijacking-claude-sessions-to-drain-usage/)

### McKesson discloses breach after ShinyHunters claims theft of 284M patient records
The healthcare distribution giant confirmed unauthorized access after the extortion group said it used vished Okta credentials to reach McKesson's Salesforce and Snowflake environments, exfiltrating roughly 1TB of data including SSNs and medical records before demanding a $55M ransom. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/mckesson-discloses-breach-after-shinyhunters-claims-patient-data-theft/)

### PaperCut ships a second emergency patch after researchers bypass the first fix
Researchers at watchTowr and Huntress found ways around PaperCut's initial fix for two chainable, actively exploited flaws (CVE-2026-81578, CVE-2026-82078) in its NG/MF print management software, prompting a second round of patches covering versions 24 through 26. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/papercut-releases-second-emergency-patch-for-exploited-flaws/)
