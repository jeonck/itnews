## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic quietly pitches Congress its own federal AI safety bill
Politico reported Friday that Anthropic representatives have been privately pitching Capitol Hill lawmakers on a draft bill to regulate frontier-model safety and security, even as the company publicly backs state-level rules like California's SB 53. The move shows Anthropic trying to shape federal AI legislation directly rather than just reacting to competing proposals from Senate and House offices. — [The Daily Guardian](https://thedailyguardian.com/business/anthropic-quietly-pitches-its-own-ai-safety-bill-to-lawmakers-politico-reports-2-766716/)

### Replit's CEO says he's dropped Anthropic for OpenAI — insiders disagree
At TechCrunch Disrupt, Replit CEO Amjad Masad said the company stopped using Anthropic's models, citing cost and its own benchmarks showing OpenAI's Sol and Astra now do better on vibe-coded apps. A person familiar with the partnership disputed the claim, and Replit has made no public announcement ending the Anthropic relationship, underscoring how contested "who's winning" claims are in the coding-agent market. — [Crypto Briefing](https://cryptobriefing.com/replit-masad-anthropic-multi-model-disrupt/)

## 🛠 Development

### DeepSeek opens Search and Fetch APIs to outside developers
DeepSeek quietly added standalone Search and Fetch APIs to its pricing page, billed per-request at $0.75 per 1,000 successful calls rather than by tokens, drawing from the same account balance as its model usage. Queries from Claude Code's and Codex's built-in web-search tools get a two-week fee waiver, suggesting DeepSeek is courting coding-agent traffic specifically. — [SessionWatcher](https://sessionwatcher.com/news/deepseek-search-fetch-api-pricing-claude-code-codex-web-search)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Stripe agrees to buy AI model router OpenRouter for ~$7.5B
Stripe's largest acquisition to date — reportedly $7.5B, split roughly $1.5B to founders and $6B to investors — comes less than three months after OpenRouter raised $113M at a $1.3B valuation in an Alphabet-led round. OpenRouter gives developers one gateway to 400+ models from 80+ providers; Stripe is framing the deal around AI billing and usage-based payments infrastructure rather than the routing tech itself. — [The Stack](https://www.thestack.technology/stripe-acquires-openrouter-setting-up-a-battle-for-ai-routing-business/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Russia's APT28 breaches a NATO-country naval shipbuilder
Symantec says the group (tracked as Swallowtail/BlueDelta/Fancy Bear) hit a naval shipbuilder, a law enforcement agency, and a military-engine maker across two NATO member states, using its HOOKEDGE backdoor plus a new reflective loader that decrypts and runs payloads entirely in memory. Attackers reportedly returned to the shipbuilder's network repeatedly over about a week, stealing credentials and tunneling in via SSH. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/russian-apt28-breaches-nato-naval.html)

### Oracle's October Critical Patch Update lands with 1,283 fixes
Oracle's quarterly Critical Patch Update, due out today, carries pre-release warnings of 13 new Database patches (up to CVSS 8.8, some remotely exploitable without authentication) and 2 Oracle APEX patches scoring as high as 9.8. As always, Oracle urges customers to apply the update immediately rather than wait, since pre-release details can shift before the final advisory posts. — [Oracle](https://www.oracle.com/security-alerts/cpuoct2026.html)

### Nikkei discloses breach of 1.83M records via a compromised dev cloud service
Japan's Nikkei said an attacker used credentials obtained through an unidentified method to access a cloud service used for internal system development on October 7, exposing roughly 1.83 million records of names, addresses, and email addresses — no credit card data was included. It's the latest in a string of breaches at the publisher this year, following separate 2025 and 2026 compromises of employee Slack, Microsoft 365, and Google Workspace accounts. — [Nippon.com (Jiji Press)](https://www.nippon.com/en/news/yjj2026101600745/)
