*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google rolls out Gemini 4 Argon, its new frontier model, to trusted cyber defenders
Google DeepMind's Gemini 4 Argon beats GPT-6 Astra and Claude Opus 5.5 on 12 of 18 published benchmarks and supports up to 1M output tokens for long, multi-step reasoning, but initial access is restricted to select cybersecurity partners and US government testers via Google's Fairwind Program. — [9to5Google](https://9to5google.com/2026/09/30/gemini-4-argon-announcement/)

### Ai2 open-sources AstaBrief 8B, a model built to write cited research reports
The Allen Institute for AI released an 8B-parameter, Apache 2.0-licensed model that turns a research question and retrieved literature into a cited report in one pass, running roughly 3.5x faster than its Claude-powered "Thinking" pipeline while preserving citation quality. — [Hermes AI](https://hermes-ai.net/news/ai2-s-astabrief-8b-writes-cited-research-reports-3-5x-faster-in-one-pass/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Meta reportedly plans to cut its metaverse budget by up to 30%
Bloomberg reports Zuckerberg directed Reality Labs to find savings far deeper than the 10% trim asked of other divisions, with cuts targeting Horizon Worlds and Quest VR that could trigger layoffs as soon as January, as Meta redirects spending toward its AI buildout. — [UploadVR](https://www.uploadvr.com/meta-reality-labs-reportedly-facing-up-to-30-budget-cut/)

### Valon raises $150M at a $2.3B valuation to push AI agents into mortgage servicing
The round, backed by a16z and others, underscores continued investor appetite for applying AI agents to back-office financial workflows even as broader "AI bubble" concerns persist elsewhere in the market. — [TechStartups](https://techstartups.com/2026/10/06/startup-funding-news-today-october-6-2026-reactor-vinci-spiko-hadrian-whitelab-genomics-more/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Anthropic merges Project Glasswing into an expanded Cyber Verification Program
Launched October 6, the program unifies prior cyber-access efforts into three tiers — Defense Access, Red Team Access, and Specialized Access for safety-critical systems like flight OS and power grids — giving vetted security teams reduced-safeguard access to Opus 5.5, Sonnet 5.5, and Mythos 5.1; Anthropic says partners have already surfaced over 129,000 verified vulnerabilities since April. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/cyber-verification-program)

### Cisco patches a fifth SD-WAN zero-day exploited in the wild this year
CVE-2026-76504, an API authentication bypass in Cisco Catalyst SD-WAN Manager, was exploited before a patch was available, marking the fifth time in 2026 that attackers have hit Cisco's SD-WAN management stack with a zero-day. — [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/01/new-cisco-sd-wan-zero-day-exploited-in-the-wild-cve-2026-76504/)

### Critical FortiMail zero-day under active exploitation
CVE-2026-104286 lets unauthenticated attackers write arbitrary files on FortiMail systems via crafted HTTP/HTTPS requests; Fortinet has shipped fixes and it is already being exploited in the wild, so admins should patch immediately. — [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/10/02/fortinet-fortimail-vulnerability-cve-2026-104286/)
