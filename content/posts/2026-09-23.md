*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic launches Claude Opus 5.5, cheaper and faster than Opus 5
Anthropic's first release since CEO Dario Amodei called for the industry to "pace the frontier," Opus 5.5 matches Claude Fable 5.1 on agentic coding, computer use, and multidisciplinary reasoning while costing 40% less to run ($4/$20 per million tokens) and generating output over 30% faster. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/22/anthropic-releases-opus-5-5-with-lower-prices-and-fable-level-performance/)

### OpenAI cuts GPT-6 API prices in half with new Sol and Luna models
GPT-6 Sol now runs $2/$10 per million input/output tokens (down from $4/$20) and GPT-6 Luna comes in at $0.10/$0.50, with gains in coding, computer use, and factual accuracy — an aggressive response to Anthropic's pricing move the same day. — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/openai-releases-gpt-6-sol-and-luna-models-slashing-api-costs-50-or-more)

### OpenAI, Anthropic, DeepSeek, and Moonshot to brief the UN Security Council on AI risks
In a first for the Council, frontier labs from both the US and China — plus AI safety researcher Yoshua Bengio and Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue — address the 15-member body Wednesday on AI risk, days ahead of the Trump-Xi summit and amid Amodei's push to slow AI development. — [Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/world-news/deepseek-openai-and-anthropic-to-brief-un-security-council-on-ai-this-week-126092201553_1.html)

## 🛠 Development

### Alphabet's Intrinsic open-sources its industrial robotics platform under Apache 2.0
Unveiled at ROSCon 2026 as Intrinsic Core, the ROS-compatible release bundles real-time hardware-agnostic control, Nvidia FoundationPose-based pose estimation, motion and grasp planning, and simulation/calibration tooling — a significant bet on open infrastructure for "physical AI" development. — [SiliconANGLE](https://siliconangle.com/2026/09/22/googles-robotics-unit-intrinsic-open-sources-its-foundational-infrastructure-for-intelligent-robots/)

### GitHub Copilot CLI 1.0.89 adds Claude Opus 5.5 support hours after its release
The same-day update also improves managed-connector consent flows with a copyable authorization URL, showing how fast coding-agent tooling now tracks new frontier-model releases. — [GitHub](https://github.com/github/copilot-cli/releases/tag/v1.0.89-0)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Data-center builder Accelevation files for a US IPO targeting a $5.4B valuation
The Olympus Partners-backed firm, which designs the power and cooling infrastructure behind AI data centers, is offering 30 million shares at $20–$24 to raise up to $720 million, mostly to refinance debt — the latest sign of investor appetite for AI-infrastructure listings. — [KSL/Reuters](https://www.ksl.com/article/51627006/accelevation-targets-up-to-54-billion-valuation-in-us-ipo)

### Shopify brings Shop Pay checkout to Meta's Muse AI agent as Amazon locks Muse out
The partnership lets Muse users complete purchases from Shopify merchants directly inside the AI assistant, intensifying the agentic-commerce race just as Amazon moves to block the same agent from its own storefront. — [PYMNTS](https://www.pymnts.com/commerce/ecommerce/2026/shopify-brings-shop-pay-checkout-solution-to-metas-muse-ai-agent/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### F5 patches actively exploited BIG-IP APM zero-day, CISA adds it to KEV
CVE-2026-94127, a CVSS 9.8 heap-based buffer overflow, lets unauthenticated attackers achieve remote code execution on BIG-IP APM instances configured with an OAuth authorization-server profile; F5 shipped hotfixes and an emergency iRule after confirming in-the-wild exploitation. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/f5-patches-critical-big-ip-apm-zero-day.html)

### WordPress 7.1.2 fixes a critical unauthenticated RCE affecting nearly every version since 4.7
CVE-2026-87902 (CVSS 9.2) lets attackers manipulate WordPress's page-template resolution to execute arbitrary PHP files without logging in; the fix ships in 7.1.2 and 24 backported releases down to 4.7.37, and unpatched sites are expected to face automated scanning immediately. — [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/09/23/cve-2026-87902-wordpress-7-1-2-security-release/)

### Meta hot-fixes a Muse zero-day that let local malware hijack the AI agent's dictation
Researcher Patrick Wardle disclosed that an unprivileged local process on macOS could silently redirect Muse's voice-dictation traffic to an attacker-controlled endpoint; Meta shipped a fix roughly 16 hours after the public disclosure. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/21/meta-muse-ai-app-flaw-lets-local-malware-redirect-dictation-traffic/5297980)

### Graphalgo malware campaign spreads into Terraform providers and Go modules
Researchers found trojanized Terraform providers (including a typosquat of the widely used `kreuzwerker/docker`) and malicious Go modules deploying a RAT with dual Slack-bot and Ethereum-smart-contract command channels — the first known malware distributed through Terraform providers, extending a campaign that began on npm. — [Aikido Security](https://www.aikido.dev/blog/graphalgo-terraform-go-modules)
