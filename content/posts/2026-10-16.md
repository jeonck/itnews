*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic gives outside auditors employee-like access to its models
Anthropic said six staff from METR, a nonprofit AI evaluator, now have access to its systems comparable to an employee's, and that METR will publish its own risk assessments; Anthropic says it cannot veto publication but can require redaction of narrow categories of information. The move follows Anthropic's pledge for stronger third-party assessment and its signing of the White House Accord on Super Intelligence, and sits alongside similar tiered-access arrangements with SecureBio and Accenture. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/an-update-on-independent-evaluators)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Anthropic opens a Singapore office, signs AI-safety MOU with the government
Anthropic co-founder Ben Mann and Singapore's Ministry of Digital Development and Information signed an MOU on model evaluation, testing standards, and incident response, during Singapore International Cyber Week. Anthropic says Singapore ranks second of 121 countries in Claude.ai usage relative to population, with GovTech Singapore alone running more than 1,500 engineers on Claude Code. — [DevDiscourse](https://www.devdiscourse.com/article/business/3992335-anthropic-opens-office-in-singapore-partners-with-government-on-ai-safety-and-adoption)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### SAP warns of maximum-severity OAuth2 flaw in its Cloud SDK for Python
CVE-2026-89111, rated a perfect 10.0 on CVSS, lets an unauthenticated attacker manipulate a tenant-subdomain value to redirect OAuth2 token requests to a server they control, handing over the application's client ID and secret; multi-tenant SAP BTP applications are the most exposed. SAP's interim guidance is to validate the tenant subdomain against an allowlist before passing it to the SDK, pending a full patch rollout. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sap-warns-of-maximum-severity-oauth2-ssrf-security-flaw)

### Drupal patches a critical remote-code-execution flaw in core
CVE-2026-108097, in Drupal core's Field UI module, isn't directly exploitable on its own but can be chained through contributed modules — at least one path requires no authentication — to achieve remote code execution. Fixed in Drupal 11.4.10, 11.3.19, and 10.6.19; sites still on Drupal 9, 8.9, or other end-of-life branches won't get a patch and need to upgrade. — [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/critical-drupal-vulnerability-allows-remote-code-execution/)
