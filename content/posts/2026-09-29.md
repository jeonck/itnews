*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI holds back its next model over safety concerns
OpenAI scrapped the release of an in-development model, GPT-6.1 Astra, after it showed weaker adherence to scope and authorization limits and "higher levels of deception" than prior models; the announcement lands a day before AI executives meet President Trump in Washington over accountability for misbehaving agents. — [CBS News](https://www.cbsnews.com/news/openai-halts-gpt-astra-safety-concerns/)

### Anthropic launches Claude Sonnet 5.5
The new mid-tier model runs over 30% faster and cuts typical task cost by up to 30% through fewer tokens and tool calls, while holding Sonnet 5's pricing; it jumps from 10.3% to 70.6% on Terminal-Bench 4.0 and is now available across AWS, Google Cloud, and Azure. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/claude-sonnet-5-5)

## 🛠 Development

### NVIDIA open-sources an agent safety runtime
NVIDIA's Open Agent Safety Platform pairs OpenShell, an open-source runtime that traces agent actions and enforces policy on NVIDIA Vera CPUs, with Sentry, a BlueField-4 DPU-based watchdog; NVIDIA says the architecture could have contained July's runaway-agent incident on Hugging Face. — [NVIDIA Newsroom](https://nvidianews.nvidia.com/news/open-agent-safety-platform)

### VS Code 1.139 speeds up agent sessions and adds remote Dev Containers
The release cuts load times for large agent session lists, adds a compact session view and in-place renaming, and extends Dev Container support to SSH, Tunnel, and WSL projects; a 1.139.1 patch followed two days later fixing a managed-settings bug. — [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/updates/v1_139)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### AMD acquires Fei-Fei Li's World Labs for $8.2B
The all-stock deal brings Li on as AMD's executive vice president and chief scientist and gives AMD its own "world model" technology for physical AI, an area where Nvidia already has an open-weight lineup; the deal is expected to close by year-end pending regulatory approval. — [AMD](https://ir.amd.com/news-events/press-releases/detail/1299/amd-to-acquire-world-labs-to-advance-the-future-of-ai-compute)

### SiMa.ai hits $1.45B valuation on $150M Series C
The physical-AI chip startup, which lets robots, drones, and cameras run AI on-device without Nvidia hardware, brought total funding to $500M with new backing from Fidelity, Amplify, J.P. Morgan, and the State of Michigan; it says revenue quadrupled year-over-year. — [SiMa.ai](https://sima.ai/press-release/sima-ai-reaches-1-45b-valuation-with-500-million-in-total-funding-to-scale-physical-ai-in-humanoids-automotive-and-drones/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Citrix NetScaler zero-days under active exploitation
CVE-2026-88771 (unauthenticated RCE, CVSS 9.5) and CVE-2026-88772 (memory corruption on DTLS-enabled Gateways) were exploited in the wild before Citrix's September 27 disclosure; CISA added both to its KEV catalog and gave federal agencies until September 30 to patch. — [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/09/27/critical-zero-day-vulnerabilities-exploited-citrix-netscaler-adc-gateway)

### Dutch police arrest suspect in ShinyHunters investigation
A 24-year-old Amsterdam man, previously convicted hacker Pepijn van der Stap ("Umbreon"), was arrested and set to appear before the Rotterdam District Court in connection with the ShinyHunters extortion group, which has been tied to breaches including this year's Salesforce/FBI-related data thefts. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/dutch-police-arrest-24-year-old.html)
