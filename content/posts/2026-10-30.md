*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Apollo economist: AI has delivered "zero financial payoff" outside tech
Apollo Global Management's chief economist Torsten Slok analyzed the S&P 493 (the S&P 500 minus the seven biggest tech names) and found flat profit margins and flat revenue per worker, concluding AI spending hasn't yet moved the bottom line at most large companies. The finding adds hard data to a growing debate over whether enterprise AI investment is paying off as fast as the hype suggests. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/ai-delivers-zero-financial-payoff-to-most-companies-according-to-apollo-economist/)

### Grok releases the first seven minutes of its AI-generated "Odyssey"
xAI's Grok Imagine, working with Wonder Studios, published Episode 1 of an AI-generated adaptation of Homer's Odyssey, three months after Elon Musk promised a full-length film to rival Christopher Nolan's live-action version. Reviewers found the visuals more polished than expected but said the storytelling still lacks the craft and emotional pull of traditional filmmaking — a useful data point on where generative video actually stands. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/25/grok-releases-the-first-seven-minutes-of-its-ai-generated-odyssey/)

### Meta quietly launches AI fashion app Style Sketch
The app builds a virtual closet from a user's Instagram photos or new uploads, then suggests outfit combinations and lets users preview looks on a virtual model matched to their body type. It's live only in Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand so far, and fits a broader Meta push to ship many smaller AI apps quickly. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/meta-quietly-launches-an-ai-fashion-app-called-style-sketch/)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub makes Copilot Fusion generally available
Announced at GitHub Universe, Fusion replaces manual model-picking with automatic routing and orchestration across models, letting developers choose an effort level (Standard, High, Max) instead of a specific model. It ships across VS Code, the Copilot CLI, JetBrains IDEs, and the Copilot app, with enterprise admins able to restrict which models it can draw on. — [Source](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-10-28-route-and-orchestrate-models-with-github-copilot-fusion)

### GitHub's Octoverse 2026: AI agents now open 40%+ of all pull requests
GitHub's annual report, also unveiled at Universe, shows commits, Actions jobs, and merged PRs all accelerating sharply after June, with Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella citing a figure of over 40% of GitHub pull requests now opened by AI agents (up from 17% in March). Agent-authored PRs are also merging slightly faster than human-authored ones, a sign agentic coding is moving from novelty to default workflow. — [Source](https://github.blog/news-insights/octoverse/octoverse-commits-pushes-reach-new-highs-in-2026-as-developers-build-with-agents/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### DoorDash agrees to acquire Instacart for $17 billion
DoorDash will pay $65 per share for Instacart in an all-approved deal expected to close in late 2027 or early 2028, combining DoorDash's restaurant-delivery network with Instacart's grocery business; Instacart's board walked away from separate talks with Uber to take the offer. It's one of the largest consolidations yet in on-demand delivery. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/doordash-to-acquire-instacart-for-17b/)

### Microsoft offers buyouts to roughly 60,000 employees
The voluntary exit offer covers about a quarter of Microsoft's workforce — staff at level 70 and below in the US, UK, Canada, and India — as the company redirects spending toward AI infrastructure. Employees have until early December to decide, following a smaller buyout round and 15,000 layoffs earlier in 2025. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/26/microsoft-offers-buyouts-to-around-60000-employees-reports-say/)

### Amazon's cloud business booms, but overall results miss expectations
AWS posted strong revenue growth on continued AI demand, and CEO Andy Jassy said AWS is on track to become a $1 trillion business — but heavy spending plans and misses elsewhere in Amazon's results left the overall report short of Wall Street's expectations. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/amazon-reported-booming-cloud-revenue-but-otherwise-missed-expectations/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Binance suspends withdrawals after suspected multibillion-dollar hack
The world's largest crypto exchange halted withdrawals on multiple networks after users reported funds draining from its internet-connected hot wallets; crypto trackers estimate at least $2 billion stolen, with some estimates reaching $5.7 billion as the theft may still be ongoing. Circle and Tether have already frozen roughly $2.3 billion combined in stablecoins tied to the incident, and Binance says it's investigating and that user funds will be made whole. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/binance-pauses-withdrawals-as-hackers-suspected-of-stealing-billions-in-crypto/)

### Polymarket briefly halts trading after third-party vendor compromise
The same day as the Binance incident, prediction-market platform Polymarket paused trading after a compromised third-party vendor let hackers drain roughly $2 million in user funds — another reminder that supply-chain compromises of front-end dependencies remain a reliable path into crypto platforms even when the core exchange itself isn't breached. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/29/binance-pauses-withdrawals-as-hackers-suspected-of-stealing-billions-in-crypto/)
