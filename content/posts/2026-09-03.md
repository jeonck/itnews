*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google ships Gemini 3.8 Flash and a cybersecurity-focused Cyber variant
Google's third Flash release in six weeks claims its best reasoning and coding performance yet, outperforming larger frontier models on long-horizon software engineering benchmarks at a fraction of the cost, while the new Cyber variant targets governments and trusted partners through Google's limited-access Fairwind program. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/02/with-gemini-38-flash-google-reminds-everyone-its-still-in-the-race/5294049)

### Meta takes Muse Code out of beta with an SDK preview and paid tiers
Less than a month after launch, Meta's terminal-based coding agent graduates to general availability with inter-session messaging, a rewind feature for undoing agent actions, a developer-preview SDK for building custom agents on top of it, and three subscription plans starting at $5/month. — [The New Stack](https://thenewstack.io/muse-code-sdk-pricing/)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub Copilot code review can now approve pull requests
Every Copilot code review now includes an approval assessment, and admins can opt in to let Copilot submit an actual approval that counts toward a repository's required-approvals rule — off by default, and dismissed like a human reviewer's approval if new commits land afterward. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-01-copilot-code-review-can-now-approve-pull-requests/)

### Google purges the last Manifest V2 extensions from the Chrome Web Store, including uBlock Origin
The final phase of Chrome's migration away from Manifest V2 removed all remaining MV2 listings — uBlock Origin included — from search and the store catalogue; already-installed copies keep working but can no longer get updates through Google's marketplace, pushing users toward Manifest V3 alternatives like uBlock Origin Lite. — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/chrome-manifest-v2-extensions-removed-3678445/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Uber cuts 3,300 jobs — its biggest layoffs since the pandemic
CEO Dara Khosrowshahi is cutting roughly 10% of global staff and 20% of managerial roles to remove organizational layers built up during years of growth, redirecting spending toward ride-sharing, delivery, and the company's robotaxi push. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/02/uber-is-laying-off-10-of-staff-or-3300-people/)

### Snowflake shares jump 22% as AI coding-agent adoption drives a revenue beat
Snowflake posted Q2 revenue of $1.55B (up 35% year-over-year) and raised its annual outlook, crediting growth partly to its CoCo AI coding agent, which grew to 9,100 accounts in the quarter as customers increasingly use Snowflake's tools to power their own AI agents. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/02/snowflake-snow-q2-earnings-report-2027.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### SonicWall discloses two actively exploited SMA1000 zero-days, one a perfect-10
CVE-2026-83548 (CVSS 10.0, pre-auth SSRF) can be chained with CVE-2026-83549 to achieve unauthenticated remote code execution on SMA1000 appliances; SonicWall has confirmed in-the-wild exploitation and both flaws are now in CISA's KEV catalog, with hotfixes available for affected 6210/7210/8200v models. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sonicwall-warns-of-actively-exploited-sma1000-zero-day-flaws/)

### 33-hour BGP hijack diverted Softaculous traffic and pushed a malicious Virtualizor update
Attackers announced a hijacked block of Hetzner IP space to redirect Softaculous's billing site and Virtualizor's software-update endpoint, obtaining a valid Let's Encrypt certificate through the hijacked route and delivering a malicious update package to some installations because the update client didn't verify package signatures; affected customers are advised to reset credentials. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/01/33-hour-bgp-hijack-of-softaculous-traffic-prompts-security-scramble/5293608)
