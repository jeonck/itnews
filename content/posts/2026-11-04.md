*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Chinese open-weight model GLM-5.3 pulls off complex cyberattacks in controlled tests
AI security firm Irregular found Zhipu/Z.AI's GLM-5.3 completed 3 of 13 multi-stage attack scenarios, breaching protected services and coordinating across machines without refusing a single offensive task — NIST separately called it the most capable open-weight model for cybersecurity tasks released so far, though still roughly four months behind leading US systems. — [Source](https://www.khaleejtimes.com/business/chinese-ai-model-carries-out-complex-cyberattacks-in-controlled-tests-researchers-say-2)

### Ai2 releases StateBench, exposing where frontier models lose track of long documents
The new benchmark tests whether models can track entity values that change across a document; frontier models like Gemini 3.8 Flash, Claude Opus 5.5, and GPT-6 Sol stayed accurate on simple updates but accuracy on complex chained dependencies fell to 13% or lower at long context lengths. — [Source](https://huggingface.co/blog/allenai/statebench)

### Google DeepMind turns Antigravity into an agentic chemistry workbench
New cheminformatics skills let Google's Antigravity platform mine literature and patents for compounds, run matched molecular pair analysis, and propose new drug-candidate molecules with synthesis routes — grounded in RDKit, ChEMBL, and PubChem, with DeepMind stressing the designs are hypotheses still needing lab validation. — [Source](https://deepmind.google/blog/agentic-cheminformatics-with-google-antigravity/)

## 🛠 Development

### Anthropic quietly steers Claude Code away from recreating existing commercial games
A two-sentence addition to Claude Code's Opus 5.5 prompt blocks requests to rebuild a specific existing game pixel-for-pixel, citing Anthropic's usage policy on IP — original games, genre clones, and projects built from a user's own designs are still unaffected, though how licensed or client-owned recreations are handled remains unclear. — [Source](https://www.gamedeveloper.com/programming/anthropic-appears-to-have-ordered-ai-tool-claude-to-stop-remaking-video-games)

### Hugging Face proposes lightweight "Model Integration Contracts" for Transformers
The new contracts pair a small CPU-sized fixture model, a runnable usage example, and human-reviewed baseline outputs so regressions can be caught in pull requests instead of after merge — the team points to a real UMT5 attention bug that went undetected for nearly two months as the motivating case. — [Source](https://huggingface.co/blog/transformers-mic)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Anthropic's revenue hit $17B last quarter, but compute now eats 58% of it
Anthropic's quarterly revenue rose from $11.5B to $17B, yet compute spending hit $10B and climbed to 58% of revenue (from 48% the prior quarter), widening operating losses; the company pays SpaceX $1.25B a month for compute and says its own data-center buildout won't keep pace with demand anytime soon. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/anthropic-is-still-grappling-with-the-economics-of-compute/)

### Apple tightens App Store age ratings for dating and AI-companion apps
Starting November 16, apps with dating, romantic, or companion-AI features — potentially including apps like Character.AI and Replika — must carry new content descriptors and an 18+ rating, part of a broader update to how Apple classifies location-sharing and relationship-oriented apps. — [Source](https://techcrunch.com/2026/11/03/apple-restricts-dating-apps-and-ai-companions-to-users-18-and-up/)

### Super Micro's preliminary results blow past guidance on AI demand
Ahead of its November 3 earnings call, Super Micro estimated fiscal Q1 2027 revenue at $18.5–19.5B — well above its earlier $14.5–15.5B guidance — and gross margin at 12.0–12.5% versus a prior 10.4–10.8%, crediting surging demand for AI platforms. — [Source](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/0001375365/000137536526000025/smcifirstquarterfy2027busi.htm)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Binance suffers record $6.3B crypto theft linked to North Korea
Blockchain intelligence firm TRM Labs says hackers drained roughly $6.36 billion from Binance wallets in about 20 minutes — the largest crypto theft on record — with TRM and Chainalysis both attributing it with high confidence to North Korea's TraderTraitor group; the FBI is involved in the response, and Binance says it froze about $3 billion in assets and will cover all losses. — [Source](https://www.nextgov.com/cybersecurity/2026/10/fbi-involved-response-63-billion-binance-hack-linked-north-korea/416946/)

### CISA adds actively exploited WordPress Core race-condition bug to its KEV catalog
CVE-2026-119977, a time-of-check/time-of-use race condition in WordPress Core's update process (CVSS 8.1), was added November 3 after evidence of active exploitation; affected sites should update to 7.1.4 or 7.0.8. — [Source](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/11/03/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog)

### Chainalysis discloses breach via "offensive agentic techniques"
The blockchain analytics firm said an external actor used AI-driven tooling to access internal systems and expose customer names, emails, user IDs, and organization roles — Chainalysis says investigation content, credentials, and session tokens were not affected, and the incident appears unrelated to the Binance theft. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/chainalysis-confirms-intrusion-into.html)
