*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google ships Gemini 3.8 Live with Live Avatar to Gemini Enterprise
The update natively couples Gemini's live-dialogue model with near-real-time generated video, producing a 24fps avatar with lip-sync across 97 languages that can keep talking while backend tools run; all output carries SynthID watermarks, and custom avatars require enterprise allowlisting. — [Google Blog](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/models-and-research/gemini-models/gemini-3-8-live-with-live-avatar/)

### Sanders and Casar introduce a bill to ban superintelligent AI outright
The "Ban Artificial Superintelligence Act" would permanently prohibit AI exceeding human cognitive performance, pause advanced frontier development until a new cabinet-level Department of Artificial Intelligence sets safety rules, and impose corporate dissolution plus up to 20 years in prison for violations — one of the most aggressive AI bills yet filed in Congress. — [Sen. Sanders' office](https://www.sanders.senate.gov/press-releases/news-sanders-casar-introduce-legislation-to-ban-artificial-superintelligence-and-temporarily-pause-advanced-ai-development/)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub sets a global default-enablement policy for Copilot features
A new "AI Controls" setting lets enterprises choose whether future Copilot features (including Copilot Code Review and MCP servers in Copilot) turn on automatically, stay off by default, or are left to individual orgs; the policy takes effect October 22, giving admins a 28-day window to configure it now. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-24-default-enablement-of-copilot-features-for-copilot-business-and-enterprise/)

### Microsoft rotates its NuGet author-signing certificate
Starting September 23, new NuGet packages signed by Microsoft use a new certificate fingerprint; teams enforcing trusted-signer allowlists or certificate-pinning policies need to add the new fingerprint now or risk failed package verification. — [.NET Blog](https://devblogs.microsoft.com/dotnet/microsoft-author-signing-certificate-update-2026/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Anthropic commits $11.6B to Akamai for CPU-based cloud capacity
The seven-year deal — six times the size of a prior agreement — centers on general-purpose CPU compute rather than GPUs, comes with an option to expand to roughly $20B, and hands Akamai a warrant convertible into up to ~5% of its stock; it pushes Anthropic's disclosed compute commitments past $500B in under a year. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/25/anthropic-to-pay-akamai-11-6-billion-over-seven-years-in-cloud-deal/)

### Amazon blocks Meta's Muse from shopping on its site
Amazon says Meta's AI shopping agent accessed its store without authorization, didn't identify itself as automated, and captured customer login credentials; Amazon is now also moving to block similar agents from Google and OpenAI, sharpening a fight over who controls the customer relationship as agentic commerce scales up. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-09-21/amazon-blocks-meta-s-muse-ai-agent-from-its-retail-site)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### ShinyHunters claims a breach of the FBI's jobs website
The group says it exploited a zero-day in Oracle PeopleSoft to pull sensitive data on nearly all FBI agents and applicants — names, SSNs, addresses, and emergency contacts in samples reviewed by reporters — allegedly in retaliation for an FBI FLASH report on its activity; FBIjobs.gov remains offline while the Bureau investigates. — [Federal News Network](https://federalnewsnetwork.com/cybersecurity/2026/09/fbi-investigates-apparent-breach-of-its-jobs-website-hackers-claim-to-have-sensitive-employee-data/)

### Gyazo breach exposes 23.6M user records and 490M image metadata entries
Attackers exploited a flaw in the image-hosting service's upload server on September 11, stealing names, emails, password hashes, device IDs, and session tokens, plus metadata for hundreds of millions of older images; Gyazo is forcing password resets and urges users to change reused credentials elsewhere. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/gyazo-breach-exposes-2362-million-user.html)

### Critical unauthenticated WordPress core RCE under active exploitation
CVE-2026-87902 (CVSS 9.2) lets attackers abuse WordPress's page-template resolution to include arbitrary local PHP files and, in many configurations, execute code with no login required; Patchstack observed scanning begin less than five hours after the September 22 patch (WordPress 7.1.2) shipped, so unpatched sites are already being probed. — [Patchstack](https://patchstack.com/articles/cve-2026-87902-attackers-started-probing-wordpress-sites-hours-after-the-patch/)

### Unpatched OnePlus/OPPO root-access flaw disclosed after 4+ months with no fix
Researcher Rasmus Moorats chained two flaws in OxygenOS privileged services to let a zero-permission app silently gain root on a stock OnePlus 15 with no user prompt; OnePlus acknowledged the bug in May and confirmed it also affects all OPPO devices, but Moorats went public September 24 with still no patch available. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/unpatched-oneplus-flaws-let-installed.html)
