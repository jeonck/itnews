*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI's frontier models land on Google Cloud, completing the big-three sweep
Google Cloud added GPT-6 Astra, GPT-6.1 Sol, and GPT-6 Luna to its Model Garden in private preview, billed through Google Cloud Marketplace — meaning OpenAI's flagship models are now available across all three major hyperscalers (AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud) alongside Anthropic's Claude and Google's own Gemini. — [Source](https://www.techzine.eu/news/applications/145174/openais-models-are-finally-fully-supported-on-google-cloud/)

### Google lets its own Gemini Enterprise customers run rival Claude models
Gemini Enterprise administrators can now enable Claude Opus 5.5 and Claude Sonnet 5.5 directly inside the Gemini Enterprise app, with a follow-up update adding per-user quota controls so one heavy user can't burn through a shared subscription pool. It's a telling sign that Google would rather sell access to a competitor's model than lose the enterprise seat entirely. — [Source](https://docs.cloud.google.com/gemini/enterprise/docs/release-notes)

## 🛠 Development

### GitLab patches a maximum-severity, 12-year-old unauthenticated RCE
CVE-2026-110264 (CVSS 10.0) is an integer overflow in Onigmo, the regex engine GitLab ships with Ruby, letting an unauthenticated attacker run arbitrary code on self-managed instances going back to version 7.0 (2014). There's no workaround — GitLab.com and Dedicated are already patched, but self-managed admins need to update to 19.5.1 or one of the other fixed releases immediately. — [Source](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/critical-gitlab-flaw-cvss-100-could.html)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Jim Keller's Tenstorrent raises $1.5B at a $6.2B valuation
The AI chip startup's Series E, co-led by Trace Capital and Innovation X with Fidelity, ARK, Morgan Stanley, and Tether among the backers, lands five months after Tenstorrent reportedly turned down acquisition interest from Intel and Qualcomm at up to $5B. The company says the money funds volume manufacturing of its Galaxy AI servers and its chip-licensing business. — [Source](https://dealroom.co/news/163940-tenstorrent-raises-1-5b-series-e-doubling-valuation-to-6-2b/)

### Ex-OpenAI research chief's manufacturing-AI startup Arda raises $230M at $1.5B
Bob McGrew, who led OpenAI's reasoning research before ChatGPT's launch, pitches Arda as a Cursor-like coding agent for factory floors: it reads a product's CAD files and generates assembly instructions and station layouts automatically. Defense contractor Anduril, Arda's first customer, says work instructions that used to take 10 hours now take 15 minutes. — [Source](https://thenextweb.com/news/arda-bob-mcgrew-232m-1-5bn-ai-manufacturing)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### "PHP2Shell" RCE flaws are under active exploitation — and reach into Fortinet, Cisco, and Palo Alto gear
CVE-2026-109618 and CVE-2026-109619 let an unauthenticated attacker corrupt PHP's memory and execute code as the web server, affecting any PHP install that serves requests (PHP-FPM, mod_php, FrankenPHP, LiteSpeed). Cloudflare says it was already seeing exploitation attempts before patches shipped, and because Fortinet, Cisco, and Palo Alto all embed PHP in management interfaces, their customers are exposed too — Palo Alto and Cisco have no fix out yet. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/php-ships-emergency-patches-for-critical-php2shell-flaws/)

### CISA and FBI warn SafePay ransomware has hit 600+ critical infrastructure targets
The joint advisory with German and UK partners says SafePay has breached businesses and critical infrastructure across North America, South America, Europe, and Australia since August 2024, getting in through stolen credentials, brute-forced VPNs, and known edge-device vulnerabilities, then deleting backups to block recovery. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/cisa-and-fbi-safepay-ransomware-has-hit-over-600-critical-infrastructure-organizations/)
