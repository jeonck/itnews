*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic ships Claude Fable 5.1 and Mythos 5.1, cutting agentic costs sharply
Fable 5.1 is generally available with a 1-million-token context window and a 75% price cut on cache reads (down to $0.25/M tokens), which Anthropic says lowers typical workload costs by roughly 25% and agentic workload costs by up to 45%; the more capable Mythos 5.1 stays restricted to trusted-access programs for cybersecurity and life-sciences work. — [VentureBeat](https://venturebeat.com/technology/anthropics-claude-fable-5-1-and-mythos-5-1-arrive-with-a-75-cost-reduction-for-fable-cache-reads)

### OpenAI says its next model, "Astra," is capable enough to need stronger guardrails
OpenAI disclosed that an upcoming model, significantly more capable than the publicly available GPT-5.6 Sol, will require extra safety layers throughout development and eventual release — a rare public acknowledgment that a model's capability jump is outpacing its current safeguards. — [U.S. News](https://money.usnews.com/investing/news/articles/2026-09-01/openai-says-upcoming-model-is-so-capable-it-requires-stronger-guardrails)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub Copilot drops a wave of older models from Chat, edits, and completions
As of September 1, several older models were deprecated across Copilot Chat, inline edits, ask/agent modes, and code completions; Claude Sonnet 4.6 remains available only to individual subscribers on annual plans, pushing most users toward newer defaults. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-31-selected-github-copilot-models-deprecated/)

### GitHub CLI adds native media attachments for issues, PRs, and comments
A new repeatable `--attach` flag lets `gh` upload a local image or video and reference it inline in an issue, pull request, or comment body, closing a long-standing gap between the CLI and the web editor's drag-and-drop uploads. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-01-github-cli-media-in-issues-pull-requests-and-comments/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### EU classifies ChatGPT as a "Very Large Online Search Engine" under the DSA
The European Commission's first-of-its-kind designation — triggered by ChatGPT reporting more than 45 million average monthly EU users — subjects it to formal risk assessments, independent audits, and regulator data access ahead of a December compliance deadline, with fines of up to 6% of global turnover for noncompliance; Reddit and Roblox were designated Very Large Online Platforms in the same round. — [Euronews](https://www.euronews.com/next/2026/08/31/eu-places-chatgpt-reddit-and-roblox-under-strictest-digital-safety-rules)

### Anthropic signs $35B cloud deal with Nvidia-backed Lambda for a Texas data center
The agreement covers roughly 350 megawatts of AI training and inference capacity on a Nueces County, Texas campus, with Nvidia playing triple duty as chip supplier, Lambda equity backer, and lease anchor — arriving days after Anthropic's separate $45B compute commitment to Nscale in West Virginia. — [Forbes](https://www.forbes.com/sites/jonmarkman/2026/09/01/anthropic-books-35-billion-to-nvidia-backed-lambda-for-cloud-capacity/)

### Apple accuses OpenAI of destroying evidence in trade-secrets lawsuit
In a filing backing its push for expedited fact-finding, Apple says a former iPhone engineer who moved to OpenAI used a confidential Apple circuit schematic in his new role; a judge is set to hear arguments on Apple's preliminary-injunction motion October 1. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/01/apple-openai-lawsuit-ai-gpt-devices)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Attackers actively exploiting critical Langflow and Ruby on Rails RCE flaws
VulnCheck observed intrusion attempts within hours of CVE-2026-0768 (unauthenticated RCE in Langflow's custom-component code validator, CVSS 9.8) and CVE-2026-66066 "KindaRails2Shell" (CVSS 9.5, leaks Rails secrets and master keys) landing on CISA's Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, with command-and-control infrastructure traced to a host in Israel. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/attackers-exploit-critical-langflow-and.html)

### CISA adds two actively exploited PaperCut vulnerabilities to its KEV catalog
CVE-2026-81578 and CVE-2026-82078 affect PaperCut NG/MF print-management software — a platform ransomware crews have weaponized in prior campaigns — triggering a mandatory patch deadline for federal agencies under CISA's binding directive. — [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/08/31/cisa-adds-two-known-exploited-vulnerabilities-catalog)
