*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI brings GPT-6 and a new "Intelligent UI" to ChatGPT
OpenAI's release notes confirm GPT-6 is now live across ChatGPT tiers, with paid plans running GPT-6 Sol and Free/Go users on GPT-6 Luna. The headline feature, Intelligent UI, lets ChatGPT choose a response format per question — interactive charts, calculators, forms, or diagrams instead of always defaulting to plain text — and OpenAI says GPT-6 Instant answers web-search questions roughly 44% faster than its predecessor. — [OpenAI ChatGPT release notes](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes-0)

### Google Cloud launches a universal "Gemini agent" that can also run on Claude
Google's new enterprise agent, unveiled at its Gemini at Work event, retains memory and context across Gmail, Docs, Sheets, and Chat, spins up specialized sub-agents for parallel work, and keeps running in the cloud even when a device is offline. Notably, it routes tasks to whichever model fits best — including Anthropic's Claude — rather than staying Gemini-only, underscoring how even Google now treats model choice as a feature rather than a moat. — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/google-cloud-introduces-gemini-agent-as-universal-ai-agent-for-work/)

## 🛠 Development

### Supabase raises $150M and acquires database startup Turso
The round, led by Singapore's GIC with Alphabet's CapitalG also participating, lands just four months after Supabase's $500M Series F at a $10.5B valuation. The Turso acquisition adds technology for cheaply provisioning huge numbers of isolated databases, which Supabase says is aimed squarely at AI agents — the company claims 70% of new databases on its platform are now created by agents rather than humans. — [Pulse2](https://pulse2.com/supabase-raises-150-million-and-acquires-turso/)

### Stack Overflow's 2026 Developer Survey: AI use is universal, trust is not
Based on more than 30,000 responses from 169 countries, the survey found 65.9% of developers now use AI coding assistants or agents, but only 6.6% say they'd trust AI output for an important decision outright. Nearly half said trust depends on being able to verify the answer, and 78.7% said source attribution matters when judging AI-generated code. — [Stack Overflow Blog](https://stackoverflow.blog/2026/10/06/the-results-of-the-2026-developer-survey-are-here/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Schneider Electric to buy industrial software maker PTC for $22.6 billion
The all-cash deal pays $205 per PTC share, a 42% premium, and comes a day after the Financial Times reported the two sides were close at a lower price. It's one of the largest industrial-software acquisitions of the year and extends Schneider's push to bundle PTC's CAD/PLM and IoT software with its own automation and energy-management business; the deal isn't expected to close until Q3 2027 pending regulatory approval. — [Robotics 24/7](https://www.robotics247.com/article/schneider-electric-to-acquire-ptc-for-22.6-billion)

### Manus parent Butterfly Effect raises $500M+ in first round since Meta deal collapsed
The Chinese AI agent startup's Series C was led by Boyu Capital and IDG Capital, with existing backers Tencent, HSG, and ZhenFund also participating. It's Butterfly Effect's first funding since Chinese regulators forced it to unwind a planned Meta acquisition earlier this year over concerns about losing AI talent to the West; the company is reportedly now weighing a Hong Kong listing. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/08/chinas-manus-raises-over-500m-in-first-funding-round-since-split-with-meta/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Critical FortiMail zero-day under active exploitation, no patch yet available
CVE-2026-104286 (CVSS 9.8) lets an unauthenticated attacker write arbitrary files to FortiMail appliances via crafted HTTP/HTTPS requests. Fortinet confirmed in-the-wild exploitation in its advisory, and CISA added the flaw to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog the same day; fixed builds (8.0.2, 7.6.7, 7.4.9) are still pending, so Fortinet's only interim guidance is to disable the IBE feature or lock down management-interface access. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/fortinet-warns-of-critical-fortimail-flaw-exploited-in-zero-day-attacks)

### Citrix patches a third critical NetScaler flaw in two weeks
CVE-2026-107406, a memory-overflow bug rated CVSS 9.5 that can lead to remote code execution or denial of service on SAML-configured NetScaler ADC/Gateway deployments, arrived just days after admins finished patching two other actively-exploited NetScaler zero-days. Citrix says it has seen no exploitation of this one yet, but the pace of back-to-back critical NetScaler bugs this month is straining patch cycles for admins who just upgraded. — [CSO Online](https://www.csoonline.com/article/4230642/citrix-warns-of-actively-exploited-netscaler-flaw-days-after-zero-day-patch-rush.html)
