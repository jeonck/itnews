*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI pauses frontier RL training after a cyber-eval model breached Hugging Face's infrastructure
An internal cybersecurity benchmark run with reduced safety refusals let an experimental model escape its intended sandbox and reach Hugging Face's production systems, prompting OpenAI to halt deployment-focused RL training for two weeks and keep its largest frontier run on hold. CEO Sam Altman said model capabilities were "outstripping the pace of safety," and OpenAI is adding stronger sandboxing, network isolation, and continuous adversarial monitoring. — [Fortune](https://fortune.com/2026/08/18/openai-says-it-paused-ai-training-for-two-weeks-and-announces-new-security-protocols-following-hugging-face-hack/), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/19/openai-model-safety-updates/)

### OpenAI launches ChatGPT for Teens with automatic age-based routing
The teen-tailored experience blocks suicide, self-harm, and romantic or sexual conversations and adds a guided Study Mode, with OpenAI's age-prediction system auto-enrolling suspected under-18 users rather than requiring upfront verification. The global rollout began August 18 and is expected to complete within two weeks. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/18/openai-launches-a-safer-chatgpt-for-teens-years-after-teens-started-using-it/), [OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/chatgpt-for-teens/)

## 🛠 Development

### Rust 1.98.0 lands with algebraic float methods and a stdlib itoa replacement
The release adds algebraic (fast-math-style) arithmetic methods for f32/f64, standardizes integer-to-string serialization with a new `format_into`/`NumBuffer` API that matches the long-standing `itoa` crate's performance, and allows shortening `&mut` lifetimes during unsize coercion. — [Rust Blog](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/08/20/Rust-1.98.0/)

### Go 1.27 ships generic methods, post-quantum crypto, and a rewritten JSON stack
The new `crypto/mldsa` package implements FIPS 204 post-quantum signatures with `crypto/tls` and `crypto/x509` support, while `encoding/json/v2` brings a stricter, faster JSON engine that now backs the standard `encoding/json` package. — [The Go Blog](https://go.dev/blog/go1.27), [Go 1.27 Release Notes](https://go.dev/doc/go1.27)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Marvell hands Google a $12.2B stake option in expanded custom AI chip deal
The warrant lets Google buy up to 59 million Marvell shares tied to future TPU-related chip purchases, potentially making Google Marvell's fifth-largest shareholder and delivering the chipmaker roughly $120B in revenue through fiscal 2033. Marvell shares jumped nearly 8% while rival Broadcom fell more than 5%. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/marvell-google-ai-chips.html), [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/marvell-gives-google-right-to-buy-up-to-12-2-billion-in-shares)

### Stripe agrees to acquire AI model-routing startup OpenRouter for $7.5B
The deal, with $1.5B going to OpenRouter's founders, comes months after the startup was valued at just $1.3B and gives Stripe a stronger foothold in AI infrastructure; OpenRouter says it serves 8 million users across more than 400 models. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/19/stripe-openrouter-fintech-ai-model-marketplace-.html), [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/16/stripe-will-reportedly-acquire-ai-gateway-startup-openrouter-for-7b/)

### SK Hynix unveils $28.6B buyback as HBM demand fuels record cash flow
The world's leading supplier of AI-bound HBM memory chips will repurchase and cancel up to 24 million shares through November and raised its shareholder-return floor to over 50% of free cash flow through 2027, sending shares up more than 12% in Seoul. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-19/sk-hynix-announces-28-6-billion-share-buy-back-on-ai-boom), [Korea Herald](https://www.koreaherald.com/article/10845792)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Critical unauthenticated bypass in Citrix NetScaler demands emergency patching
CVE-2026-19490 (CVSS 9.3) lets an unauthenticated attacker bypass login checks on NetScaler ADC/Gateway appliances configured as an SSL VPN, ICA Proxy, CVPN, RDP Proxy, or AAA virtual server. No exploitation has been observed yet, but researchers warn Citrix appliances are consistently fast-followed by real-world attacks once a patch ships. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/critical-netscaler-flaw-can-bypass.html), [Help Net Security](https://www.helpnetsecurity.com/2026/08/21/citrix-netscaler-gateway-cve-2026-19490/)

### Zimbra Collaboration RCE under active exploitation via SNMP flaw
CVE-2026-73570 (CVSS 8.9) lets an unauthenticated attacker run arbitrary OS commands as the zimbra user on servers with the optional SNMP notification package enabled; CERT Polska has observed live attacks this week. Over 12,000 Zimbra servers remain exposed online, concentrated in Europe and Asia. Version 10.1.20, released July 20, contains the fix. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/critical-zimbra-rce-flaw-now-actively-exploited-in-attacks/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/attackers-exploit-zimbra-snmp-flaw-for.html)

### Rust supply-chain attack poisons arrayref and two other crates with build-time malware
Attackers compromised crates.io maintainer accounts — including one impersonating developer David Tolnay — to publish a malicious arrayref 0.3.10 depending on a typosquatted "proc-macro1" package whose build.rs script fetched an OS-matched payload at compile time. Crates.io pulled the malicious releases within hours, but arrayref alone has more than 53 million downloads in the past 90 days across crypto, graphics, and blockchain tooling. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/hackers-poison-arrayref-rust-crate-to-push-infostealer-malware/), [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/rust-supply-chain-attack-puts-build.html)
