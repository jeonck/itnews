*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI's Jalapeño chip beats Nvidia Blackwell on power efficiency
OpenAI published first benchmarks for its custom inference chip, built with Broadcom, showing 1.5–1.9x more AI work per watt and 1.7–3.6x lower latency than the Blackwell systems it was tested against at Hot Chips; SemiAnalysis independently verified the results, though only small-scale deployment is planned before 2027. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/openai-jalapeno-ai-chip-nvidia.html)

### Salesforce and Anthropic launch Claudeforce
The expanded partnership puts Claude inside Agentforce as a reasoning model and ships "Salesforce in Claude," a plugin with 37 prebuilt sales skills letting sellers query and act on live CRM data without opening Salesforce; Salesforce shares jumped 12% on the news. — [Salesforce](https://www.salesforce.com/news/press-releases/2026/08/26/salesforce-and-anthropic-announce-claudeforce/)

### Zhipu AI reveals GLM-5.3-Flash, a natively multimodal open-weight model
Zhipu confirmed the viral "Ox Alpha" model developers had been testing on OpenRouter is GLM-5.3-Flash, a 320B-parameter (18B active) MoE model released under MIT license that reportedly beats GLM-5.2 on coding and agentic tasks at a tenth of the price. — [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/08/27/zhipu-identifies-ox-alpha-as-glm-5-3-flash-and-releases-model-weights/)

## 🛠 Development

### LibreOffice 26.8 ships with Paragraph Composer and zero AI
The release adds a new text-layout algorithm for Writer, native OpenType variable-font support, calculated fields in Calc pivot tables, and better round-tripping of complex Microsoft Office charts — while the Document Foundation reiterated the suite ships with no generative-AI features and no network calls. — [OMG! Ubuntu](https://www.omgubuntu.co.uk/2026/08/libreoffice-268-released)

### Rust Foundation launches Maintainers in Residence program
The Rust Project and Rust Foundation announced their first Maintainers in Residence, directing $350,000 in donations toward year-long, funded roles maintaining critical infrastructure like the compiler, standard library, Cargo, and Clippy. — [Rust Foundation](https://rustfoundation.org/media/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Meta settles teen-addiction lawsuit for up to $16.68 billion
Meta agreed to pay 29 U.S. states over claims it designed Facebook and Instagram to be addictive to minors, and will roll out mandatory teen protections including a midnight–6am blackout, a default 2-hour daily limit, and hidden like counts. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/26/meta-social-media-trial-settlement.html)

### Amazon shuts down Mechanical Turk after 21 years
The crowdsourced-labor marketplace that Jeff Bezos once called "artificial artificial intelligence" closes September 30, as AI-training data-labeling work shifts to newer platforms like Scale AI, Mercor, and Prolific. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/25/amazon-service-that-jeff-bezos-called-artificial-ai-is-shutting-down.html)

### Korean AI startup Wrtn raises $72M at a $722M valuation
Wrtn Technologies' Series C, joined by Coreline Ventures, Goodwater Capital, and the Korea Development Bank, makes it the first Korean AI service startup to top a 1 trillion won valuation; the funds go toward overseas expansion. — [Korea Times](https://www.koreatimes.co.kr/business/tech-science/20260826/wrtn-raises-72-mil-in-series-c-funding-round)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### CISA gives federal agencies until today to patch max-severity Oracle flaw
CVE-2026-21962 (CVSS 10.0), affecting Oracle HTTP Server and the WebLogic Server Proxy Plug-in, has been under active exploitation since January; CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog on August 24 with an August 27 remediation deadline for federal agencies. — [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/08/24/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog)

### GitLab flaw exploited within days of disclosure
CVE-2026-19478 (CVSS 9.4), a GraphQL code-injection bug letting unauthenticated attackers modify or delete public projects, was being exploited in the wild within roughly two days of disclosure. Fixed in GitLab 19.2.4, 19.1.6, 19.0.8, and 18.11.11. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/gitlab-cve-2026-19478-comes-under.html)
