*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Microsoft's Nadella backs "deliberate pacing" for AI, opens MAI models to public scrutiny
Responding to Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei's essay calling for a slowdown in frontier AI development, Microsoft's chief said the company "welcomes" a more careful approach to alignment and will publish a Code of Conduct governing its in-house MAI models for public consultation — an unusual move to open internal safety guidelines to outside review. — [Neowin](https://www.neowin.net/news/microsoft-ceo-demands-human-control-for-superintelligence/)

### New Real-SWE benchmark tests coding agents on real enterprise codebases
Y Combinator-backed Specific Labs' Real-SWE runs frontier models against licensed private production code rather than public repos, and even the top scorer — Anthropic's Fable 5.1 running in Claude Code — resolved only 38.8% of tasks, well behind scores on public benchmarks, with OpenAI's GPT-6 Astra and Google's Gemini 3.8 Flash trailing further. — [Specific Labs](https://withspecific.com/benchmarks/real-swe)

## 🛠 Development

### VS Code 1.137 adds scheduled agent automations and a voice mode
The release lets developers schedule recurring agent tasks (hourly, daily, weekly, or on demand), adds an experimental hands-free voice mode for talking to agents, and brings GitHub Issues/PR management directly into the Agents view. — [Visual Studio Code](https://code.visualstudio.com/updates/v1_137)

### GitHub ships Project HydraFusion, runtime multi-model orchestration for Copilot CLI
Available now via /experimental in Copilot CLI on all plans, HydraFusion automatically picks a per-task execution pattern — a single model, or a cheap model drafting with escalation to a stronger one only when needed — to balance quality, cost, and latency instead of making developers choose a model themselves. — [GitHub Blog](https://github.blog/ai-and-ml/github-copilot/project-hydrafusion-frontier-quality-via-multi-model-orchestration/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Apple ships iOS 27 with a rebuilt, conversational Siri
The update, rolling out globally today, gives Siri its own dedicated app, multi-turn conversation memory, and "personal context" access to calendars, emails, and on-screen content for task automation — English-only and in beta for now, with the EU and China excluded at launch over regulatory issues. — [NewsBytes](https://www.newsbytesapp.com/news/science/ios-27-releases-today-new-features-and-how-to-update/story)

### Anthropic and OpenAI's push to slow AI development collides with Trump and Wall Street
Both labs' recent calls to "pace the frontier" now have to survive resistance from financial markets betting on continued rapid scaling and from the Trump administration, which has rejected slowing down given competition with China — the same fault line that pushed Microsoft to stake out a middle position this week. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/14/daily-open-ai-slowdown-trump-anthropic.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Critical Plesk Backup Manager flaw lets low-privileged users gain root
CVE-2026-68488 (CVSS 9.9) is a symlink race condition in Backup Manager's subscription-restore process that lets a low-privileged customer account seize ownership of files outside its own hosting environment, up to full root; Plesk shipped hotfixes on September 10 for Plesk Obsidian on Linux. — [Cybersecurity News](https://cybersecuritynews.com/plesk-backup-manager-flaw/)

### Revolut discloses breach after scammers impersonated a government agency
The fintech confirmed a "limited" number of customers had passport and driver's license copies, contact details, and possibly account statements and transaction histories exposed after an attacker used a spoofed government-agency email domain to trick Revolut into handing over the data; the company says its systems and customer funds were unaffected. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/12/revolut-confirms-customer-data-breach-through-fake-government-requests/)
