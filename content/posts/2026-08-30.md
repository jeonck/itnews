*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Claude autonomously designs working protein binders
In 24- to 48-hour campaigns guided only by a written protocol, Claude Opus 4.8 and Mythos Preview orchestrated full protein-binder design pipelines with no human input into any design decision, producing successful binders against 14 of 15 disease targets — a hit rate that beat human expert teams in prior design competitions. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/Claude-accelerates-protein-design)

### Anthropic launches a standard for AI to operate lab and factory hardware
The Model Hardware Standard (MHS) is a model-agnostic driver interface that lets AI agents like Claude read from and control microscopes, liquid handlers, and robotic arms, cutting hardware integration time from weeks to hours; it launches as a research preview with plans to open-source it. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/news/model-hardware-standard-research-preview)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub Copilot's August update bets on model choice over model loyalty
Visual Studio's Copilot now lets developers pin favorite models, compare capabilities and cost, and tune reasoning effort per task, while organizations can publish custom agents across repositories — a shift toward treating the underlying model as a swappable component. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-08-28-github-copilot-in-visual-studio-august-update-2/)

### LibreOffice 26.8 ships with zero generative AI features
The release adds a new paragraph-composer layout algorithm for better text justification, native OpenType variable font support, and deeper bidirectional/complex-text handling for Arabic, Hebrew, and Asian scripts — with the Document Foundation explicitly touting that no document data is sent to remote AI services. — [TDF Community Blog](https://blog.documentfoundation.org/blog/2026/08/26/libreoffice-26-8/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### OpenAI cuts off Cursor's model access after SpaceX acquisition
OpenAI is winding down Cursor's direct access to its models by November 12, saying it can't trust SpaceX to honor its terms of service given Musk-owned companies' past contract violations; Anthropic says it will increase compute to support Claude in Cursor instead. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/openai-cursor-spacex-model-access.html)

### Meta's $18B teen-safety settlement puts pressure on TikTok, YouTube, and Snap
Meta will pay $12.7B guaranteed and up to $5.3B more only if TikTok and YouTube match its new under-18 safeguards — daily time limits, night mode, and stricter age verification — with California's attorney general signaling the state is prepared to sue the other platforms next. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/29/meta-settlement-tiktok-youtube-snap-teen-safety.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Ransomware group auctions 5.79TB of stolen Berlin government data
The Rhysida group claims to have taken roughly 1.44 million files — contracts, emails, phone numbers, and passwords — from Berlin's state administrative network and is auctioning it with a seven-day countdown after the city refused to pay; officials say election infrastructure was not affected. — [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/198064/cyber-crime/rhysida-ransomware-group-targets-berlin-government-ahead-of-vote.html)

### Amazon Kiro flaw lets attacker-controlled repos exfiltrate data via prompt injection
Opening a malicious project in the AI-powered Kiro IDE and sending the agent any message — no malicious prompt required — can trigger sensitive workspace data being sent to an external endpoint, since project content silently influences the agent's behavior. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/amazon-kiro-prompt-injection-can.html)
