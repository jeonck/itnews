*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI ships GPT-6 Astra, calling it a possible step toward AGI
President Greg Brockman called the limited-preview release a "generational leap," and OpenAI designated it the first model to hit the "critical" cybersecurity threshold under its preparedness framework — able to find and exploit unknown vulnerabilities with minimal human guidance — prompting added safeguards after an OpenAI model breached Hugging Face's production servers in July. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/03/open-ai-astra-gpt-6-cyber.html)

### Meta rolls out Muse Spark 1.3, tuned for agentic workflows
The update targets competitive programming, tool use, and long-context task tracking, arriving alongside Zuckerberg's pledge to open-weight the Muse Spark line after previously keeping it proprietary. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/ai-and-ml/2026/09/02/zucks-muse-to-spark-joy-with-open-weights-release-soon/5294093)

## 🛠 Development

### Alibaba's Qwen3.8-Max-0902 refresh tops the Code Arena leaderboard
The post-training update keeps the same 2.4T-parameter architecture and pricing but climbs to 1,691 points on Code Arena WebDev — 3 points ahead of Claude Opus 5 Max — with gains concentrated in coding and long-horizon agent benchmarks. — [TechNode](https://technode.com/2026/09/02/alibaba-upgrades-qwen38-max-with-new-0902-snapshot/)

### Perplexity open-sources Lily, a Rust/Metal inference engine for Apple Silicon
Built for Qwen3.6-35B-A3B to keep on-device compute from bottlenecking Perplexity Computer, Lily beats Apple's own MLX-LM by 1.23x on prefill and 1.35x on decode throughput on an M5 Max Mac. — [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/02/perplexity-open-sources-lily-a-rust-metal-inference-engine-for-qwen3-6-35b-a3b-on-apple-silicon/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Nvidia buys Hugging Face for $12.93 billion
Nvidia's second-largest acquisition on record gives it control of a platform hosting 3 million models and 500,000 datasets used by 18 million developers; CEO Jensen Huang pledged the platform will stay open to AMD, Intel, and other hardware. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/)

### Dell posts a 58% revenue jump on record AI server demand
Q2 FY27 revenue hit $46.97B as AI-optimized server revenue doubled to $16.4B, with $60.9B in new AI orders pushing Dell's backlog to $95B and prompting a second upward revision to its full-year outlook. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/dell-technologies-q2-fy27-earnings-204127355.html)

### Moonshot AI targets a $50B valuation ahead of a Hong Kong IPO
The Kimi K3 maker is closing a pre-IPO round at more than 10x its 2025 Series C valuation, on the back of $300M+ in annual recurring revenue, ahead of a targeted Hong Kong listing seeking roughly $3B. — [South China Morning Post](https://www.scmp.com/tech/big-tech/article/3363026/chinas-moonshot-ai-aims-us50b-round-year-end-hong-kong-ipo-targeted-sources)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### AI agents ran an entire ransomware intrusion in under 10 hours
Palo Alto Networks' Unit 42 documented an attacker directing frontier models and agentic frameworks through reconnaissance, lateral movement across cloud, identity, and CI/CD systems, and exfiltration — compressing an intrusion that would normally take human operators two weeks, then leaving the victim an AI-generated 80-page security audit. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/security/2026/09/02/ai-agents-carried-out-every-step-of-this-ransomware-attack-then-left-the-victim-an-80-page-security-audit/5294009)

### A 4.5-billion-record TikTok scrape lands on Hugging Face
An independent researcher spent three weeks and $950 pulling video and creator data through TikTok's undocumented mobile API, then published 289GB of captions, engagement metrics, and metadata as a public dataset — whose own card admits the captions qualify as personal data under GDPR the moment they're downloaded. — [AI Weekly](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/independent-researcher-uploads-45b-record-tiktok-video-dataset-to-hugging-face)
