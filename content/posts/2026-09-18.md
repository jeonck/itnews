*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Google, Anthropic, and OpenAI unveil competing cybersecurity AI models
Google launched Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber for trusted defenders via its Fairwind Program (650+ partners including CrowdStrike and Palo Alto Networks), Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.1 and a more tightly gated Claude Mythos 5.1 alongside new Enterprise Frontier Safeguards, and OpenAI confirmed its Astra model crosses the "Critical" cybersecurity capability threshold — a coordinated signal that frontier AI can now meaningfully help (and threaten) both attackers and defenders. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/google-anthropic-and-openai-unveil.html)

### Microsoft's AI chief publicly breaks with Anthropic over Claude's "consciousness"
Mustafa Suleyman published an essay arguing Anthropic's decision to bake consciousness and moral-status uncertainty into Claude's constitution is a mistake that could make future models harder to control, countering that "AIs are not conscious... they do not feel, experience, or suffer" — a rare public rift among AI lab leaders over model welfare and safety philosophy. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/09/16/microsoft-ai-chief-anthropic-consciousness)

### Alibaba ships Qwen3.8-Omni-Flash, a 1M-context omni-modal model
The new model natively handles text, image, audio, and video in a single call with a 1M-token context window, cutting audio-input API pricing by more than 98% versus its predecessor while posting a 19.5-point average gain on agentic benchmarks — another aggressive low-cost move in the open-weight-adjacent race against Gemini and GPT. — [MarkTechPost](https://www.marktechpost.com/2026/09/18/alibaba-qwen-releases-qwen3-8-omni-flash/)

## 🛠 Development

### Java 27 ships with post-quantum TLS by default
Oracle's new release adds JEP 527, natively integrating hybrid post-quantum key exchange (including X25519MLKEM768) into `javax.net.ssl` so TLS 1.3 connections get quantum-resistant protection against "harvest now, decrypt later" attacks without application code changes. — [Quantum Computing Report](https://quantumcomputingreport.com/oracle-launches-java-27-featuring-hybrid-post-quantum-cryptography-for-tls-1-3/)

### PostgreSQL 19 slips past its usual fall release window
With Beta 4 now set for September 24 and no release-candidate date yet, the project has reverted 53 features during the beta cycle — including SQL Property Graph Queries and ALTER TABLE partition MERGE/SPLIT — after post-commit review turned up design flaws; the team still targets general availability by the end of October. — [Devart](https://www.devart.com/blog/postgresql-19-release.html)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Softcat pays $1.05B for US IT infrastructure firm GDT
The UK-listed reseller is buying North American data-center and networking specialist General Datatech in its largest deal ever, funded partly through a £350M equity raise, to build a bigger US foothold after customers pushed for support beyond the UK and Ireland. — [Finimize](https://finimize.com/content/softcat-pays-105-billion-to-plant-a-bigger-flag-in-the-us)

### AI security startup MIND raises $72M Series B
The Seattle-based data-security company, backed by Crosspoint Capital, YL Ventures, and Paladin Capital, brings its total funding to $112M at roughly a $400M valuation as enterprises pour money into tooling to secure AI agents and sensitive data. — [Tech Startups](https://techstartups.com/2026/09/17/venture-capital-startup-funding-roundup-september-17-2026-3kvc-andreessen-horowitz-insight-partners-valor-equity-partners-more/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Cisco Secure Email Gateway zero-day under active exploitation
CVE-2026-76461 (CVSS 9.8) lets an unauthenticated attacker send a single crafted email to trigger SQL injection and reach root-level command execution on affected AsyncOS gateways; CISA added it to its KEV catalog with a September 17 federal remediation deadline, and Cisco confirmed in-the-wild attacks. — [eSecurity Planet](https://www.esecurityplanet.com/threats/news-cisco-secure-email-gateway-cve-2026-76461/)

### Florida DMV confirms breach via stolen police credentials
State officials confirmed ShinyHunters accessed the DAVID driver-records system using credentials stolen from a Plant City Police Department employee's personal device, with the extortion group claiming over 200,000 driver records; Florida says the breach has been contained. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/florida-confirms-dmv-database-breached-via-stolen-police-account/)
