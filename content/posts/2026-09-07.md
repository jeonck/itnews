*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Claude produces the first fully computer-verified proof of Fermat's Last Theorem
Working largely autonomously over 11 days with an internal research model, Claude wrote 13 million lines of Lean code and proved 29,500 intermediate theorems to formalize Andrew Wiles' proof — a task mathematicians had expected to take several years by hand. — [Anthropic](https://www.anthropic.com/research/formalizing-fermats-last-theorem)

### Sam Altman apologizes for "messy" GPT-6 Astra rollout
OpenAI's launch gave enterprise customers on its Daybreak platform first access while Plus and Pro subscribers — who normally get new models on day one — were left waiting, prompting Altman to promise a broader rollout "in the near future" and grant paying users a bonus usage reset for each day of lost access. — [Unite.AI](https://www.unite.ai/sam-altman-apologizes-as-gpt-6-astra-staged-launch-denies-paid-access/)

## 🛠 Development

### Broadcom launches TrueSource for open-source supply-chain security
The new portfolio adds human-reviewed, verified build artifacts and hardened container images for Java, Python, and Node.js — alongside Spring Enterprise support and data-service coverage for PostgreSQL, RabbitMQ, MySQL, and Valkey — betting that manually vetted patches beat automated scanning alone for supply-chain trust. — [SD Times](https://sdtimes.com/open-source/broadcom-introduces-truesource-for-open-source-software-security/)

### pnpm 12 ships a stable Rust rewrite, cutting install times up to 90%
The JavaScript package manager's core is now roughly two-thirds Rust, eliminating overhead from routing filesystem operations through Node.js; the project's own benchmarks show a sample uncached install dropping from 8.22 seconds to 5.19 seconds versus pnpm 11, while preserving existing workflows and lockfiles. — [The Register](https://www.theregister.com/devops/2026/09/04/javascript-installer-pnpm-recast-in-rust-because-ecmascript-cant-keep-up/5294394)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Nvidia confirms $12.93 billion acquisition of Hugging Face
The deal — Nvidia's second-largest ever, after the $20 billion Groq asset purchase — hands it control of the platform that 18 million developers use to share AI models, datasets, and applications; Hugging Face had turned down a smaller Nvidia investment last year to stay independent. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/03/nvidia-confirms-it-will-buy-hugging-face-for-12-9-billion/)

### Anthropic delays IPO marketing to mid-October, still eyeing a $2 trillion valuation
Reuters reports the prospectus won't go public until late September, with roadshows pushed to mid-October and the listing targeted to close before the US midterm elections; the company is separately finalizing a credit facility of up to $15 billion. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/05/anthropic-ipo-launch-shifts-toward-mid-october-reuters.html)

### Gimlet Labs raises $300 million at a $3 billion valuation
The Series B, led by Andreessen Horowitz just six months after an $80 million raise, funds a "multi-silicon" inference cloud that splits AI workloads across GPUs and specialized chips from Nvidia, AMD, Intel, Arm, Cerebras, and d-Matrix to cut latency and cost. — [GlobeNewswire](https://www.globenewswire.com/news-release/2026/09/04/3356707/0/en/now-valued-at-3-billion-gimlet-labs-raises-300-million-in-series-b-led-by-andreessen-horowitz-for-industry-s-first-multi-silicon-inference-cloud-for-agentic-ai.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Unpatched "StyleSmuggler" zero-day is actively backdooring Magento and Adobe Commerce stores
Disclosed by security firm Sansec, the unauthenticated RCE flaw affects all current versions — including fully patched 2.4.9 installs — by abusing Magento's own template rendering and email systems; Adobe has not yet assigned a CVE or shipped a fix, and the interim advice is to disable GraphQL. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/unpatched-magento-and-adobe-commerce.html)

### Nearly 22,000 Microsoft Exchange servers exposed to mailbox-hijack bug
CVE-2026-62911, an authentication-bypass flaw reported by DEVCORE's Orange Tsai, lets a low-privileged attacker take over every mailbox on an Exchange Server 2016, 2019, or Subscription Edition box; exploit code is already public, and Germany's BSI says roughly 85% of the country's on-premises servers remain unpatched. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/nearly-22-000-microsoft-exchange-servers-vulnerable-to-hijack-attacks/)
