*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Alibaba launches Wan3.0, a 30-second AI video model
Released a day after Alibaba's $10.2 billion Hong Kong share placement, Wan3.0 doubles single-pass clip length to 30 seconds and now generates video from text, images, audio, and documents like PDFs and slide decks. — [Dataconomy](https://dataconomy.com/2026/08/24/alibaba-launches-wan30-a-30-second-ai-video-generation-model/)

### Claude suffers a multi-hour global outage
Anthropic saw elevated errors across claude.ai, the API, Claude Code, and Claude Cowork starting around 05:06 UTC on August 24, hitting Opus 5, Fable 5, and Opus 4.8 with widespread "529 Overloaded" errors before stabilizing roughly three hours later — the latest in a string of disruptions this month. — [Android Authority](https://www.androidauthority.com/claude-outage-august-24-3702012/)

## 🛠 Development

### Bun 1.4 ships its full Rust rewrite
Bun's runtime moved from Zig to Rust — roughly 535,000 lines ported — cutting idle CPU usage 5x, memory use up to 35%, and startup time 50% on Linux, while adding built-in browser automation, Bun.Image, and JSON5/JSONL support. — [Bun Blog](https://bun.com/blog/bun-v1.4)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Hugging Face explores a $13B sale
The open-source AI hub has reportedly hired a bank to gauge buyer interest, which would nearly triple its $4.5 billion 2023 valuation; no deal or buyer has been confirmed. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/08/24/hugging-face-reportedly-in-talks-to-be-acquired-for-13b/)

### Nvidia weighs a multibillion-dollar Perplexity investment
Talks reportedly center on a new round that would value Perplexity above $30 billion, extending Nvidia's pattern of backing both open-weight model builders and the consumer apps that run on top of them. — [Yahoo Finance](https://finance.yahoo.com/technology/ai/articles/nvidia-reportedly-weighs-perplexity-investment-111111172.html)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### GitLab patches critical unauthenticated GraphQL flaw
CVE-2026-19478 (CVSS 9.4) let unauthenticated attackers modify or delete public projects and user data via a GraphQL directive; watchTowr reported in-the-wild exploitation attempts within minutes of disclosure, and self-managed instances need immediate patching to 19.2.4 or later. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/critical-gitlab-graphql-flaw-could-let.html)

### Sakura Internet breach may affect 1.36 million accounts
The Japanese cloud and hosting provider found that a separate sales-management system, beyond the rental-server intrusion it first disclosed, may have exposed names, emails, contract details, and billing information for up to 1.36 million customer accounts. — [BleepingComputer](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sakura-internet-hack-exposes-data-of-up-to-136-million-accounts/)
