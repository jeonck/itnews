*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### OpenAI safety lead resigns, calls the company's culture "broken"
David Robinson, who spent three and a half years leading the writing of safety reports for OpenAI's model launches, resigned and published an essay arguing the company's launch pace leaves too little room for careful safety work — the fourth safety staffer to leave OpenAI in about a week. — [Yahoo News](https://www.yahoo.com/news/science/articles/openai-safety-employee-resigns-claiming-163001344.html)

### Meta's Muse Spark helps mathematicians crack six open research problems
Working with mathematicians over several months using nothing but the standard Meta.ai chat interface in Thinking Mode, Muse Spark contributed to six papers spanning probability, differential equations, and group theory, five of which answered previously open questions in their fields. — [RuntimeWire](https://runtimewire.com/article/meta-muse-spark-six-mathematics-research-papers)

## 🛠 Development

### IBM brings its Bob coding agent to self-hosted and air-gapped environments
IBM Bob's agentic development platform — including its BobShell IDE and parallel tool calling — can now run entirely within a customer's own infrastructure using Nvidia Nemotron or Poolside Laguna models, targeting enterprises whose regulated data can't leave their perimeter. — [IBM Newsroom](https://www.ibm.com/new/announcements/ibm-bob-expands-to-self-hosted-environments-for-sensitive-and-mission-critical-enterprise-software)

### Qodo 3.0 adds governance and quality gates for agentic coding factories
The release connects AI coding agents to an organization's codebase knowledge, architectural standards, and review rules before a pull request even exists, and unifies review and PR triage across GitHub, GitLab, Bitbucket, Azure DevOps, and Gerrit. — [Qodo](https://qodo.ai/blog/introducing-qodo-3-0)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Schneider Electric to acquire PTC for $22.6 billion
The French conglomerate's biggest-ever acquisition buys Boston-based PTC at $205/share, a 42% premium, adding CAD and PLM industrial software to build what CEO Olivier Blum called "the industry's most complete Software & AI powerhouse." — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-10-05/schneider-electric-to-acquire-ptc-for-more-than-20-billion)

### Federal judge rules Flock license-plate searches are "indiscriminate mass surveillance"
An Oklahoma judge suppressed evidence from a drug case after ruling that a deputy's warrantless search of Flock's nationwide camera network violated the Fourth Amendment, intensifying a national fight over the police surveillance technology. — [Washington Post](https://www.washingtonpost.com/technology/2026/10/02/police-search-using-flock-was-form-mass-surveillance-judge-rules/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### GitLab patches critical 9.9-severity AI Gateway RCE flaw
CVE-2026-90970 let an authenticated Duo Agent Platform user craft a malicious prompt template that escaped the sandbox and ran arbitrary commands on self-hosted AI Gateway hosts; GitLab.com and GitLab Dedicated customers were unaffected. — [Security Affairs](https://securityaffairs.com/200283/hacking/cve-2026-90970-critical-gitlab-ai-gateway-flaw-fixed.html)

### Citrix confirms new NetScaler zero-day exploited days after its last patch
CVE-2026-88779, an 8.7-severity memory overflow in NetScaler ADC/Gateway SAML configurations, is being used to knock SAML deployments offline; CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog with a October 7 federal remediation deadline. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/10/new-netscaler-zero-day-exploited-in.html)

### CISA flags actively exploited FortiMail zero-day
CVE-2026-104286, a 9.8-severity path-traversal bug in Fortinet's email security gateway, lets unauthenticated attackers write arbitrary files to the underlying system; federal agencies had until October 4 to patch. — [CISA](https://www.cisa.gov/news-events/alerts/2026/10/01/cisa-adds-one-known-exploited-vulnerability-catalog)

### Google freezes its open-source bug bounty program over a flood of AI-generated reports
Google paused its OSS VRP — which covers projects like Go, Angular, and Protocol Buffers — after a "significant rise" in automated, mostly invalid AI-generated vulnerability submissions overwhelmed reviewers; it plans an update in early 2027. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/10/04/google-froze-its-open-source-bug-bounty-program-due-to-a-significant-rise-in-ai-submissions/)
