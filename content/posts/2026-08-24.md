*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Anthropic's IPO filing will flag AI backlash as a risk factor
Sources tell CNBC that Anthropic's upcoming prospectus — for an offering expected to value the company near $1 trillion — will list negative public sentiment toward AI and data centers as a risk, a rare admission as it heads toward a Nasdaq listing amid growing public opposition to AI infrastructure buildout. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/08/21/-anthropic-ipo-filing-will-show-ai-backlash-as-risk-sources-say.html)

### Meta pulls another OpenAI researcher into Superintelligence Labs
Luke Metz, who had returned to OpenAI from Mira Murati's Thinking Machines Lab earlier this year, is now joining Meta's Superintelligence Labs to report to Alexandr Wang — the latest move in an escalating talent war among frontier AI labs. — [Axios](https://www.axios.com/2026/08/24/meta-hires-openai-luke-metz)

## 🛠 Development

### Rust 1.98.0 ships algebraic float methods and faster integer formatting
The release stabilizes "algebraic" floating-point operations that let the compiler apply real-number optimizations, a new `format_into` method for buffered integer formatting competitive with the `itoa` crate, and stronger guarantees around `ManuallyDrop`. — [Rust Blog](https://blog.rust-lang.org/2026/08/20/Rust-1.98.0/)

### AWS expands Bedrock AgentCore's web search tool to Europe and Asia Pacific
Web Search on Amazon Bedrock AgentCore now supports domain and published-date filtering and is rolling out to the Ireland and Tokyo regions, giving agent builders outside the US managed, cited web grounding without data leaving their AWS environment. — [AWS](https://aws.amazon.com/about-aws/whats-new/2026/08/web-search-amazon-bedrock/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### SoftBank plans a record $6.3B retail bond sale to fund its OpenAI bet
The seven-year yen bond offering, the largest ever by a Japanese issuer, adds to SoftBank's more than $60 billion in OpenAI commitments — and analysts say the company still faces a shortfall exceeding $20 billion even after the raise. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-24/softbank-plans-record-1-trillion-retail-bond-offering-in-japan)

### Nvidia warns its biggest customers of 15%+ price hikes on AI servers
Soaring memory prices — server DRAM has roughly doubled since Q1 2026 and now accounts for a quarter of high-end rack costs — are pushing Nvidia to raise prices on systems built around its Vera Rubin and Grace Blackwell chips, with increases taking effect on shipments starting early next year. — [Bloomberg](https://www.bloomberg.com/news/articles/2026-08-22/nvidia-customers-notified-about-ai-related-price-hikes-above-15)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Microsoft patches a maximum-severity Entra ID flaw
CVE-2026-69836 (CVSS 10.0) is a deserialization bug that could let an unauthenticated attacker execute code against Microsoft's cloud identity service with no user interaction; Microsoft has already deployed the server-side fix, though it walked back an initial claim that the flaw was exploited in the wild. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/microsoft-entra-id-flaw-cvss-100.html)

### Zimbra SNMP flaw under active exploitation
Attackers are exploiting CVE-2026-73570, an unauthenticated command-injection bug in Zimbra Collaboration's SNMP notification handling, to run arbitrary OS commands on unpatched mail servers; a fix has shipped since July, but CERT Polska says exploitation attempts are ongoing against organizations that haven't updated. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/08/attackers-exploit-zimbra-snmp-flaw-for.html)
