*Everything that mattered in tech over the last day or so, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### Thinking Machines Lab ships Inkling 2 Small, plans open weights next week
The Mira Murati-led startup's second-generation model is a 594B-parameter mixture-of-experts (17B active) with image input and variable effort, scoring 31 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index. It's live now on Tinker with closed weights; full weights are due under Apache 2.0 next week pending safety red-teaming. — [Source](https://thinkingmachines.ai/news/inkling-2-small/)

### Ai2's StateBench shows frontier models collapsing on long-document state tracking
The new open benchmark grades whether models can track changing variables across long documents at four difficulty levels; Ai2 found frontier models' accuracy fell to 13% or lower on the hardest tier, while small models trained from scratch on the task generalized far better. — [Source](https://allenai.org/blog/statebench)

## 🛠 Development

### GitHub makes "@GitHub" in Slack and Teams generally available
The integration, previously in preview, lets teams query and act on issues, PRs, and repo activity from chat without switching context. It shipped alongside a new pull request overview page, now in public preview. — [Source](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-06-github-copilot-in-slack-and-teams-is-generally-available)

### GitHub expands secret scanning detectors, adds SpaceX as a partner
The update broadens the set of detectable credential patterns and adds SpaceX to GitHub's partner program for validating leaked secrets in near real time, part of a steady cadence of security-focused changelog entries this week. — [Source](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-11-05-secret-scanning-upgrades-detectors-adds-spacex-as-partner)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Google commits $17B to its first Wyoming AI data center
The Laramie County project pairs the data center with a 702MW natural-gas fuel-cell plant using carbon capture, supplied by Bloom Energy and Tallgrass Energy. Google says the site will be air-cooled to limit strain on local water supplies, after a local poll found only 30% support for more AI data centers in the state. — [Source](https://blog.google/innovation-and-ai/infrastructure-and-cloud/global-network/wyoming-data-center/)

### Thinking Machines Lab reportedly closing $5B round at $40B valuation
The round, led by Accel with Nvidia said to be contributing roughly $2.5B, could close within days according to anonymously sourced reporting; it follows an earlier attempt at a $50–60B valuation that collapsed, after which three co-founders returned to OpenAI. — [Source](https://the-decoder.com/thinking-machines-lab-reportedly-seeks-up-to-5-billion-dollars-in-new-funding/)

### AI inference startup Fal doubles valuation to $16B in $1.3B round
Bessemer led the Series F, with Nvidia, T. Rowe Price, and others participating; the valuation is double what it was in April, as Fal says demand for GPU inference capacity continues to outrun supply. — [Source](https://thenextweb.com/news/fal-1-3b-bessemer-16b-valuation-ai-inference)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Cloud breach at Iris Ohyama's online store exposes up to 7.7 million customers
Iris Plaza, the e-commerce arm of Japanese appliance maker Iris Ohyama, found suspicious files in an external cloud service on October 28 and shut down its store; exposed data may include passwords and birthdates for roughly 5.8 million members and contact details for 1.9 million nonmembers. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/iris-ohyama-data-cloud-breach-exposes-info-of-77-million-customers/)

### SonicWall patches actively exploited SSL-VPN MFA bypass
CVE-2026-19868 lets an attacker with valid credentials for an alternate login method bypass SAML-enforced MFA on SonicOS firewalls; CISA added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog and gave federal agencies until November 20 to patch. No workaround exists — only the firmware update fixes it. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/sonicwall-warns-of-new-ssl-vpn-mfa-bypass-flaw-exploited-in-attacks/)

### "*sshole" flaw lets attackers bypass OpenSSH password auth on musl-based Linux
CVE-2026-123158, present since 2016, affects OpenSSH on Alpine Linux, OpenWrt, and other musl-based systems without PAM when password authentication is enabled; an overly long password can trigger the bypass. Fixed in OpenSSH 10.6p2 — admins who can't upgrade immediately should disable password authentication. — [Source](https://www.bleepingcomputer.com/news/security/openssh-flaw-lets-attackers-log-into-password-disabled-linux-accounts/)
