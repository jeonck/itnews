*Everything that mattered in tech over the last 24 hours, in about two minutes.*

## 🤖 AI / ML

### ChatGPT co-creator launches Jev, a new kind of "judgment" model
TypeSafe AI — founded by former OpenAI researcher and RLHF co-inventor Diogo Almeida — came out of stealth with Jev, a "System One" model trained via Reinforcement Learning for Calibrated Decisions to output fast, calibrated judgments and probabilities inside other software instead of generating text; the startup also disclosed $40M in seed funding. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/18/a-new-kind-of-ai-model-from-a-chatgpt-inventor-is-thrilling-developers/)

### Google DeepMind launches an institute to debate AGI's societal impact
The new DeepMind Institute, directed by Demis Hassabis, Shane Legg, and James Manyika, will publish outside researchers' essays on AGI preparedness — including warning-sign detection in AI reasoning and workforce disruption — as Google seeks a platform for views that diverge from official company policy. — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/09/17/google-deepmind-launches-institute-to-widen-the-agi-debate/)

## 🛠 Development

### PyTorch 2.14 ships NVGEMM backend and native Apple Silicon linear algebra
The release adds an autotuning GPU matmul backend with low-precision NVFP4 support on Blackwell, a redesigned distributed backend (nccl2) with fault tolerance for recovering training jobs without full restarts, native Metal-tuned linear algebra on Apple Silicon, and eager-mode support for Python 3.15. — [PyTorch](https://pytorch.org/blog/pytorch-2-14-release-blog/)

### GitHub Copilot lets developers tune auto model selection for cost vs. quality
Copilot's "auto" mode now offers three configurable tiers — efficiency, balance, and intelligence — that change how aggressively it trades off cost, latency, and quality per prompt, rolling out across VS Code, the Copilot CLI, and the Copilot app. — [GitHub Changelog](https://github.blog/changelog/2026-09-14-configure-cost-and-quality-in-copilot-auto-model-selection/)

## 🏢 Big Tech & Startups

### Salesforce targets $63B+ revenue by fiscal 2030, doubles down on AI at Dreamforce
At its Dreamforce conference, Salesforce projected more than $63 billion in revenue by fiscal 2030 and pushed "Claudeforce" — letting salespeople query Salesforce data from inside Anthropic's Claude — as CEO Marc Benioff works to convince investors that AI is accelerating, not cannibalizing, its SaaS business. — [CNBC](https://www.cnbc.com/2026/09/16/salesforce-issues-revenue-target-of-63-billion-for-fiscal-2030.html)

### Temporal raises $550M Series E at a $12.55B valuation
Lightspeed led the round, with Wellington, Goldman Sachs Alternatives, and Tiger Global co-leading, as demand grows for Temporal's "durable execution" infrastructure that keeps long-running agentic AI workflows resilient to failures; the company's annualized revenue run rate has topped $250M, up more than 200% year over year. — [GeekWire](https://www.geekwire.com/2026/temporal-raises-550m-hits-12-55b-valuation-as-agentic-ai-wave-fuels-massive-growth/)

## 🔐 Security & Cloud

### Cisco patches maximum-severity ISE zero-day under active exploitation
CVE-2026-76460 (CVSS 10.0) is an unauthenticated API authentication bypass in Cisco Identity Services Engine and ISE-PIC that lets attackers gain root-level control of the appliance; CISA confirmed in-the-wild exploitation and added it to its Known Exploited Vulnerabilities catalog, and Cisco has shipped emergency patches. — [SecurityWeek](https://www.securityweek.com/active-exploitation-triggers-emergency-patch-for-cisco-ise-zero-day/)

### Critical Check Point flaw allows unauthenticated root code execution
CVE-2026-91843 (CVSS 9.8) is a pre-authentication stack overflow in Check Point Security Management and Log Server triggered by an overly long username at login, letting an attacker run code as root with a single network request; Check Point has shipped a LivePatch fix and says it has no evidence of exploitation yet. — [The Hacker News](https://thehackernews.com/2026/09/critical-check-point-management-server.html)
